Build a move-abroad plan in the right order

A move becomes easier to evaluate when immigration, money and practical logistics are treated as separate workstreams. Start with the legal residence route, then estimate the cash required before departure, arrange health or travel cover where appropriate, plan banking and transfers, and only then commit to housing or transport that cannot be refunded.

CountryPilot planning sequence: eligibility → documents → total first-year budget → insurance/healthcare → banking and money transfer → housing → departure checklist.

Use numbers before choosing a destination

Compare destinations using the same assumptions: visa/application costs, deposits, first-month housing, flights, insurance, tuition where relevant, transport and an emergency reserve. A destination with lower rent can still require more cash up front if deposits, insurance or immigration fees are higher.

Do not make irreversible commitments too early

  • Verify the immigration route on the destination government's official site.
  • Do not assume a job offer automatically provides work authorization.
  • Check refund terms before paying tuition, deposits or relocation services.
  • Verify landlords, recruiters and advisers independently.
  • Keep a cash buffer for delays, temporary accommodation and exchange-rate changes.
