Free job-offer safety tool

Visa Sponsorship Job Verification Checklist

Run a structured check before trusting a sponsorship job offer, paying a recruiter, sending sensitive documents or spending money on relocation.

Important: This tool cannot prove that an offer is genuine and is not legal or immigration advice. It is designed to help you identify checks that still need to be completed. Always use the current official immigration authority before acting.

How to verify a sponsorship job offer

Verification is stronger when you separate four questions: does the employer really exist, is the job genuine, is there a lawful immigration route for the role and candidate, and is the person contacting you actually authorised by the employer? A positive answer to one question does not prove the others.

EvidenceUseful forNot enough by itself
Employer website and business identityConfirming the organisation existsProving the recruiter or vacancy is genuine
Official sponsor register where availableConfirming sponsor permission for a routeProving every advertised job by that name is real
Written job offerChecking duties, pay, location and partiesGuaranteeing a visa outcome
Official immigration guidanceChecking the legal route and current requirementsReplacing individual legal advice in a complex case

United States sponsorship check: employer history is not a guarantee

For U.S. opportunities, verify the vacancy on the employer’s own careers site and identify the exact immigration category the employer expects to use. Past H-1B or other petition activity can help with research, but it does not prove that a current role will be sponsored. Use the US Visa Sponsorship Jobs Guide alongside USCIS and Department of State sources.

Country-specific starting points

United States: start with USCIS work-authorisation guidance and the U.S. Department of State employment-visa overview. United Kingdom: check the Home Office sponsor register. Canada: confirm the permit type through IRCC work-permit guidance. Australia: check the Home Affairs sponsored-worker visa options.

Related CountryPilot guides: US sponsorship jobs, UK Skilled Worker visa, Canada work permits, and Australia IT sponsorship jobs.

Methodology & citation

The checklist separates employer identity, vacancy verification, immigration-route fit, written offer terms and common scam signals. Completing every item reduces uncertainty but does not prove that an offer is genuine or that a visa will be approved.
