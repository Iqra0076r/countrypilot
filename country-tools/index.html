Why CountryPilot builds free planning tools

International moves and applications often involve several costs and document decisions at the same time. A visa applicant may be checking financial evidence while also estimating deposits, insurance and transfer fees. A student may need to compare tuition and living costs before deciding whether a destination is affordable. CountryPilot's tools are designed to make those planning steps clearer without asking you to upload passports, bank statements or other sensitive documents.

Use your own numbers, then verify official rules

The calculators intentionally use figures you enter rather than pretending that one global average fits everyone. Visa fees, proof-of-funds thresholds, tuition, insurance requirements, exchange rates and housing costs can change. Treat the result as a planning estimate and confirm any legal, immigration or financial requirement with the relevant official authority or regulated provider.

Available planning tools

The tool library now covers UK sponsor-licence checks, sponsorship-job verification, visa document preparation, proof-of-funds planning, relocation budgets, study-abroad budgets, international money-transfer costs, student-insurance comparisons and student-loan repayment scenarios. Each tool links back into CountryPilot's detailed country and topic guides so you can continue researching the underlying decision.

For destination-specific information, continue to the country directory, visa and immigration guides, student insurance guides, international money transfer guides or moving-abroad planning hub.
