What proof of funds is trying to show

Proof of funds is usually intended to demonstrate that an applicant can support themselves, and sometimes accompanying family members, without relying on unauthorised work or prohibited public support. The relevant amount and evidence rules depend on the immigration route, destination and family composition.

Use the official threshold for the exact route

A student route, skilled-worker route, visitor application or family application may use different financial rules even within the same country. Some routes specify a main-applicant amount plus an additional figure for each dependant, while others calculate funds by months, tuition, accommodation or location. Always start with the current official source.

Eligible funds may be less than your account balance

Some money may be unavailable because it is committed to tuition, deposits or other costs. Immigration rules can also restrict which account types, owners, investments or recently transferred funds are acceptable. The calculator therefore lets you exclude money you do not want to rely on when testing your position.

A personal buffer is not an official requirement

The optional buffer is simply a planning figure. It can help account for exchange-rate movement, bank charges or day-to-day expenses, but it must not be confused with the legal minimum. If an authority publishes a precise threshold or exchange-rate rule, follow that rule.
