Australia employer-sponsored visas

482 vs 186 Visa: Australia Sponsored Pathway Comparison

Compare the main employer-sponsored skilled routes before reading the full Home Affairs criteria. This tool narrows the pathway to research; it does not assess eligibility.

Open Home Affairs comparison
Important: Visa rules, occupation requirements, income thresholds, skills assessments and exemptions can change. Use this as a route-selection aid only and confirm the current Home Affairs criteria before acting.

482 visa requirements: current planning snapshot

For the Skills in Demand (subclass 482) Core Skills stream, Home Affairs currently states that applicants must be nominated for an eligible occupation, meet the relevant salary rules, have at least one year of relevant work experience, meet English requirements unless exempt, and hold a skills assessment where one is required. For nominations lodged from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, the published Core Skills Income Threshold is AUD 79,423; the annual market salary rate rules also apply.

Home Affairs — Skills in Demand 482 → · Salary requirements →. Reviewed 7 October 2026.

186 visa: permanent employer nomination

The Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) is a permanent employer-nominated pathway. It has different streams and requirements from the temporary 482 route, including skills-assessment requirements for Direct Entry unless an exemption applies. Do not assume that holding a 482 automatically guarantees a 186 outcome.

How to use this comparison

Start with the employer and job: confirm whether the employer is genuinely offering sponsorship, which visa pathway it intends to use, the nominated occupation, work location and salary basis. Then verify the route-specific criteria on Home Affairs.

For job-offer checking, use the Visa Sponsorship Job Verification Checklist. For role-specific guidance, continue to Australia IT Sponsorship Jobs or Australia Finance Sponsorship Jobs.

Source: Australian Department of Home Affairs sponsored skilled-worker visa comparison. CountryPilot is not affiliated with the Australian Government.

Looking for independent or state-nominated skilled migration instead? Subclasses 189, 190 and 491 use a different points-tested process. Try the Australia Skilled Visa Points Calculator; do not confuse those with employer-sponsored subclasses 482, 186 and 494.
