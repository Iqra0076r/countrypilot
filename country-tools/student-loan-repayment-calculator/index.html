Student Loan Repayment Calculator

Planning aid only: this calculator assumes a standard fixed-rate amortising loan. Real student or education loans can have variable rates, grace periods, fees, subsidised interest, income-based repayment, currency risk or different compounding rules. Use the lender's official repayment illustration before borrowing.

How the monthly payment is estimated

The calculator uses a standard amortisation formula for a fixed-rate loan. The payment is designed to reduce both principal and interest over the selected term. If the APR is zero, the principal is simply divided across the repayment months.

Why total interest matters

A longer repayment term can reduce the monthly payment but increase the total interest paid. A shorter term usually raises the monthly payment while reducing total interest. Compare affordability and total cost rather than choosing a term based only on the smallest monthly figure.

Extra payments can change the schedule

The optional extra-payment field shows how adding money to the scheduled payment can accelerate repayment in this simplified model. Before making extra payments on a real loan, check whether the lender applies them directly to principal and whether any restrictions or prepayment charges apply.

International students should also consider currency risk

If your loan is denominated in one currency but your future income will be in another, exchange-rate changes can alter the real burden of repayment. Tuition, insurance, housing deposits and visa costs may also need to be financed separately.
