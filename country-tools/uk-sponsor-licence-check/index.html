Scope: This tool checks sponsor-readiness signals and sends you to the live Home Office register. It does not apply for a sponsor licence, submit a Skilled Worker application, issue a Certificate of Sponsorship, or guarantee that an employer will sponsor you.
Important: CountryPilot does not maintain the official sponsor register and cannot certify that an employer is licensed. The Home Office register can change frequently. Always confirm against the live GOV.UK source before applying, paying anyone, or making relocation commitments.

How a UK sponsor licence check should work

The Home Office register lists organisations licensed to sponsor workers on Worker and Temporary Worker routes. It includes the organisation name, location, sponsorship rating and route. A reliable check matches more than the name alone.

CheckWhat to matchWhy it matters
OrganisationExact or clearly equivalent legal/trading nameSimilar names can belong to different businesses.
LocationTown/city and employer identityHelps distinguish similarly named organisations.
RouteSkilled Worker or other route relevant to the jobA licence for one route does not automatically cover another.
RatingCurrent rating shown in the registerThe live register is the source to rely on.
VacancyReal employer careers page and written job termsA licence does not authenticate every advert or recruiter.

What the sponsor register does not prove

It does not prove that a recruiter contacting you works for the organisation, that the role meets Skilled Worker requirements, that the salary is sufficient, that a Certificate of Sponsorship will be issued, or that a visa will be granted. Those are separate checks.

Continue with the UK Sponsor Verification Guide, the UK Skilled Worker Visa Guide, or the Sponsorship Job Verification Checklist.
