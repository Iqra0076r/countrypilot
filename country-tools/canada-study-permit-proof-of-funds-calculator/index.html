These living-expense amounts do not include tuition or transportation. IRCC requires applicants to show enough resources for tuition, living expenses and transportation, and for programs longer than one year applicants must also explain how the full duration will be financed.

What can support a proof-of-funds file?

IRCC lists examples such as tuition/housing payment evidence, education loans from a bank, scholarships, recent employment evidence, sponsor/support letters, Canadian or foreign bank accounts, GICs, and bank statements. The exact evidence should fit the source of your funds and your circumstances.

For debt planning, continue to Canada Student Loan Eligibility. For broader costs, use the Canada Tuition Financing Guide and Canada Scholarships Guide.

Methodology & citation

The calculator combines the published IRCC living-expense amount for the selected family size with tuition, transportation and any optional buffer entered by the user. It does not assess source-of-funds credibility or Quebec requirements.
