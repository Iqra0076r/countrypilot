    Official Schengen visa fees

    Since 11 June 2024, the European Commission sets the short-stay (Type C) Schengen visa fee at €90 for adults and €45 for children aged six to below twelve; children under six pay nothing. Nationals of countries with an EU visa facilitation agreement pay a reduced €35 (Cabo Verde: €67.50).

A valid application must also include travel insurance with at least €30,000 medical coverage valid across all Schengen states.

    What the fee covers — and what it does not

    The €90 Schengen visa fee covers processing of the application only. It does not cover external visa-centre service charges — VFS Global or TLScontact, typically €15–€35 per applicant depending on the country — nor travel insurance, translations, courier or photo costs. Use the calculator's service-fee line to budget those extras separately.

    Who does not pay the fee

    The Visa Code waives the Schengen visa fee for school pupils, students and accompanying teachers travelling for study, plus researchers, and representatives of non-profit organisations aged 25 or under attending seminars, conferences or sporting and cultural events. Family members of EU/EEA citizens covered by free-movement rules are also exempt.
