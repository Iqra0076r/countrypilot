    Official Schengen visa fees

    Since 11 June 2024, the European Commission sets the short-stay (Type C) Schengen visa fee at €90 for adults and €45 for children aged six to below twelve. Children under six pay nothing. Nationals of countries with an EU visa facilitation agreement pay a reduced €35 (Cabo Verde: €67.50). A valid application must also include travel insurance with at least €30,000 medical coverage valid across all Schengen states.

    What the fee covers — and what it does not

    The visa fee covers processing of the application only. It does not cover external visa centre service charges (for example VFS Global or TLScontact, typically €15–€35 per applicant depending on country), travel insurance, translations, courier or photo costs. The calculator adds a service-fee line so you can budget those separately.

    Who does not pay the fee

    The Visa Code waives the fee for school pupils, students and accompanying teachers travelling for study, researchers, representatives of non-profit organisations aged 25 or under attending seminars, conferences or sporting/cultural events, and family members of EU/EEA citizens covered by free-movement rules.

    How to use the result

    Enter each traveller in the correct age band, choose your nationality's fee class, add any exempt applicants, then add the service fee your visa centre charges and optionally a currency conversion rate. Use the total as the consular-fee portion of your trip budget — but treat the result as an estimate: service fees change, and your consulate's official page is authoritative.
