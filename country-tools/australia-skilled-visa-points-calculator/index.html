Australia PR Points Calculator: 189, 190 & 491

Get a transparent, indicative points total for subclass 189, 190 or 491 before checking official SkillSelect requirements. This is a self-assessment—not a formal skills assessment, migration advice, or an invitation prediction.

Reviewed 7 October 2026: This calculator is an independent planning aid for the 189, 190 and 491 points test. It does not predict invitations or determine visa eligibility. Confirm every claim with the Department of Home Affairs before submitting an Expression of Interest.

Your indicative score

Choose only claims you can support with evidence. Some items have detailed legal conditions that this tool cannot verify. Calculations stay in your browser.

Additional qualifying factors

Do not overclaim points: Employment points for Australian and overseas experience have a combined maximum of 20. Regional study normally depends on meeting the Australian study requirement. Nominated visa points require qualifying nomination/sponsorship and cannot be assumed before that is confirmed. All claims need evidence. Your occupation, English qualifications, skills assessment and nomination rules must be checked separately.

Evidence-readiness checklist

Before relying on an indicative score, make sure every points claim can be supported. This checklist does not add points; it helps you identify where evidence or official verification is still missing.

Completing every item improves preparation only. It does not confirm visa eligibility, an invitation, or a visa grant.

What the score means

The Department of Home Affairs states that subclass 189, 190 and 491 are points-tested visas and that the threshold for an Expression of Interest is 65. Reaching the threshold is not a guarantee of invitation; other visa and occupation requirements apply, and actual invitation scores can be higher.

These are not the same as employer-sponsored subclasses 482, 186 and 494. To compare those different pathways, use the Employer-Sponsored Visa Comparison Tool.

Occupation eligibility is separate from points. Use the Australia Skilled Occupation List guide to verify the current list, ANZSCO code/version and assessing authority for your intended pathway.

Official sources and methodology

Point values use the published Home Affairs 189, 190 and 491 points tables. The tool awards only the chosen values and applies the maximum 20 employment-points cap. It does not validate occupation eligibility, salary, assessments, English test validity, partner evidence, or any other immigration requirement. No data is transmitted or stored.
