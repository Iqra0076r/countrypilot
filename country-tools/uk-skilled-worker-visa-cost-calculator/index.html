UK Skilled Worker Visa Cost Calculator

Estimate application fees, Immigration Health Surcharge and maintenance-fund planning for a Skilled Worker application. The calculator includes the fee change scheduled for 8 October 2026.

Checked 7 October 2026: GOV.UK has published a fee schedule taking effect on 8 October 2026. Choose the schedule that matches the date you expect to submit. Always verify the final fee immediately before payment.

What this calculator includes

It estimates the application fee for a standard Skilled Worker application, the usual Immigration Health Surcharge of £1,035 per person per year where applicable, and maintenance funds you may need to demonstrate. Maintenance funds are not a government fee; they are shown separately because they affect cash planning.

Methodology and exclusions

The estimate multiplies the selected published application-fee band by the number of applicants, then adds the user-entered IHS period where the surcharge applies. Maintenance is displayed separately because it is evidence of funds, not a fee. The estimate excludes optional priority services, translations, English tests, tuberculosis testing, biometrics-related travel, legal advice, document procurement and other personal costs.

2026 application-fee change

The Home Office fee table published for 8 October 2026 increases standard Skilled Worker application fees. For applications outside the UK, the standard fee becomes £819 for up to three years and £1,618 for more than three years. For in-country applications, it becomes £943 and £1,865 respectively. Jobs on the Immigration Salary List use lower fee bands.

Maintenance funds

GOV.UK currently states that a main applicant will usually need £1,270 available unless an exemption applies. Dependants generally require £285 for a partner, £315 for the first child and £200 for each additional child, again subject to the route’s evidence/exemption rules.

How to interpret the estimate

The result separates money that is normally paid as part of the application process from funds that may only need to be available as evidence. Treat those categories differently in your budget. Application fees and the immigration health surcharge are not the same thing as maintenance funds, and neither category should be confused with employer relocation support, legal fees, travel, translations or document costs.

For a household application, calculate the scenario you actually expect to submit rather than multiplying a single-person result without checking each dependant's position. If the route, application location, permission length, Immigration Salary List status or family composition changes, run a fresh calculation.

When to check the official figures again

Re-check GOV.UK immediately before payment and again if the application is delayed across a published fee-change date. Keep a dated screenshot or note of the official fee page used for your planning. This calculator is most useful as a budgeting worksheet: it helps you identify the categories to plan for, while the government payment screen remains the final source for the amount actually due.
