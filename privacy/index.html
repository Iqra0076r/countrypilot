Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CountryPilot may collect, use and disclose information when you visit countrypilot.info, contact us, use site features or view advertising. It also explains the role of cookies and advertising partners such as Google.

Effective: October 5, 2026Contact: hello@countrypilot.info

1. Information you provide directly

If you email CountryPilot or contact us through a linked communication channel, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, page URL and any attachments. Please avoid sending sensitive identity, financial, health or immigration documents unless they are genuinely necessary for the request.

2. Information generated when you use the site

Like most websites, hosting providers, security systems, analytics tools or advertising services may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, requested URL, approximate region, timestamps, cookie or local-storage identifiers and basic interaction information. CountryPilot does not use this technical information to determine your immigration eligibility or make automated decisions about you.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons and similar technologies can be used to keep services functioning, remember choices, measure site performance, prevent abuse and support advertising. Some technologies are set by CountryPilot and others may be set by third-party providers.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

CountryPilot may display advertising supplied through Google AdSense or other advertising partners. When Google advertising is active, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Information about how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services is available at Google's partner-sites information page. Where additional third-party advertising vendors or networks are used, they may also place or read cookies or use other identifiers subject to their own policies and available opt-out mechanisms.

5. Consent for users in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

When advertising requiring consent is enabled for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, CountryPilot intends to use a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework where Google requires it. The consent interface may allow eligible users to consent, decline, or manage advertising and measurement choices. The exact options shown can depend on the advertising configuration and applicable law.

6. Analytics and performance measurement

CountryPilot currently uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and real-user page performance. Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as a privacy-first service that does not collect visitors' personal data. On CountryPilot it is enabled through Cloudflare's automatic setup for the domain rather than through a manually embedded analytics tag in the page source.

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not currently support custom events or UTM-parameter reporting, so it is used for aggregate traffic and performance measurement rather than detailed conversion attribution. CountryPilot may later enable an additional analytics service such as Google Analytics for event and conversion measurement. If a service that uses cookies or advertising identifiers is enabled, its configuration and any required consent controls should be reviewed before activation.

7. How information may be used

  • to operate, secure and troubleshoot the website;
  • to respond to messages, correction requests and privacy enquiries;
  • to improve navigation, content and site performance;
  • to detect spam, abuse, fraud or security incidents;
  • to measure audience and advertising performance where enabled;
  • to comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate rights.

8. Disclosure of information

Information may be processed by service providers that host, secure, measure or monetize the site, subject to their roles and terms. We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process or a valid request from a competent authority, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and rights of CountryPilot, users or others. We do not sell user conversation content or submitted correction messages to advertisers.

9. Data retention

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to correspondence, maintaining security records, resolving disputes or complying with legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by service provider.

10. External links

CountryPilot links to government sites, universities, providers and other third parties. Their privacy practices are controlled by those organizations, not by CountryPilot. Opening an external link may allow the destination site to receive technical information normally sent by a browser.

11. Children's privacy

CountryPilot is a general-audience international information site and is not designed as a service directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children for advertising personalization. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

12. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or withdrawal of consent. You can also control cookies through browser settings and, where available, the site's consent interface. Advertising choices may additionally be managed through provider tools such as Google Ads Settings.

13. Security

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We use reasonable organizational and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the site, while users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive documents through ordinary email.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site, advertising setup, analytics stack or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

15. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@countrypilot.info.

Advertising activation note: CountryPilot should not enable personalized Google advertising for EEA, UK or Swiss traffic until an appropriate Google-certified CMP has been configured. This page provides the disclosure layer; the CMP itself must be activated in the advertising setup.
