CountryPilot editorial desk

Review desk · reviewer of every guide

The CountryPilot editorial desk is the small review desk behind every guide on this site. It fact-checks each guide against official immigration sources before publication, and re-verifies fee figures and eligibility rules on a rolling schedule.

“CountryPilot editorial desk” is an organizational byline: it identifies the publication responsible for reviewing the page. It is not a fictional individual, and it does not claim a professional license or government affiliation. Where specialized legal, medical, tax, or financial advice is needed, readers should use the responsible authority or a properly qualified professional.

What the desk checks before publication

  • Every factual claim is checked against an official source — the destination’s immigration authority, foreign ministry, embassy, or the provider describing its own product.
  • Fee figures, income thresholds, and eligibility rules are verified against the current official page and carry a stated “as of” date.
  • On immigration, safety, health, insurance, banking, and legal topics, wording is kept cautious: general research guidance, never individualized advice.
  • Links are checked to land on the real government or primary source — never a second aggregator standing in for it.

Rolling re-verification

Guides are re-checked on a schedule, and the re-verification updates the “Updated” date on the page plus a dated note in the article’s update history. If a rule changed between checks, the guide is corrected; the correction is recorded in the public corrections log.

Corrections and accountability

If you identify a material error or a more current official source, contact hello@countrypilot.info. Specific corrections that identify the page, the claim, and the supporting evidence are reviewed against the official source. See the Corrections Policy.

How this byline is used

Articles carry “By Nadeem Tanwari” with “Reviewed by CountryPilot editorial desk” on every guide, so it is always clear who wrote the page and who checked it.

Advertising independence

CountryPilot may display advertising, including advertising served by Google. Advertising does not give an advertiser editorial control over our conclusions or source hierarchy. Our advertising-related data disclosures are in the Privacy Policy.
