Contact CountryPilot

You can contact CountryPilot about corrections, source updates, editorial questions, privacy matters or general enquiries. For the fastest routing, include the page URL and a short description of what you want us to review.

Email hello@countrypilot.infoVisit our Facebook page

Corrections and source updates

If you believe a visa rule, scholarship detail, safety statement, banking explanation, work-permit point or other factual statement is inaccurate or outdated, please send the exact URL, the sentence or section concerned, and—where possible—a link to the current official source. We review material corrections in line with our Corrections Policy.

Editorial questions

For questions about how an article was researched, why a source was used, or how CountryPilot handles software-assisted drafting, email us and reference the article. Our general methodology is also explained in the Editorial Policy and Sources Policy.

Privacy requests

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to hello@countrypilot.info. Please do not send passport scans, national identity numbers, bank statements, medical records, visa application files or other sensitive documents unless we specifically request a minimal piece of information needed to address your request. CountryPilot does not need those documents for ordinary editorial correspondence.

Advertising and business enquiries

Advertising or business enquiries may also be sent to the same address. Any commercial relationship must remain separate from editorial conclusions. We do not sell guaranteed visa outcomes, admission decisions, jobs, government appointments or preferential editorial treatment.

Important: CountryPilot is an independent information publication. We cannot issue a visa, intervene in an application, guarantee a scholarship, arrange employment, provide emergency consular assistance or give individualized legal, medical, tax or financial advice.

Response expectations

We prioritize messages that identify a specific factual issue or broken source. We may not be able to answer individual eligibility questions, especially where an authority or regulated professional must assess personal circumstances. If your matter is urgent or concerns a live application, contact the responsible government authority, embassy, institution, provider or qualified adviser directly.
