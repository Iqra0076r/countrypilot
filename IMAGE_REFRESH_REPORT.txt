CountryPilot image refresh report
================================
Date: 2026-10-05

What was changed
----------------
1. Replaced repeated article/category visuals with article-specific generated artwork.
2. Created 1,775 unique SVG cover images under /images/generated/.
3. Updated homepage, country pages, category pages, article pages and related-article cards to use the new visuals.
4. Preserved the CountryPilot brand style while making each guide visually distinct.
5. Kept all existing article URLs and page structure intact.

Result
------
- Category pages no longer reuse the same small set of stock article images again and again.
- Homepage destination cards now use country-specific guide artwork.
- Related article blocks on article pages are visually more varied.

Technical note
--------------
The new images are lightweight SVG assets so they stay sharp, load quickly and can scale cleanly on desktop and mobile.
