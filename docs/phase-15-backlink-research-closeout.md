## Practical execution split

### Direct / resource-style targets
The first 13 domains are the strongest research-to-outreach candidates. They demonstrate pages that actively curate external resources or useful links. Their replication strategy is resource inclusion, expert/resource suggestion, or a narrowly relevant utility.

### Secondary / long-cycle targets
Domains 14–20 are primarily evidence that high-quality immigration, relocation and migration research can earn citations in HR/legal, academic, journal or policy contexts. These are not all appropriate for cold “add my link” outreach. They should inform linkable-asset creation and selective relationship outreach.

## Data-integrity rules
- Referring-domain Authority Score, source Page AS, follow/nofollow, first-seen and last-seen remain N/A where Semrush did not supply them.
- No “no nofollow marker” assumption is converted into “follow verified.”
- A public citation pattern is not automatically called an attainable backlink.
- Government-hosted stakeholder submissions are evidence patterns, not normal outreach targets.
