Nomad Tax

The visa gets you in; the tax code decides what it costs. This lane covers the money side of living across borders: where you become tax resident, how flat-tax regimes for newcomers actually work, and the double-tax basics that keep remote workers from paying twice. It is for nomads and remote employees who have outgrown ‘just don't think about it’ — the point where income crosses borders and the paperwork has to keep up. Everything here starts from official tax-authority material, because this lane has zero tolerance for folklore.
