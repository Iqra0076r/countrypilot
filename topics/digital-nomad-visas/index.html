Digital Nomad Visas

Remote work untied the desk from the country, and consulates have spent the last three years catching up — some with proper nomad visas, others with remote-work clauses bolted onto old permits. This topic tracks the routes that actually work: income thresholds, qualifying health insurance, the tax-residence edges that follow, and the paperwork errors that get applications rejected. It is for remote employees, freelancers, and founders who earn in one country and want to live in another without guessing at the rules. Every guide here is checked against the consulate or immigration authority behind it, not against travel-blogger hearsay.

— Nadeem Tanwari, Founder of CountryPilot
