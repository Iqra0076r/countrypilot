Retirement Visas

Retirement visas ask a simple question — can you support yourself without working here? — and then bury the answer in income floors, health-insurance clauses, and renewal traps. This topic gathers the long-stay routes for retirees and financially independent movers: Europe's non-lucrative permits, income-based residence, and the countries that court pensioners most deliberately. It is for anyone planning a move measured in years rather than trips, where a rejected renewal is the expensive mistake. Coverage in this lane is young — what exists today is verified against official sources, and more guides are being written.

— Nadeem Tanwari, Founder of CountryPilot
