Startup Visas

Founder visas are immigration's awkward middle child: governments want the jobs and the tax base, but few have built a route founders can actually plan around. This topic follows entrepreneur and startup residence routes — what an ‘innovative business’ means in each rulebook, the capital or endorsement each one demands, and where the nomad visa is honestly the better door while your company qualifies. It is for founders and operators moving themselves, not just their code. The lane is early in our library — today's guides are the verified footholds, and more are being written.
