Golden-Visa Alternatives

When Spain switched off its golden visa in April 2025, Europe's residency-by-investment map was redrawn overnight — and a decade of agent marketing went stale in the same week. This topic keeps the new map current: Portugal's fund-only route, Greece's zone-tiered property rules, Hungary's investor permit, and the honest read on which schemes are quietly next on the chopping block. It is for investors weighing capital against residence, and for families whose applications must survive a two-year queue. We verify thresholds and eligibility against the official gazettes and migration agencies, because this is the lane where wrong figures cost real money.

— Nadeem Tanwari, Founder of CountryPilot
