Relocation Calculators

This lane gathers the estimators we run the numbers through ourselves: the full cost of a move, the savings thresholds consulates demand, and what money transfers actually cost once the fees and spreads land. It is for anyone who has read the visa guide and is now staring at a spreadsheet. The calculators are free estimators — always verify the official figure before you act — and the guides below carry the verified 2026 numbers they run on.
