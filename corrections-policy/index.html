Corrections Policy

CountryPilot aims to correct material errors and outdated claims in a clear, practical way. International rules can change quickly, so a correction process matters most where an inaccurate statement could affect an application, payment, trip, safety decision or other significant action.

Last updated: October 5, 2026Report an issue: hello@countrypilot.info

What to report

You can report factual errors, outdated requirements, broken official links, incorrect fees or deadlines, misleading descriptions, attribution problems, significant spelling errors in names, or information that appears to conflict with a current authoritative source.

How to send a correction

Email hello@countrypilot.info and include the CountryPilot page URL, the specific passage at issue, what you believe is wrong, and a current supporting source where available. A direct government, regulator, university, provider or other first-party link is especially helpful.

How we evaluate reports

We consider the authority of the supplied evidence, whether the issue is material, the date and scope of the rule, and whether it applies to all readers or only a particular nationality, route, institution or circumstance. If a claim depends on context, the correction may be a clarification rather than a simple replacement.

How corrections are made

Minor typographical or formatting changes may be made without a correction note. Material changes should update the page and, when useful to readers, explain the corrected point. Updating a page does not mean every external rule on the page was freshly verified on that date.

What we cannot do

We cannot alter a government decision, reverse a visa refusal, guarantee admission, recover money from a third party or provide individualized legal representation. Where the problem concerns another organization, the appropriate remedy may be to contact that authority or provider directly.

Correction contact: hello@countrypilot.info. Please do not send unnecessary passports, bank statements or other sensitive documents.
