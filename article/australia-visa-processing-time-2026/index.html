Australia Visa Processing Time 2026: Home Affairs Medians, Percentiles, and Peak-Season Planning

Australia publishes monthly visa processing medians — and in 2026 visitor visas are decided in under a day while skilled visas take months. Here is what the August 2026 data says by route, and how to plan around it.

Quick Answer

Home Affairs' August 2026 medians: visitor visas (subclasses 600, 601 and 651 combined) under 1 day; Student visas 22 days; skilled (temporary) visas 134 days. These are aggregate medians — individual cases vary with nationality, file completeness and peak periods. The ETA (601) costs AUD $20 via the official app and is usually instant; the eVisitor (651) is free. Lodge Visitor 600 applications 6–8 weeks before travel in busy seasons.

Australia's Department of Home Affairs publishes monthly processing-time headlines — median decision times by visa category — plus an interactive tool with percentile breakdowns per subclass. It is one of the more transparent systems in the world, and the August 2026 data tells a striking story: visitor visas are among the fastest anywhere, while skilled and student routes take weeks to months.

This guide sets out the 2026 medians, what drives the wide spread between the fastest and slowest files, the fully digital application system (there are no visa stickers — the grant lives against your passport number), and how far ahead to lodge each route. Figures are October 2026; Home Affairs refreshes the medians monthly.

The August 2026 medians, explained

Home Affairs publishes a single median per category each month. For August 2026: visitor visas (the 600 Tourist stream, the 601 ETA and the 651 eVisitor combined) were decided in under 1 day at the median — meaning half of all visitor grants were faster than that. Student (500) visas sat at 22 days, and skilled temporary visas at 134 days.

A median is not a promise: the department's interactive tool shows the 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles per subclass, and the 90th percentile is where planning should happen. A '22-day median' student route can still mean two months for a file at the slow end. Use the live percentile tool for your subclass rather than any single published figure.

The fully digital system: what it means for timing

Australia has no visa labels, stamps or stickers. The ETA (601) is granted through the official Australian ETA app, the eVisitor (651) and 600 through ImmiAccount, and the grant is recorded electronically against your passport number — airlines check it at check-in via the same system. VEVO (Visa Entitlement Verification Online) lets you confirm any grant's status and conditions.

Because everything is digital, there is no passport-return wait after the decision — but there is also nowhere to hide a weak file. Upload clear colour scans: poor-quality images are a documented cause of processing delays and re-submission requests. Keep your ImmiAccount credentials — extensions, condition checks and future applications all run through the same login.

What slows an Australia file down

  • Peak-season volume. Student intake periods and the December–January holiday surge push medians up; lodge 600s 6–8 weeks before travel in busy seasons.
  • Genuine Student (GS) assessment. Student files that raise questions about study intent go into deeper assessment — the biggest driver of slow student cases.
  • Health and character checks. Medical examinations and police clearances add their own external timelines on top of Home Affairs processing.
  • Poor document quality. Blurry scans trigger re-submission requests that reset your effective queue position.
  • ETA copycat apps. The 651 is free and the 601 costs AUD $20 — any site or app charging far more for 'ETA processing' is a markup intermediary, not a government channel, and adds no speed.

When to apply: route by route

Visitor 600 / ETA / eVisitor: despite the sub-1-day median, lodge the 600 6–8 weeks before travel in busy seasons — medians describe the past, not your file. ETAs and eVisitors can be done days before travel, but do them the moment flights are considered.

Student 500: lodge as soon as the CoE is issued and funds evidence is settled; the 22-day median means most files clear in under a month, but GS assessment can double it. For February intakes, October–November lodgement is the safe window.

Skilled routes (482 and similar): the 134-day median is the number to plan around — employer nomination, skills assessment and the visa application are sequential steps, and the total pipeline is measured in months, not weeks.

Checking the live numbers

Home Affairs updates the monthly visa-processing-times publication and the interactive percentile tool on its website — check both for your subclass the week you lodge. The tool's 75th/90th percentile figures are the honest planning numbers; the median is the marketing number.

No one can expedite a Home Affairs decision for a fee — anyone offering to is running a scam. The only legitimate accelerant is a complete, high-quality file lodged early.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an Australia visitor visa take in 2026?

The August 2026 median for visitor visas (600, 601 and 651 combined) was under 1 day — but that is a median, not a guarantee. Lodge the subclass 600 6–8 weeks before travel in busy seasons.

How long does an Australian student visa take?

The August 2026 median was 22 days. Files needing deeper Genuine Student assessment take longer — apply as soon as the CoE and funds evidence are ready.

How fast is the Australian ETA?

Usually near-instant via the official Australian ETA app (AUD $20 service charge). The eVisitor (651) is free and similarly fast.

Can anyone speed up my Australian visa?

No. Home Affairs does not offer paid expediting, and no agent can influence the queue. A complete file with clear scans, lodged early, is the only accelerant.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Under a day for visitors, 22 days for students, 134 days for skilled temporary — the August 2026 medians set the expectations, but plan from the 90th percentile, not the median. Lodge early, upload clean scans, and use the official app and ImmiAccount channels only.
