Does the country actually have a digital-nomad route?

Italy has a digital-nomad/remote-worker visa framework; applicants should verify professional, income, insurance and documentation requirements with the competent Italian consulate because implementation details can be consular and case specific. Start with the official immigration portal and identify the legal name of the status. Private sites such as VisaGuide are useful for discovering programmes, but the government page controls whether the route is open and who qualifies.

If there is no dedicated route, do not relabel a tourist visa as a digital-nomad visa. Visitor status may restrict work even when the employer is abroad. compare it with the current Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation / immigration authorities guidance linked below.
