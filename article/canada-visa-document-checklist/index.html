Country-specific decision map

For Canada, first determine whether the issue belongs to temporary residence (study/work/visit) or permanent residence. Express Entry is a management system for specified economic programs, not a visa by itself. Work authorization may be employer-specific or open only where the rules allow it, and provincial programs can add a second layer of criteria. A practical verification worksheet

Build a one-page eligibility sheet before applying: purpose of stay, route name, sponsor/institution if required, status from which you are applying, eligibility tests, evidence, fees, biometrics/identity step, expected decision point, visa conditions and next-stage options. Then label every item as 'officially confirmed', 'still to verify' or 'not applicable'. This prevents a common failure: collecting generic documents without proving the specific legal requirement.
