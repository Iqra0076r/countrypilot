Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

The United States does not have one generic sponsored-work visa. H, L, O and other employment classifications have different petition, qualification and employer requirements. Many employment routes start with a petition filed with USCIS before a visa application abroad, so a job offer alone is not the immigration approval.

Work Permit Guide: where sponsorship is most plausible

For work permit guide roles in United States, sponsorship is most plausible where employers have persistent skill needs, the occupation fits an immigration route and the candidate can satisfy professional or technical requirements. How to verify an employer or sponsor

Use official sponsor/accreditation registers where they exist, the company's own careers site, business registry details and a professional email domain. Compare the legal employer name on the offer with the entity in the sponsor register. A recruitment agency may be legitimate, but it should be clear who the actual employer is. From job offer to lawful work

The employer and applicant may have separate steps. In some systems the employer first obtains accreditation, sponsorship approval, labour-market approval or a job check; only then can the worker apply. In others, the processes are linked. Use https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/employment.html to map the actual sequence.

Do not start work until the status authorises it. Entry as a visitor or student does not automatically permit full-time employment.
