How to use this Maldives visa requirements guide

The Maldives runs one of the world's simplest entry systems — and one absolute rule. The rule: a free 30-day visa on arrival for all nationalities except holders of Israeli passports, banned under the 3rd Amendment to Immigration Act 1/2007 (effective 15 April 2025). The simplicity: no advance eVisa, no fee, no embassy visit — but everyone must file the free IMUGA Traveller Declaration within 96 hours before arrival. Source: Maldives Immigration (immigration.gov.mv). Full framework: our Maldives Visa & Entry Requirements Guide.

Passport validity and document condition

Your passport must be machine-readable and valid for the duration of your stay — checked at the counter. In practice, one month of validity beyond departure avoids friction. Israeli passport holders — including dual nationals travelling on one — are refused entry under the April 2025 amendment.

Identify the correct entry route

Tourists (except Israeli passport holders): free 30-day visa on arrival — no advance application, no fee; extendable to 60–90 days before expiry. Work: no arrival facility — pre-approval through the employer's Xpat Online System before travel. Study: no published student visa stream; institutions sponsor students on tourist or special visas.

Transit and onward travel

Connecting through Velana International Airport (Malé)? The same entry rules apply the moment you clear immigration.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Three checks: a valid machine-readable passport, the IMUGA Traveller Declaration (within 96h before arrival), and funds — USD 100 + USD 50/day, or a confirmed hotel/resort booking (official 2026 guidance). Add a return/onward ticket.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

The Maldives sets no general vaccination requirement for tourists. The IMUGA declaration includes health questions — answer them accurately; it is the health-screening instrument. Recheck Maldives Immigration guidance shortly before travel.

Travel insurance for Maldives tourists

Insurance is not an entry condition — but on scattered atolls, seaplane medical evacuation is expensive and paid upfront by the patient. Buy comprehensive cover — see our Maldives travel insurance & health guide.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

The 30-day visa on arrival is issued at the immigration counter against your passport, IMUGA confirmation and funds evidence. It is free. Extensions to 60–90 days are handled by Maldives Immigration in Malé before day 30 expires. Overstaying is treated seriously.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

The tourist arrival visa never authorizes work — not for a resort, not remotely. Employment runs through the employer's Xpat Online System: quota first, then the work permit, all before travel. No official work-permit fee schedule is published — confirm figures with your employer, never a blog. Study has no dedicated visa stream — get the institution's arrangement in writing.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every traveller needs their own IMUGA declaration — children cannot be included on a parent's submission. Minors without both parents should carry a consent letter and birth certificate. The Israeli-passport ban applies to holders of Israeli passports — travel on the non-Israeli passport.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

Tourist: 1) Passport machine-readable and valid for your stay. 2) Book accommodation — a confirmed reservation satisfies the funds question. 3) Submit the free IMUGA declaration at imuga.immigration.gov.mv within 96h before arrival. 4) Carry USD 100 + USD 50/day and a return/onward ticket. 5) Receive the free 30-day visa at the counter. Work: Xpat quota and permit approval completed by the employer before you travel.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping the IMUGA declaration — mandatory and checked; the 96-hour window is strict.
  • Assuming a student visa exists — it does not as a published category.
  • Quoting work-permit fees from blogs — no official schedule is published; confirm via the employer.
  • Working on the tourist arrival visa — it never authorizes employment.
  • Israeli passport holders attempting entry — refused under the April 2025 amendment.

Getting it right first time

The IMUGA declaration must match the passport exactly — transliteration mismatches cause counter delays. Submit it yourself, and keep the confirmation accessible offline — Velana airport Wi-Fi is not dependable at the counter.

Fees and processing time

The 30-day arrival visa is free (USD 0); the IMUGA declaration is free. Extensions to 60–90 days are processed by Maldives Immigration (fee per current schedule — confirm on immigration.gov.mv). No official work-permit fee schedule or processing standard is published — confirm with the employer.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

The Maldives has no tourist eVisa — the only pre-travel online step is the free IMUGA declaration at imuga.immigration.gov.mv. Any site selling a "Maldives eVisa" or charging for the arrival visa is not the government.

Supporting documents checklist

Tourist entry: machine-readable passport, IMUGA confirmation (within 96h), funds (USD 100 + USD 50/day) or confirmed booking, return/onward ticket. Extension: passport, entry stamp, reason and evidence, current fee. Work: the employer handles Xpat quota and permit.

Interviews and biometrics

The Maldives operates no visa interview system for tourists — the arrival visa is decided at the counter on documents. Biometric capture is not a published pre-travel step.

Families and groups travelling together

Each family member — including infants — needs their own IMUGA declaration; there is no family bundling. Resort bookings typically cover the whole party — keep one shared folder with every traveller's confirmation.

Customs and border formalities

Strict prohibitions: alcohol, pork products and religious materials contrary to Islam are confiscated at the border. Declare medications with prescriptions. Resort islands operate under the same prohibitions.

Return and onward travel proof

A return or onward ticket is part of the standard arrival package — officers check it as proof of a temporary stay. Keep the resort transfer confirmation with your travel documents. Our Maldives travel tools & planning guide has departure checklists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa in advance to visit the Maldives?

No. Tourists receive a free 30-day visa on arrival at the immigration counter — no advance application, no fee, for all nationalities except holders of Israeli passports (banned from entry since 15 April 2025). You must still submit the free IMUGA Traveller Declaration within 96 hours before arrival.

What is the IMUGA Traveller Declaration?

The mandatory, free electronic arrival declaration at imuga.immigration.gov.mv, submitted within 96 hours before arrival. Every traveller — including children — needs their own. It covers immigration and health screening and is checked at the counter.

How much money do I need to show on arrival in the Maldives?

Official Maldives guidance: USD 100 plus USD 50 per day of stay, or a confirmed hotel/resort booking — the booking satisfies the requirement on its own. Carry a return/onward ticket as well.

Can I extend my 30-day Maldives visa?

Yes — extensions to 60–90 days are granted by Maldives Immigration. Apply in Malé before your initial 30 days expire; confirm the current fee on immigration.gov.mv.

Is there a student visa for the Maldives?

No — the Maldives publishes no dedicated student visa category, fee schedule or insurance requirement. Foreign students enter on a tourist visa or an institution/employer-sponsored special visa. Get the exact arrangement from your institution in writing before travelling.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For the Maldives, the system is simple but strict: free 30-day visa on arrival for all nationalities except Israeli passport holders (banned since April 2025), the mandatory free IMUGA declaration within 96 hours before arrival, and USD 100 + USD 50/day or a confirmed booking as funds evidence. There is no tourist eVisa to buy, no student visa stream, and no official work-permit fee schedule — employment runs through the employer's Xpat system and study through institutional sponsorship. Confirm everything on immigration.gov.mv before you fly.
