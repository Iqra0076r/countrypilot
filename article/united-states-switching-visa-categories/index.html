Evidence that decision-makers can follow

Use clear file names, consistent dates and short explanatory notes. Relationship cases should show the legal relationship and, where required, the genuine nature of the relationship. After submission and after approval

Track official messages, biometrics, interview requests and deadlines. Keep copies of everything submitted. Before travel or starting work/study, confirm any post-decision steps such as registration, digital-status activation, residence-card collection or employer notification. re-check U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) and U.S. Department of State before relying on it.

Switching status: three questions

Ask: Is in-country switching legally permitted from the current status? When can work/study under the new route begin? What happens to the old status while the application is pending?
