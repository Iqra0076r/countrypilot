UK Visa Processing Time 2026

How long UK visa decisions take in 2026 — published service standards, how the clock is measured from biometrics, ETA decision times and priority services.

The published service standards for 2026

UK Visas and Immigration publishes service standards for how long decisions should take, and the figures below are the 2026 standards confirmed on gov.uk. For applications made outside the UK, the standard is 3 weeks for the Standard Visitor visa, the Student route and the Skilled Worker route. For applications made inside the UK, the standard is 8 weeks. These are the targets UKVI is measured against — not legal guarantees for any individual case.

How the clock is measured

The published standards are measured from the biometrics appointment, not from the day you submit the online form. The practical sequence runs like this: complete the online application (10-year travel history, parents’ details, itinerary and finances), pay the fee — £135 for a Standard Visitor visa as of 8 April 2026 — then book the visa application centre appointment (TLScontact or VFS Global), where your photograph and fingerprints are captured and your documents are collected or scanned. Only then does the decision clock start. Upload your scans through the self-upload service before the appointment; the document check at the centre is for identity and collection, not for assembling your file.

ETA decision times

The Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) — £20 as of 2026, for visa-exempt nationalities including the United States — is decided on a different clock: usually within minutes, officially within 3 working days. Do not travel until it is approved; carriers check it before boarding. The ETA is valid for multiple entries over 2 years or until passport expiry, with up to 6 months per visit — but it is permission to travel, not a guarantee of entry.

Planning your timeline

The 3-week and 8-week standards are targets, not guarantees — so plan around the longer horizon. The standing guidance: apply 6–8 weeks before travel, especially in busy periods. For a summer family visit, that means starting the online form around early May; for a September course start on a Student visa, work backwards from enrolment and add the 28-day maintenance-funds holding period on top. A history of compliant visits helps nothing here, but a thin file can add time: six months of bank statements or payslips showing genuine, stable funds beats a single large unexplained deposit, which caseworkers read as funds parking and may query.

Priority and super-priority options

UKVI offers priority and super-priority decision services at some centres for an extra fee, but they are not available everywhere — check at booking. They compress the waiting time; they do not change the decision, and no agent or paid service can influence the outcome. If your travel date is immovable and the standard service window is tight, check availability early — popular centres sell priority slots out.

After the decision

Since the BRP phase-out, approved UK visas are issued as eVisas — a digital immigration status linked to your passport. After approval, verify the status is correctly linked to your current passport (especially after a renewal) and learn how to prove it with a share code for carriers and employers before you fly. Visit visas admit you for up to 6 months per visit, but you must not live in the UK through frequent or successive visits — back-to-back stays with brief absences are the classic refusal pattern at the border and in later applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a UK visitor visa take in 2026?

The published service standard is 3 weeks for applications made outside the UK, measured from the biometrics appointment (2026). In practice, apply 6–8 weeks before travel in busy periods — the standard is a target, not a guarantee.

How long do UK visa decisions take inside the UK?

The published standard for applications made inside the UK is 8 weeks (2026). Priority and super-priority services exist at some centres for an extra fee, but they are not available everywhere.

How long does an ETA take?

ETA decisions usually arrive within minutes, officially within 3 working days. Do not travel until approved — carriers check it. The ETA allows multiple entries over 2 years or until passport expiry, with up to 6 months per visit.

Does the decision clock start when I submit the online form?

No. The published service standards are measured from the biometrics appointment at the visa application centre — the day your fingerprints and photograph are captured — not from the online submission.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For UK visa processing times in 2026, plan on 3 weeks from the biometrics appointment for visitor, student and Skilled Worker applications outside the UK — 8 weeks inside — and minutes to 3 working days for an ETA. The standards are targets, not guarantees: apply 6–8 weeks before travel in busy periods, and confirm the current figures on gov.uk’s waiting-times pages before you commit to dates.
