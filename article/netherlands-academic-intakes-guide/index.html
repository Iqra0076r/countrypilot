Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

IND's 2026 study amount for higher professional education (HBO) or university is €1,130.77 per month. A recognised educational institution normally acts as sponsor for the residence-permit process, but the applicant still has to meet IND's conditions and maintain the required study progress/status.

Official page: https://ind.nl/en/required-amounts-income-requirements

Understand the education system before choosing a university

Start with the qualification structure in Netherlands: degree length, academic calendar, credit system, taught/research distinction and whether the programme leads to professional registration. Studielink and recognised/accredited institutions are useful starting points. Rankings can help with broad reputation, but programme accreditation, curriculum, graduate outcomes and location often matter more.

For regulated fields, check the professional body before applying. A university may be recognised while a specific qualification still does not grant the professional outcome the student expects. verify it against Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) before committing money or documents.

Admission requirements and realistic shortlisting

Build a three-tier shortlist: ambitious, realistic and safer options. For each programme, record academic prerequisites, language score, prerequisite courses, portfolio/research requirements, application fee and deadline. Compare the exact programme page, not only the university's general admissions page. the live Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) reference below should be consulted before action.

International applicants should also check whether credential evaluation, certified translations or country-specific documents are required. Give yourself time to obtain these before the deadline. re-check Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) before relying on it.

Intakes and application timeline

Map the intake backwards. A practical sequence is: 10–14 months out, research programmes and funding; 8–12 months out, tests and documents; 6–10 months out, submit university and scholarship applications; after admission, arrange finances, housing and immigration. The exact dates vary by institution. verify it against Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) before committing money or documents.

Do not assume every programme has both autumn and spring entry. Competitive programmes and scholarships may have one annual intake. compare it with the current Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) guidance linked below.

Tuition, living costs and funding

For Netherlands, build a first-year budget before accepting an offer. Include tuition, compulsory fees, visa/residence charges, insurance, housing deposit, furniture or setup costs, transport and emergency funds. Then map confirmed funding sources against that budget.

Scholarships should be verified on the official provider page. If loans are used, model currency movement and repayment rather than looking only at the monthly payment. the live Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) reference below should be consulted before action.

Admission is not immigration permission

After receiving an offer, review student residence permit for higher education requirements on https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/study/student-residence-permit-for-university-or-higher-professional-education. Immigration authorities may ask for financial evidence, health insurance, language evidence or proof that the course and institution are eligible.

Keep the university and visa timelines aligned. A late admission decision can compress the visa window; a visa delay can affect enrolment. Know the institution's latest-arrival and deferral policy. use Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) to confirm the live requirement where it can change.

After admission: the practical handover

Confirm acceptance, deposits, accommodation, visa documents, travel dates, orientation and registration. Keep digital and paper copies of key documents in separate places. compare it with the current Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) guidance linked below.

Before departure, understand work rights, health-care access and any address-registration requirement. These post-arrival rules can be as important as the visa itself. the live Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) reference below should be consulted before action.

Practical action checklist

  • Open the current official Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND) page and confirm the rule still applies on 2026-10-05 or later.
  • Verify programme recognition, admission requirements, refund rules and the latest visa timeline before paying a large deposit.
  • Write down any fee, threshold, deadline or processing estimate with the date it was checked.
  • Keep the official application link separate from private explanatory articles.
  • Save copies of forms, evidence and confirmations before submitting anything.
  • Do not pay an unverified recruiter, adviser, landlord, scholarship agent or lender because of urgency.
