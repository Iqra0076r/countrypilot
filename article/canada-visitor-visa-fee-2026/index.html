Canada Visitor Visa Fee 2026: TRV Cost, Biometrics, and Family Cap

Canada's visitor fee looks simple — until the biometrics line and the family cap enter. Here is the full 2026 cost of the Temporary Resident Visa, per person and per family.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20265 min read

Canada's Temporary Resident Visa — the visitor visa — has one of the clearest fee structures in immigration: a flat per-person fee, a flat biometrics fee, and a cap that limits what a family pays together. This guide states the 2026 figures exactly and explains how the pieces fit.

Quick Answer

The Canadian visitor visa (TRV) costs CAD $100 per person (2026), applied for online through an IRCC account, plus biometrics at CAD $85 per person. Families of 5 or more applying together are capped at CAD $500 total for the visa fees. The TRV allows stays of up to 6 months per entry.

Visa CAD $100/person · Biometrics CAD $85/person · Family cap CAD $500 · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 5 minutes

The per-person cost: $100 + $85

Every visitor-visa applicant pays two lines: the CAD $100 visa fee and the CAD $85 biometrics fee — $185 per person all in, for most solo travellers. Biometrics (fingerprints and photo) are collected at a visa application centre after the online submission; the fee is paid with the application, not at the centre. Both fees are set out in IRCC's published fee list.

The family cap: $500 for 5+

Families applying together get a ceiling: 5 or more family members applying at the same time pay a maximum of CAD $500 in visa fees between them. Note what the cap covers — the visa fees, not biometrics, which remain per person. For a family of five, that is $500 in visa fees plus 5 × $85 in biometrics. The cap only helps when the applications go in together; staggered family applications each pay the per-person rate.

What the fee buys — and what it does not

The fees pay for processing the application: the online assessment, the biometrics collection, and the decision. They do not buy a visa, and they are not refunded on refusal. The TRV, when granted, typically allows stays of up to 6 months per entry — the exact length is set at the port of entry, not by the visa fee. Service standards run 14 days for visitor applications outside Canada, though actuals move: recent visitor actuals were around 27 days (September 2026). Full detail in our Canada processing-time guide.

Paying correctly

Applications go through an IRCC online account, and fees are paid online as part of the submission — IRCC's fee list (ircc.canada.ca) is the authoritative schedule. Pay inside the official flow only; third-party "visa fee" sites add nothing but markup. Keep the receipts: the biometrics instruction letter that follows the submission references the payment.

The fees around the visitor visa

Travellers sometimes price the wrong product. The visitor/TRV fee is CAD $100; the study permit is CAD $150 (with proof of funds at CAD $23,448/year from 1 September 2026); the work permit is CAD $155 per person plus a CAD $100 open-work-permit holder fee where applicable. And visa-exempt nationalities (the UK among them) need only an eTA, not a TRV at all — check which lane you are in before paying.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a Canada visitor visa in 2026?

CAD $100 per person plus CAD $85 biometrics — $185 per solo applicant, with a $500 family cap on visa fees for 5+ applying together.

Is the Canada visa fee refunded if refused?

No. The fees pay for processing, which you received.

How long can I stay on a visitor visa?

Up to 6 months per entry — the officer at the port of entry sets the actual length.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

CAD $100 plus $85 biometrics per person, capped at $500 in visa fees for families of five or more applying together: Canada's visitor fee is arithmetic — just make sure you are in the TRV lane and not the eTA lane before you pay.
