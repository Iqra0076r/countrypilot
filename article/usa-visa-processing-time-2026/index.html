USA Visa Processing Time 2026: How Long Each Step Takes

How long a US visa really takes in 2026 — interview waits by consulate, ESTA timing, administrative processing, premium processing, and the planning timeline that beats the queue.

Visitor visa interview waits by post

US visitor-visa interview waits in 2026 run from about 2 weeks to over 13 months depending on the consulate — check the live State Department tool for your post.

The State Department’s Global Visa Wait Times tool, updated 21 July 2026, shows visitor-visa interview waits ranging from about 2 weeks at low-demand posts to 13+ months at the busiest. The July 2026 figures for major posts: Hyderabad ~10.5 months, New Delhi and Mumbai ~8.5 months, Karachi ~8 months, Islamabad ~7.5 months, and London ~2 months.

These figures move weekly — always check the live tool for your consulate before committing to travel dates. If you are applying from India or Pakistan, the interview wait is the entire timeline: everything else in the process takes days, while the queue takes most of a year. Our United States visa and entry requirements guide carries the full fee and wait picture.

The DS-160 to interview sequence

The B-1/B-2 process: DS-160, $185 fee, interview scheduling, documents, interview, passport return.

Work the steps in order. 1) Complete Form DS-160 online at ceac.state.gov. 2) Pay the $185 MRV fee (as of 2026). 3) Schedule the interview at the embassy or consulate — check the Global Visa Wait Times tool for your post first, and take the earliest workable slot. 4) Gather supporting documents on purpose of trip, ties to home, and funds — only the passport, DS-160 confirmation and photograph are strictly required at the interview, but officers may request the rest. 5) Attend the interview: fingerprints and a digital photograph are captured, and most applicants aged 14–79 attend in person. 6) After approval, check whether your nationality owes a reciprocity issuance fee before arranging passport return.

Prepare documents once and properly with our visa document checklist — a refused or delayed application restarts the clock at the back of the queue.

Administrative processing: the weeks you cannot plan

Post-interview administrative processing can add weeks and cannot be expedited — build slack into every plan.

After the interview, some cases go into administrative processing, which can add further weeks to the timeline — and cannot be expedited. There is no premium lane for this stage and no published schedule; it resolves when it resolves.

Do not book non-refundable travel before the visa is in hand, especially when applying from a high-wait post. A 214(b) refusal or an administrative delay discovered after you have paid for flights is the most expensive timing mistake applicants make.

ESTA timing: 72 hours minimum

VWP travellers must secure ESTA approval at least 72 hours before departure and cannot board without it.

Travellers from the 42 Visa Waiver Program countries apply at esta.cbp.dhs.gov at least 72 hours before departure ($40.27 total as of 2026). Do not board without an approved authorization — carriers check it, and there is no visa fallback at the gate.

Apply as soon as travel is planned rather than at the 72-hour mark: an ESTA that needs review leaves no room for rebooking if you cut it fine.

Premium processing: 15 business days for I-129

I-129 premium processing costs $2,965 for a 15-business-day decision — but it only speeds the USCIS petition stage.

For petition-based work routes, I-129 premium processing costs $2,965 and delivers a decision in 15 business days (from 1 March 2026). Note what it does and does not speed: it accelerates the USCIS petition stage only — it does not shorten consular interview queues or administrative processing.

Admission timing at the border

The CBP officer sets your stay on the I-94 at each entry — commonly up to 6 months for B-1/B-2, 90 days for ESTA.

The visa does not set the length of stay — the CBP officer does, on the electronic I-94: commonly up to 6 months per admission for B-1/B-2 holders, but it can be shorter. Retrieve and check your I-94 at i94.cbp.dhs.gov after every entry; the date there is the legal limit, not the visa foil.

VWP/ESTA admissions are capped at 90 days with no extension and no change of status. Overstaying — even briefly — can void the visa and trigger re-entry bars of 3 or 10 years.

Planning timeline: apply 6–12 months ahead

Start 6–12 months before travel when applying from high-demand posts like India and Pakistan.

For high-demand posts such as those in India and Pakistan, start 6–12 months before your intended travel date. Complete the DS-160 early, pay the $185 fee, and secure the earliest interview slot — then re-check the live wait-times tool, since figures move weekly.

Keep six months’ passport validity beyond your stay (unless your country is exempt, like the UK) so a renewal does not derail the timeline, and confirm your proof of funds with our proof of funds calculator before the interview. Students should start even earlier: the United States student visa guide covers the SEVIS and I-20 steps that run before the consular clock starts.

Tracking your case

Track your case on the CEAC status check after the interview, and keep your I-94 record after every trip.

After the interview, track the case on the CEAC status check. Keep the I-94 record after every trip — a clean history of entries and timely departures strengthens future applications and keeps future timelines short.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a US visitor visa take in 2026?

The interview wait is the timeline: the State Department’s Global Visa Wait Times tool (updated 21 July 2026) shows waits from about 2 weeks to over 13 months by post — for example Hyderabad ~10.5 months, Islamabad ~7.5, London ~2. Administrative processing after the interview can add further weeks and cannot be expedited.

How far in advance should I apply for a US visa?

Start 6–12 months ahead for high-demand posts such as those in India and Pakistan. Re-check the live Global Visa Wait Times tool for your consulate, since figures move weekly.

How long does ESTA take?

Apply at least 72 hours before departure at esta.cbp.dhs.gov ($40.27 total as of 2026). Do not board without an approved authorization — carriers check it.

Can US visa processing be expedited?

Post-interview administrative processing cannot be expedited. I-129 premium processing ($2,965, 15 business days, from 1 March 2026) speeds only the USCIS petition stage of work routes — not interview queues.

How long can I stay in the US once admitted?

Commonly up to 6 months per admission on a B-1/B-2 visa — check the I-94 date after every entry, since the officer can set a shorter stay. VWP/ESTA visits are capped at 90 days with no extension and no change of status.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Interview scheduling is the timeline: check the State Department’s Global Visa Wait Times tool for your post, start 6–12 months ahead at high-demand consulates, and never book non-refundable travel before the visa is issued. ESTA travellers need approval at least 72 hours before departure — for everyone else, assume the wait, not the fee, is the constraint.
