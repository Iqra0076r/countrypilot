Does the country actually have a digital-nomad route?

Canada does not present a single permanent “digital nomad visa” comparable to dedicated remote-worker residence programs; visitors and workers must comply with their actual status and work-authorisation rules. Private sites such as VisaGuide are useful for discovering programmes, but the government page controls whether the route is open and who qualifies.
