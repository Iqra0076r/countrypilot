Georgia eVisa 2026: Fees, How to Apply & Who Needs One

What the Georgia e-Visa is

The Georgia e-Visa is an electronic short-stay authorization issued through the official portal evisa.gov.ge. It is fully online — no sticker in the passport — and must be approved before travel. Each entry permits a 30-day stay, and the visa itself is valid for 120 days.

Georgia has no visa-on-arrival programme. If your nationality is outside the visa-free list, the e-Visa is the route — get it before you fly, not at the border.

Who needs the e-Visa — and who enters visa-free

Check the visa-free list before applying, because you may not need a visa at all. Many nationalities — including the US and UK — enter visa-free for up to a full year. Holders of valid US, UK, Schengen, Canadian, Australian, Japanese, Korean, New Zealand, Israeli, Irish or GCC visas or residence permits get 90 days visa-free in any 180-day period.

The e-Visa exists for nationalities outside those exemptions — including travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh. Study and work never ride on the e-Visa: those use D-category immigration visas (US$20 consular fee) through the consular channel. The all-in e-Visa cost is roughly US$36–37: US$20 consular fee, the 2% service charge, and the mandatory US$15 DuVerify identity check. The check is not optional and sits on top of the official fee — any site quoting only "US$20" is undercounting.

How to apply on evisa.gov.ge

  • Use evisa.gov.ge only. It is the official channel; every other "Georgia e-Visa" domain is a private business.
  • Check the visa-free list first. A full-year visa-free entry beats a paid e-Visa every time.
  • Complete the application on the passport you will travel with — verify the approval is linked to the current passport.
  • Pay US$20 + 2% through the portal, then complete the US$15 DuVerify identity check.
  • Wait for the decision, then save the approval offline and printed — phone screens fail at the wrong moment.

Processing time and application timeline

Third parties report e-Visa approval in 5–7 working days, longer in peak summer — but no official processing SLA is published. Treat the reported times as planning estimates, not promises: apply well before booking non-refundable flights, and treat anything closer to departure as a gamble, not a plan.

The practical timeline: 1) confirm your nationality needs the e-Visa, 2) apply at evisa.gov.ge with the travel passport, 3) pay the US$20 + 2% and complete the US$15 identity check, 4) wait for the decision, 5) verify the approval details before booking non-refundable travel.

2026 gotchas

  • No visa on arrival exists. The e-Visa must be approved before you board — do not plan a "transit without visa" routing through Tbilisi unless your nationality is visa-exempt.
  • The US$15 DuVerify check is mandatory. It is a third-party identity check on top of the US$20 consular fee; forgetting it understates your budget.
  • 30 days per entry, 120-day validity. The e-Visa is not extendable in the ordinary course — plan departure accordingly.
  • Visa-free does not mean work-free. The 1-year exemption is for visiting; work needs the D-category route with real financial evidence.
  • Never invent a proof-of-funds number. The MFA requires proof of financial support for the D3 study visa but states no minimum amount — provide genuine statements, not a fabricated threshold.
  • Insurance is an MFA condition for D-visas. Travel insurance covering accidents above 30,000 GEL is required for D-category immigration visas.

Copycat sites and scam avoidance

The official portal is evisa.gov.ge — the only legitimate online channel for short-stay visas (D-category applications run through the consular channel, geoconsul.gov.ge per the MFA). Copycat "Georgia e-Visa" domains charging multiples of US$20 are not official; lookalike branding and "guaranteed approval" language are the tell-tales.

Never pay a visa fee to a personal bank account, mobile-money number or crypto wallet. Official fees go through the portal's designated channels only.

Pre-travel e-Visa checklist

  • Visa-free exemption ruled out for your nationality on the official list
  • Application completed at evisa.gov.ge on the passport you will travel with
  • US$20 + 2% paid; US$15 DuVerify identity check completed; receipts saved
  • Approval verified (dates, entries, name spelling); saved offline and printed
  • Passport valid for the duration of the stay, with blank pages
  • Return ticket, accommodation and funds evidence ready for the border
  • Travel insurance arranged — see the Georgia travel insurance and health guide
  • Study/work plans matched to the D-category route, not the e-Visa

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official Georgia eVisa website?

The official e-Visa portal is https://www.evisa.gov.ge. Any other domain offering "Georgia e-Visas" is a private business, not the authority.

How much does the Georgia eVisa cost in 2026?

US$20 plus a 2% service charge, plus a mandatory third-party DuVerify identity check at US$15 — budget US$36–37 all-in. D-category immigration visas carry a US$20 consular fee.

Who needs a Georgia eVisa and who enters visa-free?

Many nationalities (including the US and UK) enter visa-free for up to a year; holders of valid US, UK, Schengen and other listed visas/residence permits get 90/180 days visa-free. Travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, Bangladesh and other non-exempt nationalities use the e-Visa.

How long does the Georgia eVisa take?

Reported at 5–7 working days (longer in peak summer), but this is not an official SLA — apply well ahead and do not book non-refundable travel before approval.

Is there visa on arrival in Georgia?

No. There is no visa-on-arrival programme — the e-Visa must be approved before travel.

What financial proof does the Georgian student visa need?

The MFA requires proof of financial support (a letter or 3 months of bank statements) but states no minimum amount. Provide genuine evidence; do not invent a threshold. D-category visas also require insurance covering accidents above 30,000 GEL.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

The Georgia e-Visa in 2026 is a purely online authorization at evisa.gov.ge: US$20 plus a 2% service charge, with a mandatory US$15 DuVerify identity check, approved before you fly for a 30-day stay. But check the visa-free list first — you may enter free for up to a year. No visa on arrival exists, processing estimates are unofficial, and study or work needs the D-category route with real financial evidence and 30,000+ GEL insurance. Ignore every copycat domain.
