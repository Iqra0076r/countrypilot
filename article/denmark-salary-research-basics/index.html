Salary Research Basics in Denmark sits at the intersection of the labour market and immigration law. A candidate can be qualified for the job but ineligible for the permit—or eligible for a permit but unrealistic for the vacancy. This guide separates qualification/registration, employer requirements, salary research and the legal work-authorisation process.

Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

Denmark has route-specific work schemes administered through SIRI/New to Denmark. Salary, occupation-list, employer and qualification requirements vary by scheme; applicants should identify the exact scheme before evaluating a job offer.

Official page: https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Work

Separate the job search from immigration eligibility

Search for jobs and immigration eligibility in parallel. The correct SIRI scheme, salary/occupation conditions and employer facts must be matched precisely. Create a one-page route summary before applying: route name, sponsor requirement, occupation rule, salary/skill requirement, language rule, professional registration and whether dependants can accompany.

This prevents a common failure: investing months in a job that cannot support the intended immigration route. Confirm the current position using EU Immigration Portal in the source list below.

How to verify an employer or sponsor

Use official sponsor/accreditation registers where they exist, the company's own careers site, business registry details and a professional email domain. Compare the legal employer name on the offer with the entity in the sponsor register. A recruitment agency may be legitimate, but it should be clear who the actual employer is. Confirm the current position using Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration (SIRI) / New to Denmark in the source list below.

Red flags include payment for a job offer, requests to reimburse prohibited sponsor costs, interviews conducted only through anonymous messaging apps, contracts with a different company name, and salary promises that are inconsistent with the written offer. Confirm the current position using Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration (SIRI) / New to Denmark in the source list below.

Build an overseas-ready application

A useful international CV is evidence-led: role, employer, dates, quantified responsibilities, tools/technologies, regulated credentials and language ability. Remove vague objective statements and focus on the requirements in the vacancy. Confirm the current position using Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration (SIRI) / New to Denmark in the source list below.

In the cover letter, answer the employer's risk questions directly: Are you eligible for the occupation? Do you need sponsorship? Are your qualifications recognised? When can you relocate? If the employer uses a formal sponsorship programme, say which route you believe applies without pretending to give legal advice. compare it with the current Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration (SIRI) / New to Denmark guidance linked below.

Offer-stage checks

Before resigning or paying relocation costs, verify salary, hours, probation, location, remote-work expectations, relocation support, visa costs, health insurance and family benefits. Check whether the contract is conditional on immigration approval. verify it against Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration (SIRI) / New to Denmark before committing money or documents.

A genuine offer can still be unsuitable if the salary fails the immigration threshold, the job title does not match the actual duties, or the employer is not eligible to sponsor. Confirm the current position using EU Immigration Portal in the source list below.

From job offer to lawful work

The employer and applicant may have separate steps. In some systems the employer first obtains accreditation, sponsorship approval, labour-market approval or a job check; only then can the worker apply. In others, the processes are linked. Use https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Work to map the actual sequence.

Do not start work until the status authorises it. Entry as a visitor or student does not automatically permit full-time employment. re-check EU Immigration Portal before relying on it.

How to research salary properly

Use several sources: official occupation data where available, major job boards, professional associations and real vacancies in the same city/region. Compare base salary separately from bonus, overtime, housing and pension.

For immigration, use the salary definition in the visa rules rather than a market-salary website. Some routes exclude allowances or use an hourly/annualised calculation.
