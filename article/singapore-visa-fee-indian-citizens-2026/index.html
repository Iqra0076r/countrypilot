How this Singapore visa fee for Indian citizens 2026 guide works

Singapore's tourist-visa fee is a single flat ICA processing charge of S$30 per application, non-refundable. The unusual part is the channel: individual travellers don't apply direct — an authorized agent (often a travel agency) submits the application to ICA on your behalf. The S$30 is the government component; the agent adds their own service charge on top.

What Indian citizens actually pay

Indian citizens need a tourist visa before travel: up to 30 days per visit. Apply through an authorized agent — direct individual applications aren't the normal channel — and expect 3–5 working days' processing. The S$30 ICA fee is fixed, so if an agent quotes far more, the difference is their service charge: ask what it covers. You still need the SG Arrival Card before flying, visa or not.

What the quoted fee does not include

The S$30 is the ICA processing fee only. It does not include the authorized agent's service charge — the main variable in what you actually pay — nor courier fees or document preparation. Because the fee is non-refundable, a refused or withdrawn application still costs the S$30.

Where and how to apply

Choose an authorized agent (your travel agency usually qualifies), submit your documents and pay the S$30 ICA fee plus the agent's service charge. ICA processes most applications in 3–5 working days. Separately, every traveller must complete the SG Arrival Card before entering Singapore.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

The S$30 fee is fixed — agents adding large 'express' surcharges for the standard 3–5-day service are padding their margin, not buying you speed. Pay through the agent's official channel with a receipt, and be wary of anyone promising a guaranteed visa: ICA decides, and the fee is non-refundable either way.
