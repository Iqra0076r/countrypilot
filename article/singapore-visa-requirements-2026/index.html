Singapore Visa Requirements 2026

Singapore visa requirements 2026: who needs a visa (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh), visa-free entry, the 6-month passport rule, SG Arrival Card, 96-hour transit.

Quick Answer

In 2026, most Western nationalities (US, UK) enter Singapore visa-free for short visits (typically 30 days), while nationals of India, China, Pakistan and Bangladesh need a tourist visa — S$30, 3–5 working days, via an authorized agent. Who needs a visa — and who enters visa-free

Singapore splits travellers into two clear groups in 2026. Visa-free entry: most Western nationalities — including the US and UK — enter visa-free for short visits (typically 30 days). Tourist visa required: nationals of countries including India, China, Pakistan and Bangladesh must hold a visa before travel.

Passport validity: 6 months on arrival

Singapore requires the passport to be valid for at least 6 months on arrival — one of the stricter validity rules in Asia, and airlines enforce it at boarding. Renew before travel if yours is close. Every traveller, including children, needs a separate passport, kept in good condition with blank pages for immigration procedures.

The SG Arrival Card: free, mandatory, not a visa

Every traveller — visa-free nationalities included — must submit the SG Arrival Card, the free electronic arrival and health declaration, via ICA within 3 days before arrival (including the arrival day). It is not a visa, but entry requires it. There is no vaccination certificate for ordinary visitors and no pandemic-era border health checks; yellow-fever certificates apply only for arrivals from endemic countries.

Applying for the tourist visa

The tourist visa is agent-mediated: individuals generally cannot apply direct, so you apply through an authorized visa agent. The processing fee is S$30 per application (2026, non-refundable) plus the agent's service charge, and decisions typically take 3–5 working days.

Prepare the passport bio page, a photograph meeting ICA specifications, the agent's completed form, and evidence of purpose and funds — return ticket, hotel bookings, employment at home and prior compliant travel all strengthen the file, since decisions turn on intent to leave. Documents not in English need certified English translations. There is no interview as standard. For the full fee and timing breakdown, see our Singapore visa fee 2026 guide.

Transit: the 96-hour VFTF — and no visa on arrival

Singapore offers the 96-hour Visa-Free Transit Facility (VFTF) — free — for eligible transit passengers: nationals of countries including India and CIS states who are transiting to or from a third country, hold an onward ticket departing within 96 hours, and hold a valid visa or long-term pass for Australia, Canada, Germany, Japan, New Zealand, Switzerland, the UK or the USA.

There is no general visa-on-arrival outside the VFTF. If you don't meet the VFTF conditions and your nationality requires a visa, arrange it before travel — Changi will not issue one at the counter.

How long you can stay — and the overstay line

Short-visit admissions are typically 30 days, set by the ICA officer at entry — the period granted can be shorter than the maximum, and the visit pass cannot be extended casually. Overstaying is an offence with fines and potential imprisonment; it also destroys future applications.

Work and study need separate passes

A tourist visa or visa-free entry never authorizes employment — Singapore enforces this strictly — the passes are employer-petitioned from the start. The Employment Pass (employer applies via the myMOM Portal; S$330 total fees, 2026) carries a qualifying salary rising to S$6,000/month (S$6,600 for financial services) for new applications from 1 January 2027, with an age-progressive ladder reaching S$11,500–S$12,700 at 45+. The S Pass floor is S$3,600/month (S$4,000 financial services) from 1 September 2026.

For students, the institution registers the Student's Pass via SOLAR+ / the ICA e-Service (S$45 + S$60, 2026). There is no fixed government minimum for proof of funds — ICA assesses whether funds cover actual tuition plus living costs for the full program; a practical planning figure is around S$10,000–12,000 per year in living costs, and medical insurance is compulsory (S$200–600/year group plans — see the travel insurance & health guide).

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping the SG Arrival Card. It's free and mandatory — not a visa, but required for entry.
  • Assuming visa-free means no paperwork. Visa-free nationalities still need the Arrival Card.
  • Working on a visit pass. Strictly enforced — the EP route is employer-petitioned from day one.
  • Budgeting the old EP salary. S$6,000/month (S$6,600 finance) applies to new applications from 1 January 2027.
  • Overstaying 'just a few days'. An offence with fines and possible imprisonment — and it kills future applications.
  • Applying for the tourist visa direct. Use an authorized agent; direct individual applications aren't the normal channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Singapore?

Yes. Indian passport holders need a tourist visa applied for through an authorized agent — S$30 processing fee (2026, non-refundable), typically decided in 3–5 working days. The free 96-hour VFTF covers eligible transit passengers with onward tickets and a qualifying onward visa.

What is the SG Arrival Card?

A free electronic arrival and health declaration submitted via ICA within 3 days before arrival (including the arrival day). Mandatory for all travellers — including visa-free nationalities — but it is not a visa.

Which nationalities can enter Singapore visa-free?

Most Western nationalities, including the US and UK, enter visa-free for short visits (typically 30 days). Nationals of India, China, Pakistan and Bangladesh need a tourist visa — confirm any other nationality on the ICA visa-requirements list.

Can I get a visa on arrival in Singapore?

No — there is no general visa-on-arrival outside the free 96-hour VFTF, which covers eligible transit passengers (including Indian and CIS nationals) with an onward ticket within 96 hours and a qualifying onward visa.

How long can I stay in Singapore as a tourist?

Typically 30 days per visit-pass admission, set by the ICA officer at entry. The visit pass cannot be extended casually, and overstaying is an offence with fines and possible imprisonment.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Singapore in 2026: check whether your nationality needs the S$30 tourist visa (India, China, Pakistan, Bangladesh) or enters visa-free (US, UK — typically 30 days). Keep 6 months' passport validity, file the free SG Arrival Card within 3 days before arrival, never work on a visit pass, and never overstay. Work routes run on the new salary math — S$3,600/month S Pass floor from September 2026, S$6,000/month EP qualifying salary from January 2027. Confirm time-sensitive rules on ica.gov.sg / mom.gov.sg before travelling.
