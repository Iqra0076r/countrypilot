How to use this Philippines tourist visa guide

The Philippines runs two tourist lanes: 30-day visa-free entry under EO 408 for eligible nationalities (no fee, no advance application), and the 9(a) temporary visitor visa from a consulate for nationalities that need one — granted for an initial 59 days. Start at the official portal — the Bureau of Immigration (immigration.gov.ph) — and treat it as the controlling source over any third-party summary.

The two tourist routes: visa-free vs 9(a) visa

Visa-free (EO 408): eligible nationalities — including the United States and United Kingdom — get 30 days on arrival, free. 9(a) temporary visitor visa: travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Bangladesh need this consular visa — single-entry US$30 under the February 2026 fee revision, with an initial stay of 59 days as granted. Filipino citizens enter on a Philippine passport and need no visa.

Entry timeline: arrival to extension

  • Before travel: confirm your route — visa-free on arrival or 9(a) from the consulate.
  • On arrival (visa-free): the officer admits you for up to 30 days — check the admission stamp or electronic record immediately; the permitted stay, not the visa's validity, is what counts.
  • Needing more time: apply at a Bureau of Immigration office before your stay expires — the first 29-day extension costs ~PHP 3,030 and is processed same-day at BI offices.
  • After the 9(a) is issued: verify dates, entries and name spelling immediately — errors are far easier to fix before travel.

Staying longer: extension rules

Both routes can be extended at Bureau of Immigration offices: the first 29-day extension costs ~PHP 3,030 and is issued same-day; subsequent monthly extensions run roughly PHP 4,300–4,800. Never simply remain past your admitted stay — overstaying, even briefly, can void the visa, trigger fines, and complicate every future Philippines application.

2026 gotchas travellers must know

  • No verified eVisa or visa-on-arrival channel. There is no verified Philippine eVisa or visa-on-arrival information for 2026 — if a site sells you one, it is a lookalike. Apply through a consulate or confirm with the embassy.
  • February 2026 consular fee revision. The 9(a) single-entry fee was revised to US$30 in February 2026 — old guides quoting earlier figures are stale.
  • DOJ Department Order No. 159 (9 Mar 2026). An automatic, fee-free visa extension to 1 April 2026 was granted for foreigners stranded by Middle East flight cancellations (no fees, fines or penalties) — a one-off relief measure, not a standing rule.
  • The officer sets your stay. A 9(a) visa's validity and the admitted stay are different things.
  • Pay only through official channels. Never pay a 'visa fee' to a personal bank account, mobile-money number or crypto wallet.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

A visa or visa-free entitlement is permission to travel to the border, not a guarantee of entry. Carry the passport containing the visa, a return or onward ticket, the address where you will stay, proof of funds for the trip, and evidence of ties to home (employment letter, property documents, university enrolment).

Working on a tourist entry is not allowed

Work routes run through employer-sponsored permits, not visitor entry — the verified 2026 figure is PHP 10,130 for a 1-year 9G principal permit plus US$50 for the ACR I-Card, with 9G conversion taking 16 working days. Study requires a student visa and recognised enrolment — confirm the current fee on the official portal.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid well beyond your stay with at least one blank page — airlines enforce validity at boarding and a short-validity passport means denied boarding, not a discussion at the gate. Emergency and temporary passports may face extra scrutiny; confirm acceptance with the embassy. Each child needs their own passport and, where visas apply, their own application.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid well beyond the stay, with a blank page
  • 9(a) visa approved and verified (dates, entries, name spelling) — or visa-free eligibility confirmed
  • Return or onward ticket and accommodation address accessible offline
  • Funds evidence and ties-to-home documents packed (not only on a phone)
  • Travel insurance policy with adequate medical cover
  • Embassy/consulate contact saved for emergencies

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a tourist visa for the Philippines?

It depends on your nationality. Eligible nationalities — including the US and UK — enter visa-free for 30 days under EO 408 (no fee). Travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Bangladesh need a 9(a) temporary visitor visa from a consulate before travel.

How much does a Philippines tourist visa cost in 2026?

Visa-free entry costs nothing (30 days under EO 408). The 9(a) single-entry consular visa is US$30 (Feb 2026 fee revision). Extensions at Bureau of Immigration offices cost ~PHP 3,030 for the first 29 days and roughly PHP 4,300–4,800 monthly thereafter.

How long does Philippines tourist entry processing take?

On arrival for visa-free nationalities — no advance processing. Extensions are issued same-day at BI offices. For the 9(a) consular visa, confirm the current processing time with the specific embassy before booking non-refundable travel.

Can I extend my stay in the Philippines?

Yes — apply at a Bureau of Immigration office before your permitted stay expires. The first 29-day extension is ~PHP 3,030 (same-day); further monthly extensions run ~PHP 4,300–4,800. Overstaying can void the visa and trigger fines.

What changed for Philippines entry rules in 2026?

The 9(a) consular fee was revised to US$30 in February 2026, and DOJ Department Order No. 159 (9 Mar 2026) gave stranded foreigners an automatic fee-free extension to 1 April 2026 after Middle East flight cancellations. No verified eVisa or visa-on-arrival channel exists — confirm anything you read on the official Bureau of Immigration portal.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

The Philippines rewards a route check first: 30 days visa-free for eligible nationalities (free, no advance paperwork), or the 9(a) visa at US$30 for India, Pakistan, Nigeria and Bangladesh — extendable at BI offices (~PHP 3,030 first extension, same-day). There is no verified eVisa or visa-on-arrival channel — apply through a consulate, pay only through official channels, and confirm fees on immigration.gov.ph before you commit.
