The visa types that matter for visitors

Tourist travel goes through the L tourist visa — the standard route for sightseeing. Work and study each need their own permission; a visitor entry never authorizes employment — not remote work for a foreign employer while physically in China, not unpaid help at a family business. Do not enter as a visitor intending to "sort out" work or study later; misrepresentation at entry is a separate ground for refusal or removal.

Passport validity and condition

Most countries — China included — expect your passport to be valid well beyond your trip; six months beyond your planned stay is the common airline-enforced standard. Because the exact rule for China was not verifiable from an official source in our October 2026 check, confirm the requirement on the Chinese consulate or visa application service centre or via the IATA Timatic database your airline uses before you book. Keep at least one blank page and make sure the document is undamaged.

The documents to prepare

Assemble the file before the appointment: the passport; photographs to the published specification; bank statements (3–6 months) showing you can fund the trip; an employment letter with approved leave dates; property or lease documents; prior visas and entry stamps; and the trip itinerary with bookings. Documents in another language need an English (or locally accepted language) translation with a translator's statement of accuracy. Bring originals plus one copy of everything. Book the consulate appointment early; slots are the usual bottleneck.

The 144-hour visa-free transit option

Transit is its own immigration question, separate from entry. A 144-hour visa-free transit route exists for eligible nationalities in transit — verify your eligibility on the official source before relying on it. If you change planes on Chinese soil, check whether the regime requires a transit permission even without leaving the airport; being denied boarding at origin is the common failure mode, not refusal at the Chinese airport.

Biometrics and the in-person step

Where the process requires an in-person step, expect identity checks — fingerprints and a digital photograph are standard at interviews and ports of entry. Answer questions on purpose, employment, family, prior travel and funding briefly and truthfully, matching the application exactly. At the border, devices can be inspected under border-search authority in many countries.

At the border: what officers check

Carry the passport (with the visa), a return or onward ticket, the address where you will stay, and evidence you can fund the trip. Answer questions on purpose, length of stay and funding directly and consistently with your application — contradictions are the fastest route to secondary inspection.

Mistakes that lead to refusal

  • Arriving without a visa where none on arrival exists.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the visa is decided. Processing slips everywhere; the approval comes first, the flights second.
  • Mismatched stories between the application and the interview.
  • Missing onward-travel proof at the border.
  • Trusting a random blog's fee figure. No verified 2026 China fee schedule was findable — get the number from the Chinese consulate or visa application service centre itself. Our China visa fee 2026 guide explains how the fee actually works.
