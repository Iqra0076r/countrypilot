Map the path from temporary status to long-term status

In Austria, Long-term Resident – EU / permanent settlement routes is not simply a longer version of a temporary visa. The qualifying route may depend on years of lawful residence, type of permission, absences, employment, language/integration, taxes or other conditions. Some temporary categories count differently from others.
