Quick Answer

In 2026, a Spain Schengen visa costs €90 for adults (€45 for ages 6–11, free for under-6s) plus the BLS service charge. How to read this Spain fee guide

Spain's fees come from two places. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (exteriores.gob.es) sets consular visa fees collected at consulates, and the Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration — decided through the Oficina de Extranjería — charges residence and work authorization fees through receipted Modelo 790 payment forms. That split is why a work route carries both an extranjería tasa and a consular visa fee. This guide was verified against consular pages and 2026 reporting in October 2026. Every figure below is official; where no official figure exists — such as work-authorization processing times — this guide says so. For the full entry picture, read it alongside the Spain Visa & Entry Requirements Guide.

Schengen visa fees for 2026

The uniform Schengen visa for tourism, business or family visits up to 90 days costs €90 for adults, €45 for children aged 6–11, and is free for children under 6. This fee schedule has been in force since 11 June 2024 and is unchanged for 2026. It is paid in addition to the external provider's service charge: most applicants file through BLS centres, and the BLS service charge sits on top of the €90. Applications are made in person — first-time applicants attend for biometrics — and standard processing is 15 calendar days (up to 45 maximum under the EU Visa Code), so file at least 2–3 weeks before travel.

Work authorization and visa fees

Work routes carry two official cost layers. First, the extranjería fees: Modelo 790-052 at €203.84 for the applicant, plus Modelo 790-062 at €203.84 for the employer where the salary is below 2× the minimum wage (SMI), or €407.71 where the salary is at or above 2× SMI. The 2026 SMI is €1,221/month. Second, the consular work visa of around €80 (2026), paid at the consulate after the authorization is approved. The highly qualified professional route under Ley 14/2013 (decided via UGE) is cheaper on the extranjería side: €73.26 under Modelo 790-038 (2026). On timing, this guide keeps its honesty rule: no officially published processing time exists for Spanish work authorizations, so none is printed. Do not budget a start date around a blog's number — ask the consulate for the current estimate.

Student visa fee and linked costs

The national study visa costs €90 (2026; slight variations exist between consulates). Budget the linked costs too: proof of funds at 100% of IPREM — €600/month (2026 IPREM, unchanged since 2023) — and a compliant health insurance policy of roughly €300–600 per year, with 100% coverage and no copayments, deductibles or waiting periods (2026). Applications must be filed at least 2 months before the programme starts, and the legal decision period is 1 month from submission. The Spain Travel Insurance & Health Guide explains why consulates reject policies that fall short of that standard.

The digital-nomad figure is a threshold, not a fee

A common mix-up: the published 2026 figure for Spain's digital nomad visa is an income threshold, not an application fee. The main applicant must show €2,442/month in 2026 — 200% of the 2026 minimum wage (SMI) of €1,221/month paid in 14 payments, set by Real Decreto 126/2026. The threshold moves with the minimum wage, so 2025 figures are stale. No separately published application fee for the digital nomad route appears in the verified 2026 fee schedules; confirm the consular fee with the competent consulate before paying.

BLS service charge and payment

Beyond the headline visa fees, expect the external provider's service charge (BLS) at submission — it is collected on top of the €90 Schengen fee. Spain offers no eVisa and no online payment shortcut: applications are in person at the consulate or BLS centre, and consular files can be tracked at sutramiteconsular.maec.es. The Spain Travel Tools & Planning Guide covers submission logistics and tracking tools in more detail.

Fee scams to avoid

Official fees are receipted Modelo 790 forms — never pay a 'visa fee' to a personal account. Exteriores.gob.es and the competent consulate are the only official channels. No agent can produce a work authorization faster than the Oficina de Extranjería, and none can quote an official processing time that doesn't exist. Anything priced far above the official fees and BLS charges is selling anxiety, not speed.

Budgeting a real application

Add up the real stack. A straightforward Schengen application: €90 plus the BLS service charge — no other official cost. A cuenta ajena work route: €203.84 (790-052) + €203.84 or €407.71 (790-062) + around €80 consular. A study route: €90 plus the €600/month funds evidence and €300–600/year compliant insurance. Keep the two cost layers — extranjería tasas and consular fees — separate in your spreadsheet, and never budget against a work-authorization timeline no authority has published.

A final fee audit before paying

  • Correct fee matched to the route — Schengen €90 / work 790 stack / student €90
  • BLS service charge factored in on top of the visa fee
  • Modelo 790 payments receipted — never to a personal account
  • Digital-nomad applicants: €2,442/month income threshold evidenced (2026) — it is a threshold, not a fee
  • Student file: €600/month funds evidence + compliant insurance (€300–600/yr)
  • Filed early enough — Schengen at least 2–3 weeks out; student at least 2 months before the programme
  • Consulate contact saved for emergencies

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a Spain tourist (Schengen) visa in 2026?

€90 for adults, €45 for ages 6–11, free for under-6s — in force since 11 June 2024 and unchanged for 2026 — plus the BLS service charge. Apply at least 2–3 weeks before travel; standard processing is 15 calendar days.

How much does a Spain work visa cost in total?

Extranjería fees of €203.84 (applicant) + €203.84 or €407.71 (employer) in 2026, plus a consular work visa of around €80. The highly qualified professional (UGE) route is €73.26 on the extranjería side.

How much is a Spain student visa?

€90 (2026), with slight variations between consulates. File at least 2 months before the programme starts; show €600/month funds evidence and a compliant insurance policy (€300–600/year, 100% cover, no copay/deductible/waiting).

Is there a fee for the Spain digital nomad visa?

The verified figure is the €2,442/month income threshold for the main applicant in 2026 — 200% of the €1,221 SMI set by Real Decreto 126/2026 — a threshold, not a fee. Confirm the consular application fee with the competent consulate.

Can I pay my visa fee to an agent's personal account?

No. Official fees are receipted Modelo 790 forms. Any 'visa fee' collected to a personal account is a scam — exteriores.gob.es and the consulate are the only official channels.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Budget Spain's 2026 fees from the official schedule: €90 Schengen (plus the BLS charge), the Modelo 790 work stack (€203.84 + €203.84/€407.71 + ~€80), the €90 student visa with €600/month funds evidence and €300–600/year compliant insurance — and remember the €2,442/month digital-nomad figure is an income threshold, not a fee. Pay nothing to a personal account, verify everything on exteriores.gob.es, and never plan around a timeline no authority has published.
