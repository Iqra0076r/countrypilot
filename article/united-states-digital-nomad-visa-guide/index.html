Does the country actually have a digital-nomad route?

The United States does not list a general digital-nomad visa. Remote work while physically in the U.S. must be assessed under the person’s actual immigration status and work-authorisation rules. Start with the official immigration portal and identify the legal name of the status. Private sites such as VisaGuide are useful for discovering programmes, but the government page controls whether the route is open and who qualifies.

If there is no dedicated route, do not relabel a tourist visa as a digital-nomad visa. Visitor status may restrict work even when the employer is abroad.
