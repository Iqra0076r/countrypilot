How to use this Oman tourist-visa guide

Oman added a free 14-day tourist visa in August 2026 — joining the paid eVisa and 14-day visa-free scheme for 103 countries. Apply only on the Royal Oman Police portal, evisa.rop.gov.om. (On-arrival visas ended in March 2018.) Full framework: our Oman Visa & Entry Requirements Guide.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid well beyond your stay — airlines routinely apply a six-month caution at check-in. Keep the biodata page clean and carry a scan separately.

Pick the right tourist-visa route

Under ROP Decision No. 109/2026 (Gazette 2 Aug 2026, effective 3 Aug 2026), designated nationalities get a free 14-day tourist visa, convertible before expiry under normal requirements and fees. Otherwise: 14-day visa-free entry for citizens of 103 countries, on-arrival for GCC residents in certain professions, eVisa for the rest. (verified October 2026)

Transit through Muscat

Muscat airside transit generally needs no visa if you do not enter Oman; leaving the airport needs an eVisa or visa-free entry. A transit category, where available, is cheaper and faster.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Carry the passport linked to your eVisa or authorization, a return or onward ticket, proof of funds, accommodation details, and evidence of ties to home (job, enrollment, family). Answer questions consistently with your application — discrepancies are the fastest route to secondary inspection.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Health rules follow where you have recently been: yellow-fever certificates are the classic trigger when arriving from endemic countries. Check requirements close to departure, and carry certificates in original form.

Travel insurance for Oman tourists

Insurance is rarely stamped at the border, but medical care abroad is billed at full price to visitors. Buy comprehensive cover with high medical limits — see our Oman travel insurance & health guide.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

A visa is permission to travel to the border, not an unconditional right of entry — the border officer makes the final call and sets the permitted stay. Check what was granted before leaving the counter, and keep the arrival record for the whole stay.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

A tourist entry never authorizes local work. Work visas carry a government issuance fee of OMR 20 (total process roughly OMR 86–106, 2026, corroborated by Playrol). No official student fee schedule is published — universities usually sponsor the application.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every traveler needs their own passport and visa — children are never covered by a parent's grant. Minors without both parents should carry a consent letter plus birth certificates.

A reliable eVisa application workflow

1) Confirm your route on evisa.rop.gov.om. 2) Assemble the file — passport, funds evidence, itinerary, ties to home. 3) Apply through the official channel only. 4) Pay the fee and keep the receipt. 5) Track it; no non-refundable bookings before the grant.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Paying a lookalike website. Unofficial 'e-visa' intermediaries add large fees — or are outright scams.
  • Quoting stale fees. 2026 repriced many charges; older guides are out of date.
  • Assuming a visa means a fixed stay. The border officer sets the admitted period.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the grant. Apply first, pay for flights after.

Building a stronger application file

Decision-makers want three things: consistency, purpose, and incentive to return (job, studies, property, family). Previous compliant travel is the strongest supporting evidence.

eVisas, official portals and scam avoidance

There is exactly one official portal — everything else is an intermediary or a scam. Start only from https://evisa.rop.gov.om; no one can expedite a government decision for a fee. Red flags: prices far above the official fee, wire or crypto payment pressure, and 'guaranteed approval' language.

Supporting documents checklist

Typical file: passport scan, bank statements or payslips covering several months, employment or enrollment letter, itinerary, and proof of ties to home. Upload clear colour scans — poor images are a routine cause of delays.

Interviews and biometrics

Biometrics (fingerprints and photograph) may be collected at the mission or centre after lodging — watch for the instruction; missing the appointment can void the application.

Families and groups

Each family member — including infants — needs an individual passport and visa, lodged per person. A refusal for one member does not automatically refuse the rest.

Customs and border formalities

Declare cash above the local threshold (commonly the equivalent of USD/EUR 10,000 — verify for your route), and check restricted goods before packing: food, plant and animal products draw the strictest controls, and medications should travel in original packaging with prescriptions for controlled substances.

Extensions and return travel

The free 14-day tourist visa can be converted before expiry into another tourist category under normal requirements and fees. For other grants, check whether extension is allowed; overstaying even by days can trigger re-entry bans.

How to research a route without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) evisa.rop.gov.om — the authority's own pages override everything. 2) The mission's checklists for your nationality. 3) Your government's travel advice. Any page not updated since 2025 is suspect. Our Oman travel tools & planning guide collects the live checks in one place.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid well beyond the trip; eVisa verified against the passport number
  • Free-visa eligibility checked on the official portal
  • Grant conditions understood (work prohibitions, stay limits, conversion rules)
  • Return/onward ticket and accommodation details accessible offline
  • Funds evidence and ties-to-home documents packed
  • Travel insurance with medical + evacuation cover
  • No non-refundable bookings made before the grant

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free tourist visa for Oman in 2026?

Yes. Royal Oman Police Decision No. 109/2026, published in the Official Gazette on 2 August 2026 and effective 3 August 2026, introduced a free (0 OMR) 14-day tourist visa for designated nationalities. It can be converted before expiry into another tourist visa category under normal requirements and fees. Check whether your passport is designated on evisa.rop.gov.om.

How much does an Oman tourist eVisa cost in 2026?

Official Royal Oman Police pricing: OMR 5 for a 10-day single-entry tourist e-Visa and OMR 20 for a 30-day single-entry tourist e-Visa. Citizens of 103 countries also get 14-day visa-free entry without applying.

Who can enter Oman without a visa?

Citizens of 103 countries get 14-day visa-free entry. GCC residents in certain professions retain an on-arrival option. From August 2026, designated nationalities can also use the free 14-day tourist visa route.

How long does Oman tourist eVisa processing take?

No official processing-time SLA is published — apply well ahead of travel and track the application through the official channel.

Can I work in Oman on a tourist visa?

No. Tourist permissions never authorize local work in Oman — real employment needs the proper work authorization (government issuance fee OMR 20), and working on the wrong permission risks removal and future bans.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Oman, the tourist essentials are: pick the right channel for your passport from the route table — the free 14-day visa (Decision No. 109/2026), 14-day visa-free entry for 103 countries, or the OMR 5 / OMR 20 eVisa — apply only through the official ROP portal, carry consistent evidence (passport, return ticket, funds, accommodation), and confirm everything on https://evisa.rop.gov.om before you act.
