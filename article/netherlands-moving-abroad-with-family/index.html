Housing and address registration

Housing shortages make fraud checks, registration eligibility and contract review especially important. Before signing, verify the landlord/provider, what utilities are included, deposit handling, notice period, inventory and whether the address can be used for required registration.

Health care and insurance

Banking, phone and transport
