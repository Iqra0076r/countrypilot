How to use this Azerbaijan visa and entry guide

Visa rules are not a single yes-or-no fact attached to a destination — they depend on your passport, purpose, length of stay, transit airports and sometimes recent travel history. Start with those facts before reading a visa list. The entry-status table below is the fast answer for the most common passports; the detail follows it. Always confirm the decisive rule on the official channel — ASAN Visa portal — before paying or booking.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid for at least three months beyond the eVisa's expiry — the portal states its validity rule at application; six months is the safer standard.

Identify the correct visa or entry route

Azerbaijan's ASAN eVisa at evisa.gov.az is the standard route: USD 29 total (USD 20 state fee + USD 9 service), decided within up to 3 working days (2026). It is a clean, official, state-agency system — apply nowhere else.

There is no general visa on arrival. An urgent eVisa tier is reported (higher fee, faster decision) — confirm it on the portal if you're in a hurry. As always, check whether your nationality qualifies for visa-free entry before paying.

Transit and onward travel

Heydar Aliyev (Baku) airside transit on a through ticket normally needs no visa. Leaving the airport requires the eVisa or an exemption — when in doubt, the $29 eVisa is cheap certainty.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

No official proof-of-funds figure is published for the eVisa. Carry bank statements as a matter of course.

Common documents beyond the visa itself: onward or return ticket, proof of accommodation, and evidence you can support yourself. Print everything — phone batteries die at the worst moments.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

No vaccination certificate required for entry from most countries. Standard travel immunizations apply.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Travel insurance is reported as not mandatory for the eVisa per the official portal — still recommended, as medical costs fall on the visitor.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

Your permitted stay is set by the visa, stamp or entry record — visa validity and authorised duration are not necessarily the same thing. Read the entry stamp and calculate days using the method specified for your route. Overstays in Azerbaijan can mean fines, detention or a future entry ban — know your date.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work and study need separate permits with Azerbaijani sponsorship — the eVisa covers visits only. Category fees are not published on the portal; confirm with the embassy.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Minors generally need their own passport and sometimes a parental consent letter — check the requirements for your departure country too. Dual nationals should check the rules for the passport they plan to enter on, since visa treatment follows the document presented at the border. Refugees, stateless persons and emergency travel-document holders should confirm with Azerbaijan’s embassy directly, as standard routes may not apply.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

  1. Check visa-free eligibility for your nationality on evisa.gov.az.
  2. Apply for the eVisa online — USD 29 total (2026).
  3. Upload passport scan and photograph as requested.
  4. Allow up to 3 working days (a week is comfortable).
  5. Print the eVisa approval and carry it with your passport.

The eVisa needs a passport scan and a compliant photograph entered on the ASAN portal. Have accommodation details ready — the form asks for them.

Common mistakes to avoid

    Using a copycat site. The official portal is evisa.gov.az (ASAN) — lookalikes charge markups. Expecting visa on arrival. There is no general VoA — arrive with the eVisa. Ignoring the urgent tier's uncertainty. Verify it on the portal rather than a blog. Skipping insurance. Not mandatory, but medical costs are yours. Applying last-minute. Three working days excludes weekends — plan a week.

Only use the official channel — ASAN Visa portal. Copycat sites charge markups or harvest passport data. An eVisa approval is an email or a portal record: carry the confirmation and check it shows your name, passport number and permitted entries.

Translations, certifications and document format

Documents not in the local or accepted language may need certified translations — the official checklist states the rule. Certifications, bank statements and invitation letters should be recent (typically within 3 months). Photograph standards matter more than people expect: follow the portal’s specification exactly.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Biometric capture is not documented for the eVisa. Expect standard passport control on arrival.

Families and group travel

Each traveller needs their own application or authorisation — visas are not transferable within a family or group. Apply together where the portal allows, and carry the same document set for each person. With minors, bring birth certificates or consent documentation in addition to passports.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

A visa is not a customs clearance. Declare cash above the local reporting threshold, check restricted items before packing (medications, drones, satellite equipment and certain food products are common flashpoints), and know that border officials can inspect devices. Keep receipts for high-value items you intend to bring back out.

Departure and future travel

Before leaving, confirm your exit is recorded — keep boarding passes and entry/exit stamps. If you overstayed or had any immigration incident, note it: future applications to Azerbaijan and other countries ask about it. Some nationalities need to deregister or obtain exit clearance after long stays; check before a long-term visit.

How to research a route without getting misled

Rank sources by authority: Azerbaijan’s immigration authority and foreign ministry first, then Timatic/IATA, then consular posts, then recent reputable reporting. Treat aggregator visa sites as leads, not answers — they go stale. If two credible sources conflict, say so in your notes and confirm with the authority; this guide flags conflicts the same way.

A final document audit before travel

Forty-eight hours before departure, check the passport number on the visa or authorisation, validity dates, name spelling, permitted entries and any conditions. Confirm transit airports and terminals have not changed. Put the approval, insurance and accommodation in an offline folder and print critical documents when the destination or airline may expect paper evidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Azerbaijan?

Check exemptions first — otherwise the eVisa at evisa.gov.az (USD 29, up to 3 working days, 2026) is the standard route. No general visa on arrival exists.

How much is the Azerbaijan eVisa?

USD 29 total — USD 20 state fee plus USD 9 service charge (2026, ASAN portal).

How long does the Azerbaijan eVisa take?

Up to 3 working days (reported). An urgent tier is reported — verify on the portal.

Is there visa on arrival in Azerbaijan?

No general visa on arrival. Use the eVisa or a qualifying exemption.

Can I work in Azerbaijan on the eVisa?

No — visits only. Work and study need separate sponsored permits.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Azerbaijan, the ASAN eVisa is the whole story: USD 29 (2026), up to 3 working days, at evisa.gov.az. No visa on arrival, no copycat sites — apply a week ahead and carry the printed approval.
