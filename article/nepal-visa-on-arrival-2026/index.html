How to use this Nepal visa-on-arrival guide

Visa on arrival is the default route for most Nepal visitors: USD 30 / 50 / 125 for 15 / 30 / 90 days, multiple entry, at Kathmandu, Pokhara and Gautam Buddha airports and land borders. Complete the pre-arrival form at nepaliport.immigration.gov.np — the barcode receipt speeds up the counter, though it is a pre-registration and not a full e-visa. Citizens of Afghanistan, Cameroon, Ethiopia, Ghana, Iraq, Nigeria, Somalia, Syria and Zimbabwe must get a visa in advance; Indians enter visa-free. Unusual cases: see our Nepal Visa & Entry Requirements Guide.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid well beyond your stay — airlines commonly enforce a six-month caution at check-in. Keep a blank page for stamps, the biodata page undamaged, and a photo/scan stored separately.

Identify the correct visa or entry route

Visa on arrival at airports and land borders for most nationalities, except the advance-visa-only list. Indians enter visa-free. (verified October 2026)

Transit and onward travel

Airside transit at Kathmandu generally needs no visa; land-border crossings need entry permission. Secure it before the trip — denied boarding at origin is the common failure mode.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Carry the passport, a return or onward ticket, proof of funds, accommodation details, and ties to home. Check https://www.immigration.gov.np — and stay consistent, since contradictions mean secondary inspection.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Yellow-fever certificates are the classic trigger for arrivals from endemic countries. Check with the airline close to departure; carry certificates in original form.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Not a visa-on-arrival condition, but medical care abroad is billed at full price to visitors. Buy comprehensive cover before departure — our Nepal Travel Insurance & Health Guide covers medical and evacuation planning.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

The counter is the decision: the stay (15, 30 or 90 days) matches the fee you paid. Check the stamp before leaving — the days admitted are your legal limit.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work: a visa on arrival never authorizes employment. Study: official 2026 fees are USD 30/month academic (75 non-academic, 50 research; Bangladesh 15, Maldives 25, Bhutan 15, Pakistan/Sri Lanka gratis), with proof of funds of USD 3,000 per 6 months. Gotcha: the 2025/26 budget announced free student visas — verify implementation before planning around it.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every traveler — children included — needs their own passport and own visa on arrival. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter plus birth certificates.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

1) Confirm your nationality is not on the advance-only list. 2) Complete the pre-arrival form and save the barcode receipt offline. 3) At the counter, pay USD 30 / 50 / 125 for 15 / 30 / 90 days. 4) Check the stamped stay. Advance-only nationalities must apply at a mission first.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Paying a lookalike website — start only from nepaliport.immigration.gov.np.
  • Quoting stale fees — the 2026 figures are USD 30 / 50 / 125.
  • Assuming the free student visa is live — announced, not yet confirmed on the portal.
  • Arriving unprepared — bring the barcode receipt, return ticket and funds evidence.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Officers look for consistency, purpose, and incentive to return. Prior compliant travel history is the strongest evidence. Address any prior refusal with new evidence.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

The pre-arrival form at nepaliport.immigration.gov.np is the only official online step — a barcode receipt, not a full e-visa. Red flags: fees far above the official schedule, wire/crypto pressure, 'guaranteed approval'.

Translations, certifications and document format

Typical file: passport scan, bank statements or payslips covering several months, employment or enrollment letter, itinerary, ties to home. Documents in other languages generally need certified translations.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

No pre-travel interview for visa on arrival, but officers may question any traveler — carry evidence matching your pre-arrival form. Biometrics may be collected at the counter.

Families and group travel

Each family member — including infants — needs an individual passport and an individual visa on arrival. A refusal for one member does not automatically refuse the rest.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Declare cash above the local threshold (commonly USD/EUR 10,000 equivalent) and check restricted goods before packing. Medications belong in original packaging with prescriptions. See our Nepal Travel Safety & Security Guide for border-day details.

Departure and future travel

Check the Department of Immigration's extension rules and apply through the proper channel before your permission expires — even short overstays can trigger re-entry bans.

How to research a route without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) immigration.gov.np overrides everything. 2) The mission's checklists. 3) Your government's travel advice. 2026 repriced many fees — pages not updated since 2025 are suspect.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid well beyond the trip; advance-only list checked
  • Pre-arrival barcode receipt saved offline
  • USD fee ready for the counter (15 / 30 / 90-day option chosen)
  • Return/onward ticket and accommodation accessible offline
  • Funds evidence and ties-to-home documents packed
  • Travel insurance with medical + evacuation cover
  • Required declarations prepared (cash, customs, health)
  • No non-refundable bookings before the grant

Frequently Asked Questions

Who qualifies for Nepal's visa on arrival?

Most nationalities, at airports and land borders. Afghanistan, Cameroon, Ethiopia, Ghana, Iraq, Nigeria, Somalia, Syria and Zimbabwe must apply in advance. Indians enter visa-free.

How much does Nepal's visa on arrival cost in 2026?

USD 30 (15 days) / USD 50 (30 days) / USD 125 (90 days), multiple entry (2026).

Do I need the online pre-arrival form?

Strongly recommended: nepaliport.immigration.gov.np generates a barcode receipt that speeds up the counter. It is a pre-registration, not a full e-visa.

Are student visas really free in Nepal now?

The 2025/26 budget announced free student visas — verify implementation on the official portal before planning around it.

Can I extend a Nepal visa on arrival?

Check the Department of Immigration's extension rules and apply before your permission expires — do not overstay.
