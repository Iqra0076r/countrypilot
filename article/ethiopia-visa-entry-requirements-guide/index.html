How to use this Ethiopia visa and entry guide

This guide covers how visitors enter Ethiopia — which authority decides, what it costs, and what changed recently. The deciding source throughout is the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et): Ethiopia's e-Visa is available online to citizens of all countries for tourism — single entry up to 30 days, with 90-day and business/transit variants — under the 2026 fee schedule (Regulation 587/2026).

The headline numbers: visitor $82; student $150. This guide was verified against the sources listed below in October 2026. Fees and processing times move — re-check the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) before paying anything or booking non-refundable travel.

Passport validity and document condition

Airlines — not border officers — are the usual enforcers of passport validity, and they go by Timatic. For Ethiopia, verify the current rule on the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) before travel; plan on six months' validity beyond your stay unless the official source says otherwise.

One blank page minimum, document in good condition, and details matching every booking exactly.

Transit and onward travel

When your itinerary routes through Ethiopia to a third country, the transit permission — if one is required for your passport — must be secured before the trip. Airlines enforce it at departure; Ethiopia airports will not improvise a solution at the counter.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Admission to Ethiopia is decided at the port of entry, not when the visa is issued. Pack an entry folder: passport with visa, e-Visa or approval printout, return ticket, hotel booking or host address, and bank statements or equivalent funds proof.

Officers may ask how the trip is funded and when you leave. Short, truthful, consistent answers beat long explanations every time.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

For Ethiopia, verify any health-entry requirements on the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) shortly before departure. As a rule of thumb, yellow-fever rules depend on where you have recently been, not just your nationality — carry the certificate if you have transited an endemic country.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Check the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) for any insurance mandate tied to your visa type. Either way, travel with real medical cover: visitors have no access to subsidised care in Ethiopia, and one emergency can cost more than a decade of premiums.

If your trip later extends to study, confirm whether the student route has its own insurance requirement — several countries in this group do, and the minimum cover is specified by the authorities, not by you.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

On arrival in Ethiopia, your permitted stay is 30 days. Verify the date on the stamp or system record the same day — errors are far easier to fix at the counter than after you have left the airport.

Do not work on a visitor permission, and do not assume a long-validity visa equals a long stay. Each entry is a fresh admission decision.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Plan the Ethiopia trip around the correct category from the start with work route around $1,500 (as of 2026) and study route around $150 (as of 2026). A visitor permission covers tourism, family visits and (where the rules allow) short business meetings — it does not cover taking a job, enrolling for a degree, or freelancing for local clients.

If the purpose changes, change the permission first. Entering on the wrong status and hoping to fix it later is how people end up refused, removed, or barred.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Children need individual passports and, where applicable, individual visas or e-Visas for Ethiopia — there is no family visa. If a child holds two nationalities, use one passport consistently for the whole journey and make sure any required authorisation matches that passport.

Consent letters for minors travelling without both parents: not universally mandatory, frequently asked for. Bring one.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

Do it in this order and Ethiopia entry admin stays boring (which is the goal): 1) Confirm your nationality's route in the table above. 2) Apply on the official portal (the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) — https://www.evisa.gov.et) and pay only there. 3) Save the approval offline as well as on your phone. 4) Verify the approval is linked to the passport you will actually travel on.

Build in buffer: consular queues and e-Visa approvals both slip, especially in peak season.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Budgeting from a stale fee guide. The Ethiopia visitor figure is $82 (as of 2026) — older pages quote superseded amounts.
  • Travelling on last year's rules. Ethiopia changed entry rules in 2025–2026 — check the policy notes in this guide before assuming the old process still works.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the visa is decided. Processing slips everywhere; the approval comes first, the flights second.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

The Ethiopia application is won on specifics: named employer, dated itinerary, booked (refundable) accommodation, and funds that plausibly cover the trip. Generic statements — ‘tourism’, ‘visiting friends’ — unsupported by documents are the classic weak file.

Answer only what is asked, truthfully and briefly. Volunteering complications you were not asked about rarely helps.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Bookmark the official the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) (https://www.evisa.gov.et) before you need it. For Ethiopia, third-party ‘visa services’ add no authority to your application — at best they retype your answers into the same official form for a fee, at worst they steal your documents.

Pay fees only through the portal's own payment flow, keep every receipt, and never send passport scans over unsecured chat apps to strangers.

Translations, certifications and document format

The Ethiopia supporting file has four piles: who you are, why you are going, how you pay for it, and why you will come back. Cover all four with originals plus copies, translated where needed with a statement of accuracy.

Match every name and date across documents exactly — inconsistencies, even innocent ones, invite the scrutiny you are trying to avoid.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

If your Ethiopia route includes an interview, treat it as a verification conversation: the officer is checking that the person matches the paperwork. Bring the file, answer directly, and do not volunteer unrelated complications.

Fingerprint and photo capture is normal. At ports of entry the same applies, plus possible device inspection — keep the trip's documents accessible, not buried in luggage.

Families and group travel

For Ethiopia, plan one application per person and budget the fees and appointment slots accordingly. Groups should align their interview or submission dates, and everyone should carry their own approval — shared printouts cause confusion at counters.

Where the system allows linked or group submissions, use it; where it does not, do not improvise shortcuts.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Do a customs pre-check for Ethiopia before packing: cash-reporting threshold, food/agriculture restrictions, medication rules, and duty-free allowances. These differ by country and change — the ten minutes of checking beats the argument at the red channel.

Original packaging and prescriptions for all medication; nothing in unlabelled containers.

Departure and future travel

Think of each Ethiopia trip as building a record: compliant entries and exits strengthen every future application to Ethiopia and elsewhere. One overstay can undo years of clean history.

Need more time? File for it properly and early. Letting permission lapse and hoping no one notices is the most expensive mistake on this list.

  3. Timatic / IATA for what the airline will enforce at boarding.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Ethiopia tourist visa or e-Visa in 2026?

$82 (as of 2026). Confirm the live figure on the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) before paying — fees move by regulation.

Does Ethiopia offer visa on arrival or an e-Visa?

Ethiopia's e-Visa is available online to citizens of all countries for tourism — single entry up to 30 days, with 90-day and business/transit variants — under the 2026 fee schedule (Regulation 587/2026).

What changed for Ethiopia visas in 2025–2026?

Council of Ministers Regulation No. 587/2026 (Federal Negarit Gazette, 24 Feb 2026) reset immigration service fees: 5-year permanent residence $3,000 (tripled), private-work temporary residence permit $1,500 ordinary, and introduced a 10-year Golden Visa priced at $10,000 ($12,500 express) requiring $10M investment or $5M with substantial job creation, launching within FY 2026/27.

How long does Ethiopia visa processing take?

Typically approved within 3 business days (advised to apply at least 7 days in advance) (as of 2026). Timelines vary by post and season — treat published figures as planning guides, not promises.

Where is the official Ethiopia application site?

the official e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.et) — https://www.evisa.gov.et. Bookmark it and type it yourself rather than clicking ads; copycat sites buy ads on the portal's own name and charge markups for the same form.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Ethiopia, the route check comes first: Ethiopia's e-Visa is available online to citizens of all countries for tourism — single entry up to 30 days, with 90-day and business/transit variants — under the 2026 fee schedule (Regulation 587/2026). Visitor entry costs $82 (as of 2026). Note the 2025–2026 rule change described above — it is the thing most likely to catch return visitors out.
