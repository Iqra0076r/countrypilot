UK Student Visa Fee 2026

The UK Student visa fee for 2026 is £558 for the application itself when you apply outside the UK — and the Immigration Health Surcharge of £776 per year of leave is paid on top. This guide breaks down both, plus the maintenance-money rule most applicants underestimate.

For 2026 (April 8 fee schedule), the UK Student visa costs £558 for the application when applying outside the UK, plus the Immigration Health Surcharge of £776 per year of leave, paid upfront for the whole grant. On top of the fees, Student applicants must show maintenance funds of £1,529 per month in London or £1,171 per month elsewhere, for up to 9 months, held for 28 days. The £558 application fee — what it is and when it is paid

The Student route fee is set by the Home Office fee schedule. For 2026, under the 2026 UK visa fee schedule that took effect on 8 April 2026, the fee for a Student visa application made outside the UK is £558. This is the application fee alone: it pays for UKVI to decide your application and it is non-refundable once the application is submitted and processed.

The £558 is paid as part of the online application on gov.uk, in the payment step of the official flow. When you apply from outside the UK, the fee is collected in the local currency equivalent of the sterling figure.

The £776 Immigration Health Surcharge

The Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) is separate from the application fee and is paid on top of it. In 2026 it is £776 per year for students, dependants, Youth Mobility applicants and under-18 applicants, and it is paid upfront for the whole grant of leave — so a three-year visa means three times the annual figure, in one payment.

The surcharge is mandatory for students. It buys access to the NHS on broadly the same basis as UK residents. Private health insurance does not exempt you from paying it: even with a comprehensive private policy, the surcharge is still collected. Treat the IHS as the largest line item in the budget — for most degree courses it exceeds the application fee several times over.

Maintenance funds: the cost that is not a fee

On top of the fees, Student applicants must show maintenance funds — money you must have available, not a fee paid to anyone. In 2026 the requirement is £1,529 per month for courses in London or £1,171 per month elsewhere, for up to 9 months of the course, held for 28 days. For a 9-month academic year in London that is £13,761 of available funds; outside London, £10,539.

Because the rule is about availability, not payment, this is the figure most applicants underestimate: it sits in your account as evidence, and the 28-day holding rule means it must have been there continuously. Fees you have already paid toward tuition may reduce the amount you need to show as maintenance — check the exact reduction on the live Student visa money guidance before you rely on it, since it depends on what your education provider's sponsorship documents confirm.

How to pay and what happens after

Every UK application is built around the gov.uk online system. Pay inside the official flow — third-party "UK visa" sites that charge several times the official fees are not faster and not official. After the payment step, you book a biometrics appointment at a visa application centre (TLScontact or VFS Global), where your photograph and fingerprints are taken. Upload document scans through the centre's self-upload service before the appointment, or pay for assisted scanning at the centre.

Common mistakes that cost money

  • Budgeting only the £558. The application fee is the smallest line item; the IHS (£776 per year, paid for the whole grant) and the maintenance-money requirement (up to 9 months at £1,529/£1,171) are where the real budget sits.
  • Paying through an unofficial site. Copycat sites charge several times the official fees for submitting the same application. Only gov.uk issues the visa.
  • Quoting outdated fees. The 8 April 2026 fee round invalidated a large share of older guides — treat any page still quoting pre-April-2026 figures as out of date.
  • Applying before the maintenance money is ready. The fees are non-refundable once processed; assemble the maintenance evidence first, then pay, not the other way round.
  • Forgetting the maintenance-money buffer. A balance that only just meets the requirement in your home currency can fall below it before the application is assessed — leave a margin.

A final fee checklist before you submit

  • Application fee confirmed on the live gov.uk fee page (£558 for outside-UK Student applications in the 2026 schedule)
  • IHS calculated for the full grant of leave at £776 per year — budgeted and paid upfront
  • Maintenance funds in place: £1,529/month (London) or £1,171/month (elsewhere), up to 9 months, held 28 days
  • Any tuition already paid reflected correctly in the maintenance calculation
  • Payment made inside the official gov.uk flow, never through a third-party site

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the UK Student visa fee in 2026?

£558 for the application when you apply outside the UK, under the fee schedule in force from 8 April 2026. The Immigration Health Surcharge of £776 per year of leave is paid on top, upfront for the whole grant. Always re-confirm on the live gov.uk fee page before paying, since fees change by regulation.

Is the health surcharge paid on top of the application fee?

Yes. The £776-per-year Immigration Health Surcharge is separate from and additional to the £558 application fee. It is mandatory for students, dependants, Youth Mobility and under-18 applicants, and it buys NHS access on broadly the same basis as residents.

Does private health insurance exempt me from the surcharge?

No. Private insurance does not exempt you from the Immigration Health Surcharge — it is still collected at the 2026 rate of £776 per year of leave, paid upfront for the whole grant.

How much maintenance money do I need for a UK Student visa?

£1,529 per month for courses in London, £1,171 per month elsewhere, for up to 9 months, held for 28 days. This is money you must have available — it is evidence, not a fee — and it is the figure most applicants underestimate. Tuition already paid may reduce the amount; verify the reduction on the live Student visa money guidance.

Do UK visa fees change during the year?

They can: the Home Office raised a broad set of immigration fees on 8 April 2026. Older guides quoting pre-April-2026 figures are out of date, so confirm every figure on gov.uk on the day you submit rather than relying on a cached number.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For a UK Student visa in 2026, budget three things: the £558 application fee, the £776-per-year Immigration Health Surcharge for the whole grant, and maintenance money of £1,529/month in London or £1,171/month elsewhere for up to 9 months, held for 28 days. Pay inside the official gov.uk flow, prepare the maintenance evidence before you pay the non-refundable fees, and re-confirm every figure on the live fee and money pages before submitting.
