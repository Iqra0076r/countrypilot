UK Student Visa Fee 2026: Application Fee, IHS, and Total Cost

The student visa's sticker price is only the first line of its cost. Here is the full 2026 stack — application fee, health surcharge, and the funds you must show.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20267 min read

A UK Student visa costs far more than its application fee suggests, because the Immigration Health Surcharge is mandatory and paid upfront for the whole course — and because the maintenance-funds requirement dwarfs both. This guide totals the real 2026 cost of the Student route and explains each layer.

CountryPilot Research Guide · United Kingdom · Visas & Immigration

Source-led planning guide · Updated October 14, 2026

Quick Answer

The UK Student visa application fee is £558 (2026), plus the Immigration Health Surcharge at £776 per year, paid upfront for the course length. On top of both, you must show maintenance funds of £1,529/month in London or £1,171/month elsewhere (up to 9 months), held for 28 days. The service standard is a decision in 3 weeks outside the UK from biometrics.

Application £558 · IHS £776/year · Maintenance £1,529 / £1,171 per month · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 7 minutes

Layer 1: the £558 application fee

The Student visa itself costs £558 to apply for from outside the UK (2026). It is paid online with the application, it is non-refundable, and it covers processing only. The application requires a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed sponsor — without the CAS reference, there is no application to pay for.

Layer 2: the Immigration Health Surcharge — £776 per year

The Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) is mandatory for students, dependants, Youth Mobility and under-18 applicants: £776 per year in 2026, paid upfront for the full visa length. A three-year course means three years of surcharge in one payment — this is routinely the largest single line in the visa cost. It was unchanged in the 8 April 2026 fee round, but confirm the current rate on GOV.UK before paying, because surcharge levels move by regulation.

Layer 3: maintenance funds and the 28-day rule

Beyond fees, you must prove you can support yourself: £1,529 per month in London or £1,171 per month elsewhere, for up to 9 months (2026) — and the money must have been held for 28 days ending no more than 31 days before the application. The 28-day rule is the detail that refuses real applications: the closing balance date, not the application date, is what counts, and any dip below the required level inside the window restarts the clock. Our 28-day rule explainer walks through it precisely.

The decision timeline

The published service standard for the Student visa is 3 weeks outside the UK, measured from the biometrics appointment (2026). It is a target, not a guarantee — in busy periods around autumn intake, 6–8 weeks before travel is the safer application point. Inside the UK, the standard is 8 weeks. Full detail in our UK processing-time guide.

Totaling the real cost

A realistic budget adds all three layers plus the first year of tuition the CAS is built on: £558, plus £776 × course years, plus maintenance at the London or non-London rate × months, plus tuition. Run the full figure through our study-abroad budget calculator before the CAS is even issued — discovering the total at the payment screen is how timelines slip a whole intake.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a UK student visa in 2026?

£558 application fee plus the £776/year Immigration Health Surcharge — before maintenance funds and tuition.

How much maintenance money do I need?

£1,529/month in London or £1,171/month elsewhere, up to 9 months, held for 28 days (2026).

How long does the student visa decision take?

3 weeks outside the UK from biometrics — a service-standard target, not a guarantee.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

£558 to apply, £776 per year of health surcharge upfront, and months of maintenance money held 28 days without a dip: the student visa's real price is the stack, not the sticker — total it before you start.
