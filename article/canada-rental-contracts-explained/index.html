Read the rental contract line by line

Rental law is provincial or territorial, so deposits, notices and tenant rights vary by location. Record rent, deposit, payment date, notice period, fixed term, utilities, inventory, repair responsibilities, guest/subletting rules and move-out requirements. Ask for unclear terms in writing.
