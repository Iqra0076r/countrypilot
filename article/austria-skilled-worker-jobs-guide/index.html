Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

Austria's Red-White-Red Card has several route-specific categories. For 'Other Key Workers', the official portal lists a 2026 statutory minimum gross monthly pay of €3,465, a labour-market test and a minimum of 55 points. Other RWR categories use different criteria, so that figure should not be copied across all skilled-worker cases.

Official page: https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/other-key-workers/

Separate the job search from immigration eligibility

Search for jobs and immigration eligibility in parallel. The Red-White-Red Card system is criteria-based and may combine points, salary, qualification and labour-market requirements depending on category. Create a one-page route summary before applying: route name, sponsor requirement, occupation rule, salary/skill requirement, language rule, professional registration and whether dependants can accompany.

This prevents a common failure: investing months in a job that cannot support the intended immigration route. How to verify an employer or sponsor

Use official sponsor/accreditation registers where they exist, the company's own careers site, business registry details and a professional email domain. Compare the legal employer name on the offer with the entity in the sponsor register. A recruitment agency may be legitimate, but it should be clear who the actual employer is. Build an overseas-ready application

Offer-stage checks

Before resigning or paying relocation costs, verify salary, hours, probation, location, remote-work expectations, relocation support, visa costs, health insurance and family benefits. Check whether the contract is conditional on immigration approval. From job offer to lawful work

The employer and applicant may have separate steps. In some systems the employer first obtains accreditation, sponsorship approval, labour-market approval or a job check; only then can the worker apply. In others, the processes are linked. Use https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/permanent-immigration/ to map the actual sequence.

Do not start work until the status authorises it. Entry as a visitor or student does not automatically permit full-time employment.
