UK visitor visa fees in 2026

The UK charges fees for almost every immigration application, and the controlling source for every figure on this page is gov.uk (UK Visas and Immigration). On 8 April 2026 the Home Office raised a broad set of fees — the first big fee round in the 2025–26 window — and most of the numbers below reflect that schedule. Because fees change by regulation, confirm the live figure on gov.uk immediately before paying; a fee paid at the old rate is generally not the deciding factor — the fee in force on the day you submit is.

The headline figure for tourists and family visitors: a Standard Visitor visa costs £135 for the 6-month version as of 8 April 2026 — up from £127. That covers stays of up to 6 months per visit. Travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, Bangladesh and other visa-required nationalities apply for this route online before travel. The £135 is paid as part of the online application, after which you book a biometrics appointment at a visa application centre (TLScontact or VFS Global) where your photograph and fingerprints are taken.

Long-term visit visa options

Frequent travellers with a history of compliant UK visits can buy longer-validity visitor visas: £506 for 2 years, £903 for 5 years and £1,128 for 10 years (April 2026 schedule). Each visit is still capped at 6 months — the longer validity only removes the need to re-apply — and caseworkers specifically look for a history of compliant visits before granting these; a record of overstays or frequent back-to-back visits destroys the case for one.

The ETA costs £20

Nationals of visa-exempt countries — including the United States — do not need a visa for short visits but need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA): £20 as of April 2026 (raised from £16). You apply on gov.uk or the official UK ETA app, decisions usually arrive within minutes (officially within 3 working days), and the ETA allows multiple entries over 2 years or until your passport expires, with up to 6 months per visit. Each traveller — including every child — needs their own ETA. The ETA is permission to travel, not a guarantee of entry: Border Force still applies the Standard Visitor eligibility rules on arrival.

Student visa costs

The Student route (outside the UK) costs £558 for the application itself (2026 rate) plus the Immigration Health Surcharge of £776 per year, paid upfront for the whole grant of leave. The surcharge is mandatory for students, dependants, Youth Mobility and under-18 applicants and buys NHS access on broadly the same basis as residents — private insurance does not exempt you from it. On top of the fees, Student applicants must show maintenance funds of £1,529 per month in London or £1,171 per month elsewhere, up to 9 months, held for 28 days — money you must have available, not a fee, but it is the number most applicants underestimate.

Skilled Worker costs

The Skilled Worker route requires a licensed sponsor and a Certificate of Sponsorship, and the fee table splits across employer and applicant. The sponsor pays £525 per Certificate of Sponsorship (2026) plus the Immigration Skills Charge of £480–£1,320 per year, depending on the business size and the length of the grant. The applicant pays £819 for up to 3 years or £1,618 for over 3 years when applying outside the UK (April 2026 schedule). Since 22 July 2025 the skill threshold for this route is degree level (RQF 6), so confirm your occupation qualifies before paying anything.

How and where to pay

Every UK application is built around the gov.uk online system: the visa-checker wizard, the application forms, the payment step, the ETA application, and the eVisa view-and-prove service. Pay inside the official flow — third-party "UK visa" sites that charge several times the £135/£20 official fees are not faster and not official. At the visa application centre, upload document scans through the TLScontact/VFS self-upload service before your biometrics appointment, or pay for assisted scanning at the centre. After approval, visit visas are issued as eVisas — a digital immigration status linked to your passport, proved with a share code — so check the status is linked to the right passport before departure.

Priority and super-priority services

UKVI offers priority and super-priority decision services at some centres for an extra fee, but they are not available everywhere — check at booking. They buy a faster decision, not a better outcome; no agent, adviser or paid service can influence the decision itself. For the standard timelines, see our UK visa processing time 2026 guide.

Scam avoidance

Only gov.uk (and the official UK ETA app) can issue an ETA or visa. Anyone guaranteeing approval is lying, and no agent can influence a UKVI decision. Be wary of content that still quotes the pre-April-2026 fees of £127 and £16 — the fee round invalidated a large share of older guides — and of any page that still presents BRP cards as current.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did the UK visitor visa fee change in 2026?

The Home Office raised a broad set of immigration fees on 8 April 2026 — the Standard Visitor visa went from £127 to £135 and the ETA from £16 to £20, among other changes. Fees change by regulation, so older guides quoting £127 or £16 are out of date.

Does every child need their own ETA?

Yes. Each traveller — including every child — needs their own ETA or visa; there is no family authorisation. Children’s applications are linked to a responsible adult’s in the online system, but the permission is individual and each child needs their own passport.

Is the Immigration Health Surcharge the same as the visa fee?

No. The surcharge (£776 per year for students, dependants, Youth Mobility and under-18 applicants in 2026) is paid in addition to the visa application fee, upfront for the whole grant of leave. It buys NHS access; private insurance does not exempt you from it.

Do I pay extra for priority processing?

Priority and super-priority services exist at some centres for an extra fee, but they are not available everywhere — check at booking. They buy a faster decision, not a better outcome.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For UK visa fees in 2026, the numbers that matter are £135 for the 6-month Standard Visitor visa, £20 for the ETA, £558 plus £776 per year in health surcharge for Students, and £819–£1,618 for Skilled Worker applications outside the UK — all from the 8 April 2026 fee schedule. Pay inside the official gov.uk flow, assemble a strong file first, and re-confirm the fee on gov.uk on the day you submit.
