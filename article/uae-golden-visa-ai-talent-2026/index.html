UAE Golden Visa for AI Talent 2026: Endorsement Routes

By Nadeem Tanwari, Founder of CountryPilotReviewed by CountryPilot editorial deskPublished October 15, 2026 · Updated October 15, 2026

If you work in artificial intelligence, the UAE's Golden Visa offers you something rare: a 10-year, sponsor-free residency where the most interesting door isn't the salary one. AI talent can qualify through an endorsement from the Emirates Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions — a recommendation-based route with no salary figure attached — or through the AED 30,000-a-month professional track. Which door fits you changes the evidence you prepare, the authority you approach, and whether you file in Dubai or Abu Dhabi.

The UAE Golden Visa gives AI professionals 10-year, sponsor-free residency through two routes: the exceptional-talent route, which needs a recommendation from the Emirates Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions (no salary threshold), or the skilled-professional route, which needs at least AED 30,000 a month, a bachelor's degree, and a valid UAE employment contract. Dubai files through GDRFA; Abu Dhabi files through TAMM and the Residents Office.

Two doors for AI professionals: endorsement vs salary

Most Golden Visa advice for tech workers starts and ends with a number: AED 30,000 a month. That number is real — it is the official threshold for skilled professionals in senior occupational levels, published in the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security's Golden Residency guide. But for people in artificial intelligence, it is the wrong place to start. The UAE's framework, as the official u.ae portal lays it out, treats "those with exceptional talent or rare specialisations" as a separate 10-year category — one that includes specialists in priority scientific and engineering fields and runs on recommendation letters, practical experience and accredited credentials rather than pay slips.

The distinction matters because the two doors ask for completely different evidence. The salary door wants a MOHRE-registered employment contract, a degree, and proof you earn at least AED 30,000 monthly. The endorsement door wants someone with authority to say you matter to the country's AI ambitions. An ML researcher with landmark publications but a modest academic salary, a startup CTO whose equity dwarfs her cash compensation, an open-source contributor whose work ships in products used across the Gulf — these are endorsement-door people. Chasing the salary track when your profile is really an endorsement profile means assembling the wrong dossier and getting judged on the wrong criteria.

Note: The endorsement route is not a loophole around the salary rule — it is a separate legal category with its own gatekeeper. You still need a genuinely defensible record in AI or digital technology. The question to ask first is not "do I earn enough" but "who in the UAE's AI ecosystem would vouch for me."

The endorsement door: who actually signs your nomination

The federal Golden Residency guide lists "talents in the field of digital technology" as a 10-year exceptional-talent category, and the competent authority for that talent is named explicitly in Abu Dhabi's official guidance: the Emirates Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions, or a local competent authority in the emirate. Scientists go through the Emirates Scientists Council; industry and Fourth Industrial Revolution specialists through the Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology. AI sits in the digital-technology lane, and the Council is the signature that matters.

What the Council wants to see is achievement with national relevance — the same shape of evidence other exceptional-talent categories use: significant contributions, high impact, recognition by a credible body. For an AI practitioner, that translates into a portfolio built for a reviewer, not a recruiter: published research, shipped systems, patents, technical leadership at scale, contributions to the field that go beyond a job description. The Abu Dhabi Residents Office routes digital-technology nominations through its own webapp to the competent authority, and also accepts applications through the federal ICP portal at icp.gov.ae. Either way, the visa decision follows the recommendation — the endorsement is the application, in substance if not in form.

One practical consequence: this route is genuinely available to people outside the UAE. The ICP guide confirms that eligible applicants abroad receive a six-month, multiple-entry entry visa — renewable for a further six months — to enter the country and complete the Golden Residency procedures. You do not need to be employed in the Emirates, hold a local contract, or have a sponsor waiting. That is the whole point of the exceptional-talent design: the country is buying your future contribution, not your current payroll.

The salary door: AED 30,000 a month and what counts toward it

For AI professionals who are employed in the UAE at senior levels, the professional track is the simpler, more mechanical route. The ICP's published criteria for skilled professionals in senior occupational levels are: a valid work permit in the UAE under a valid and approved employment contract, at least a bachelor's degree or its equivalent, a monthly salary of at least AED 30,000 or its equivalent in foreign currency, and comprehensive, valid health insurance for the applicant and family members throughout the residency. The occupational classification comes from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation — chairpersons and chief executives at occupational level one, specialists at level two — so your registered job title has to sit in the senior band, not just your pay.

The figure to internalize is the monthly one, and to internalize it precisely. The official wording is "at least AED 30,000" per month — a threshold, not a target — and it is assessed against the employment contract and the salary actually paid. AI salaries in Dubai and Abu Dhabi frequently clear this bar for senior engineers, research scientists and engineering managers, which is why the professional track is the workhorse route for employed tech talent. But it is also the route most exposed to paperwork failure: a contract that says one number while the bank shows another, a job title registered at the wrong MOHRE level, or insurance that lapses will all stall a file that otherwise qualified.

There is a second, subtler point. The salary door ties your residency to continued employment compliance — the visa was granted because you are a highly paid professional in a senior role, and the authorities can ask whether that is still true. The endorsement door carries no such tether. If your career plan involves moving between companies, founding something, or spending a year on research without a corporate salary, the endorsement route's independence is worth more than the salary route's simplicity.

Dubai vs Abu Dhabi: same federal law, different front desks

The Golden Visa is federal — the categories, durations and core requirements come from ICP — but you never apply to "the UAE" in the abstract. You apply through an emirate's front desk, and the two front desks that matter for AI talent behave differently. Dubai processes through the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) and its service centres; Abu Dhabi processes through the TAMM government-services platform and the Abu Dhabi Residents Office, which routes nominations to the competent authority for assessment. The u.ae portal links both channels directly from its Golden visa page, which tells you the government considers them parallel doors, not competing ones.

In practice, the difference shows up in nomination routing. Abu Dhabi's Residents Office states explicitly that applications for the digital-technology talent category need a recommendation from the Emirates Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions or a local competent authority, and that its webapp forwards nominations to the authority automatically. It also notes, candidly, that submissions for some exceptional-talent categories are "currently not available via Abu Dhabi Residents Office" — channel availability moves. Dubai's GDRFA side works the same recommendation logic through its own intake. The strategic point: apply in the emirate where you will actually live and work, confirm on the day that your category is open on that channel, and don't assume a category that is open in one emirate is open in the other.

What 10 years without a sponsor actually buys you

Strip away the marketing and the Golden Visa's value is structural: it decouples your right to live in the UAE from any single employer. The ICP guide states the residency is granted for ten years with automatic renewal and without the need for a sponsor. The official u.ae portal adds the two privileges that make that real: you can stay outside the UAE for more than the usual six-month period without the visa being cancelled — ordinary residence visas die at six months abroad — and you can sponsor family members, including spouses and children, yourself.

The ICP guide goes further on family: residence permits for spouses and children regardless of their age, as well as for parents, plus the ability to sponsor domestic workers under the applicable regulations. And there is a humane detail most coverage skips: if the Golden Residency holder dies, sponsored family members may remain in the UAE for the duration of their own permits. For AI professionals, the practical translation is freedom of movement in the labour market — change employers, take a research sabbatical, consult across companies, or found a startup — without your residency hanging on any single contract. That is the asset. The ten-year number is just how it's packaged.

The renewal math most applicants miss

"Automatic renewal" is the phrase that sells the visa, and the ICP guide does use it. But automatic describes the mechanism, not the test: at renewal, the underlying eligibility still has to hold. On the salary track, that means you are still employed in a senior occupational role, still earning at least AED 30,000 a month, and still carrying comprehensive health insurance for yourself and your family. A senior AI engineer who drops to a mid-level role or takes a pay cut below the threshold between year one and year ten has a renewal problem, not a renewal.

The endorsement track renews on a different logic — your talent didn't expire — but the recommendation that opened the door was a snapshot of your standing at application time, and the authorities can ask for current evidence. The honest way to plan is to treat year ten as a re-verification, not a formality: keep your achievements documented, your insurance continuous, and your employment or professional standing legible to a reviewer who has never met you. And if you are applying from outside the country, remember the entry mechanics the ICP guide confirms: a six-month, multiple-entry visa to come in and complete the procedures, renewable for another six months if the process runs long.

AI founders vs AI employees: which category fits

AI talent doesn't arrive in one shape, and the Golden Visa's categories reward you for picking the one that matches your actual life. If you are an employed AI engineer, research scientist or engineering manager in the UAE earning at least AED 30,000 a month in a senior MOHRE-classified role with a bachelor's degree or better, the skilled-professional track is your shortest path — mechanical, document-driven, and well understood by every service centre in the country.

If you are a founder, the entrepreneur category is the designed fit: the ICP guide grants 10 years to entrepreneurs with established businesses or successful business plans, whether based inside or outside the UAE — with published tests including an SME in an approved field with annual revenues of at least AED 1,000,000, approval of the venture idea from an accredited incubator, the Ministry of Economy or a relevant authority, or a prior venture sold for at least AED 7,000,000. And if you are a researcher or builder whose contribution is recognized in the field — publications, patents, systems at scale — the digital-technology endorsement route through the Emirates Council for AI and Digital Transactions may fit better than either, with no salary figure and no employer required. The mistake is picking the category you wish you fit instead of the one your evidence actually proves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an AI engineer get a UAE Golden Visa without earning AED 30,000 a month?

Yes, through the exceptional-talent route. Talents in the field of digital technology qualify for a 10-year Golden Visa on the basis of a recommendation from the Emirates Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions or a local competent authority — the ICP's published criteria for that route carry no salary figure at all.

Who issues the AI talent endorsement for the UAE Golden Visa?

For digital technology talents, the competent authority is the Emirates Council for Artificial Intelligence and Digital Transactions, or a local competent authority in the emirate where you apply. Abu Dhabi's Residents Office names this explicitly for its Brilliant Talents category.

What is the salary requirement for the Golden Visa professional track?

Skilled professionals in senior occupational levels need a monthly salary of at least AED 30,000 or its equivalent in foreign currency, at least a bachelor's degree or equivalent, a valid UAE work permit under a valid and approved employment contract, and comprehensive health insurance for themselves and their families.

Is the UAE Golden Visa really 10 years?

For exceptional talents and skilled professionals, yes — 10 years with automatic renewal and no sponsor required, per the ICP's Golden Residency guide. Other categories differ: entrepreneurs also get 10 years, while real-estate investors may receive 5 or 10 depending on the route.

Should I apply in Dubai or Abu Dhabi?

The federal framework is identical, but the intake differs: Dubai works through GDRFA channels, Abu Dhabi through the TAMM platform and the Abu Dhabi Residents Office, which forwards nominations to the competent authority. Apply where you will live and work, and check that your category is currently open on that channel before you build your file.

Can I apply for the Golden Visa from outside the UAE?

Yes. The ICP guide confirms that eligible applicants outside the UAE are granted a six-month, multiple-entry entry visa — renewable for a further six months — to enter the country and complete the Golden Residency procedures.

Bottom Line

For AI talent, the UAE Golden Visa is really two different visas wearing one name: an endorsement route through the Emirates Council for AI and Digital Transactions that judges your contribution with no salary gate, and a professional route that grants the same 10 sponsor-free years to anyone employed at AED 30,000 a month or more — so decide which evidence you actually have, pick the emirate whose front desk is open to your category, and build the dossier for that door only.
