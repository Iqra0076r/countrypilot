Visa Fee Changes 2026: US, UK, Schengen, Canada & Australia

Every confirmed 2026 visa fee change in one roundup: US, UK, Schengen, Canada and Australia visitor fees — what moved this year and how to budget your application.

Quick Answer

In 2026, the visitor routes that changed price are the UK's (Standard Visitor visa up from £127 to £135; ETA up from £16 to £20) and Australia's (Visitor 600 now from AUD $250). The US holds at $185 for a B-1/B-2 visa and $40.27 for ESTA, Schengen short-stay remains €90, and Canada holds at CAD $7 for an eTA or CAD $185 total (visa + biometrics) for a visitor visa. The big 2026 outlier is America's $100,000 payment on most new H-1B petitions.

United States: $185 visitor visa, $40.27 ESTA — plus the $100,000 H-1B shock

The headline US visitor routes held their price in 2026: a B-1/B-2 visitor visa costs $185 (machine-readable-visa fee, per the State Department fee schedule) and an ESTA authorization costs $40.27 total ($10.27 application charge plus $30 on approval). The C-1 transit visa is also $185. Apply only at esta.cbp.dhs.gov — copycat sites charge large markups for the same submission.

The real 2026 US story is on the work side: a September 2025 presidential proclamation imposed a $100,000 payment on most new H-1B petitions for workers outside the US, covering the 2026 cycle — on top of standard USCIS fees. It does not apply to petitions filed before the deadline, renewals, or current holders travelling. Separately, I-129 premium processing rose to $2,965 from 1 March 2026. Because legal challenges to the proclamation were reported, verify enforceability at state.gov/h-1b-faq before making plans. Students should note the $185 F-1 visa fee plus the $350 SEVIS I-901 fee still applies in 2026. See our full United States visa & entry requirements guide for the complete fee table and interview wait times.

United Kingdom: Standard Visitor £127 → £135; ETA £16 → £20

The UK repriced its two main short-stay routes: the Standard Visitor visa rose from £127 to £135, and the Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) rose from £16 to £20 — meaning visa-exempt travellers now pay £20 for the ETA before travel. These were part of a wider set of 2026 immigration fee changes, so always confirm the current schedule on gov.uk before paying. Third-party 'UK visa' sites that charge several times the £135/£20 official fees are selling anxiety, not visas. See our full United Kingdom visa & entry requirements guide for routes, validity rules and current schedules.

Schengen Europe: €90 short-stay — no fee change, but EES is now live

The Schengen short-stay visa remains €90 in 2026 for a single entry, with a reduced €45 for applicants aged 6–12 and no fee for children under 6 (fee structure confirmed across our France, Italy and Spain guides). The fee is not the story this year — the border process is: the EES (Entry/Exit System) biometric registration went live at Schengen borders in April 2026, replacing passport stamps with fingerprint and photo capture.

ETIAS is still not in operation as of October 2026 — the EU removed the Q4 2026 launch target in July 2026 and has published no new date — so no ETIAS authorization or fee applies to travellers yet. Budget €90 for the visa itself and verify the ETIAS status before booking, since it will eventually add a new step for visa-exempt nationalities. See our France visa & entry requirements guide and Poland visa & entry requirements guide for Schengen detail and EES/ETIAS status.

Canada: CAD $7 eTA — or CAD $185 all-in for a visitor visa

Canada's headline prices held in 2026. Visa-exempt nationalities flying in need only an eTA at CAD $7. Everyone else applies for a visitor visa (TRV) at CAD $100 plus CAD $85 biometrics — CAD $185 total, with a family cap of CAD $500. Beware the scam pattern: shady sites charging $50–80 to 'process' a CAD $7 eTA. Study permits sit at CAD $150 and work permits at CAD $155. See our full Canada visa & entry requirements guide for routes and processing detail.

Australia: Visitor 600 now from AUD $250

Australia's visitor route is the other confirmed 2026 increase: the Visitor visa (subclass 600) now starts at AUD $250. The cheaper electronic options are unchanged — the ETA (subclass 601) costs AUD $20 via the official ETA app (12 months validity, up to 3 months per visit), and the eVisitor (subclass 651) remains free for eligible nationalities. Check which electronic route your passport qualifies for before paying for a 600, and see our full Australia visa & entry requirements guide for eligibility and conditions.

How to budget for fee changes

  • Price the full per-person cost, not the headline fee. A family of four needs four of everything: four UK visitor visas at £135, four Canadian biometrics at CAD $85 each, four Australian ETAs at AUD $20 each. Canada caps a family's TRV fees at CAD $500 — look for equivalents elsewhere; most countries have none.
  • Pay through official channels only. MRV fees go through the embassy's designated channels, ESTA only at esta.cbp.dhs.gov, UK fees on gov.uk, Canadian fees on canada.ca, Australian fees in the official ETA app or ImmiAccount. Copycat sites sell the same application at a markup — never pay a 'visa fee' to a personal account or crypto wallet.
  • Budget for the wait, not just the fee. US visitor-visa interview waits run 2 months in London to 8.5–10.5 months at Indian posts (July 2026 table) — rebooking costs when plans slip are the hidden expense.
  • Check children's fee bands. Schengen charges €45 instead of €90 for ages 6–12 and nothing under 6; other destinations charge the full fee per child — model this before picking a destination for a family trip.
  • Re-confirm before you pay. Fees change by regulation and exchange rates move. Every figure here is confirmed in CountryPilot's October 2026 guides; the decisive source is always the live official page for your route.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which visa fees changed in 2026?

The confirmed increases are the UK Standard Visitor visa (£127 to £135) and UK ETA (£16 to £20), and Australia's Visitor 600 (now from AUD $250). US visitor routes ($185 B-1/B-2, $40.27 ESTA), the Schengen short-stay visa (€90) and Canadian routes (CAD $7 eTA, CAD $100 + CAD $85 biometrics for a TRV) held their 2026 prices.

Is the US $100,000 H-1B fee real?

Yes — a September 2025 presidential proclamation imposed a $100,000 payment on most new H-1B petitions for workers outside the US, covering the 2026 cycle, on top of standard USCIS fees. It does not affect petitions filed before the deadline, renewals, or current holders travelling. Legal challenges were reported, so verify enforceability at state.gov/h-1b-faq before relying on it.

Is ETIAS in operation yet?

No — ETIAS is not in operation as of October 2026. The EU removed the Q4 2026 launch target in July 2026 and has published no new date. The EES biometric Entry/Exit System, by contrast, went live at Schengen borders in April 2026.

What is the cheapest visitor route among the five destinations?

For visa-exempt nationalities: Canada's eTA at CAD $7 and Australia's free eVisitor 651 are the cheapest; Australia's ETA 601 is AUD $20 and the UK ETA is £20. For everyone else, the base application fees are US $185, Schengen €90, Canada CAD $185 (visa + biometrics), UK £135 and Australia from AUD $250 — before any per-person multiples.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

2026's fee story is two increases — the UK and Australia's visitor routes — against a backdrop of held prices in the US, Schengen and Canada. The number that matters most is America's $100,000 H-1B proclamation payment, the largest visa-related fee change on record. Price every traveller individually, pay only on the official portals, and re-check the live fee schedule before paying: published October 14, 2026.
