Quick Answer

The Japan visa fee for Indian citizens in 2026 is JPY 15,000 for a single-entry visa and JPY 30,000 for multiple-entry — charged only when the visa is issued. Fees rose fivefold on 1 July 2026, the first revision since 1978.

How this Japan visa fee for Indian citizens 2026 guide works

Japan's visa issuance fees are set by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and are the same regardless of nationality. The key detail: the fee is collected when the visa is granted — a refused application costs nothing in government fees. On 1 July 2026 the fees rose fivefold (single-entry to JPY 15,000), the first revision since 1978.

What Indian citizens actually pay

Indian citizens apply via the eVISA route through an accredited agency, or through a consular visa application. The JPY 15,000 single-entry issuance fee is collected when the visa is granted — not upfront with the application — so keep that timing in mind when an agency quotes you an all-in price.

What the quoted fee does not include

The JPY 15,000 is the issuance fee only. It does not include the accredited agency's service charge, document preparation or courier fees. Because the fee is charged on issuance, an agency demanding the 'visa fee' upfront before any decision is worth questioning.

Where and how to apply

Apply through an accredited agency (eVISA route) or at the Japanese mission. Submit documents, wait for the decision, and pay the JPY 15,000 issuance fee when the single-entry visa is granted. Confirm the current accredited-agency list and process on the Ministry of Foreign Affairs visa pages.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

Japan's fee is charged on issuance — anyone demanding the government fee upfront, in cash, or to a personal account is not following the official process. Use accredited agencies only, and get the service charge separated from the JPY 15,000 issuance fee in writing.
