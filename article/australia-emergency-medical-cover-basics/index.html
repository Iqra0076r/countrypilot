Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

Home Affairs currently lists the Student visa (subclass 500) at a cost from AUD 2,500, with stay generally aligned to enrolment for up to six years. Students can generally work up to 48 hours a fortnight while the course is in session, with different treatment for masters-by-research/doctoral students. New Student and Student Guardian application rules took effect on 2 October 2026, including changes affecting who can apply in Australia and family inclusion.
