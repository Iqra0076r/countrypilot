UK ETA Fee 2026: Cost, Who Needs It, and How to Apply

The UK's ETA is not a visa — but since April 2026 it costs more than it used to. Here is the current fee, who needs one, and how it differs from the visitor visa.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20265 min read

The UK's Electronic Travel Authorisation is the document most short-trip travellers from visa-exempt countries now need — and its price changed in the 8 April 2026 fee round. This guide states the current ETA fee, who it applies to, and where the ETA ends and a real visa begins.

Quick Answer

The UK ETA costs £20 as of 8 April 2026 (up from £16). It is required before travel for nationals of visa-exempt countries visiting the UK — it is not a visa, and nationals of countries like India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, and Bangladesh still need a full Standard Visitor visa (£135) instead.

What the ETA is — and is not

The Electronic Travel Authorisation is advance permission to travel, checked by carriers before boarding. It is for short visits by nationals of countries that do not need a UK visa — tourism, family visits, and permitted business. It is not a visa, it does not involve a consulate interview, and it does not replace the visitor visa for anyone whose nationality requires one. Confusing the two is the single most common ETA mistake.

Who needs one

Visa-exempt nationalities travelling to the UK need an ETA before departure — applied for online before travel. If your country requires a UK visa (India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, Bangladesh among them), the ETA is irrelevant to you: your route is the Standard Visitor visa at £135, applied for online with biometrics and a ~3-week service standard. Check your nationality's position on GOV.UK before paying for either.

The £20 fee and the April 2026 change

The fee round of 8 April 2026 raised the ETA from £16 to £20 — a 25% rise that, like the visitor visa's £127→£135 move, invalidated older guides overnight. The fee is paid per application at the time of the online application. As with all UK immigration fees, it is set by regulation and can move again: the live GOV.UK figure is the one to pay against, not a remembered one.

ETA vs Standard Visitor visa

The comparison in one line: the ETA (£20) is advance travel permission for visa-exempt nationals, applied for online with no interview; the Standard Visitor visa (£135) is a full visa for visa-required nationals, applied for online plus a biometrics appointment, decided in about 3 weeks. Different travellers, different documents, different prices — make sure you are pricing the right one. Full detail on the visa route in our United Kingdom visa guide.

Applying correctly

Apply through the official GOV.UK route before travel — the ETA must be in place before you fly, and carriers check it. Third-party sites sell "ETA assistance" at a markup for a process designed to take minutes; the official channel is the whole process. Keep the confirmation accessible for the journey.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the UK ETA in 2026?

£20 per application, since 8 April 2026 (previously £16).

Do Indians or Pakistanis need a UK ETA?

No. Visa-required nationalities need the Standard Visitor visa (£135); the ETA is for visa-exempt nationalities only.

Is the ETA a visa?

No — it is advance permission to travel for short visits, checked before boarding. It involves no interview and no biometrics.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

£20 since April 2026, applied for online before travel, for visa-exempt travellers only: the ETA is simple — the only trap is buying it when what you actually need is the £135 visitor visa.
