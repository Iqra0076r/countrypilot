USA Visa Fee 2026: What Every US Visa Application Costs

Every official US visa fee for 2026 in one place — the $185 visitor, student and transit visa applications, the $40.27 ESTA, the $350 SEVIS fee, and the work-route costs that dwarf them all.

Quick Answer

In 2026, the US visitor (B-1/B-2), student (F-1) and transit (C-1) visa applications each cost $185. ESTA authorization for Visa Waiver Program travellers costs $40.27, and F/M students also pay a $350 SEVIS I-901 fee. The B-1/B-2 visitor visa fee: $185

The B-1/B-2 visitor visa application costs $185 in 2026 — one fee per application, covering the standard tourist and business visitor route.

The standard machine-readable-visa (MRV) fee for a B-1/B-2 visitor visa is $185 as of 2026, per the State Department’s official fee schedule at travel.state.gov. The fee covers the visa application itself. A B-1/B-2 visa is often issued for up to 10 years with multiple entries — but the fee is per application, not per year of validity, and each admission is decided separately by the CBP officer at the port of entry, commonly for up to 6 months and recorded on the electronic I-94.

Travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh all follow this route, since none of those nationalities qualify for the Visa Waiver Program. Pay the MRV fee only through the embassy’s designated payment channels — the State Department warns never to pay a “visa fee” to a personal bank account or crypto wallet. ESTA authorization: $40.27

ESTA costs $40.27 in total for 2026 — a $10.27 application charge plus $30 on approval — and is only available at the official CBP site.

Citizens of the 42 Visa Waiver Program countries — including the United Kingdom — do not need a visa for tourism or business visits of up to 90 days. They need an approved ESTA instead, applied for only at esta.cbp.dhs.gov. The total cost is $40.27 as of 2026: a $10.27 application charge plus $30 on approval.

Apply at least 72 hours before departure, and never board without an approved authorization — carriers check it. The most common money mistake here is paying a copycat site: third-party domains charge large markups for submitting the exact same $40.27 application, so use only the official CBP site.

Student route: $185 plus the $350 SEVIS fee

A US student visa costs $185 for the F-1 application plus a $350 SEVIS I-901 fee, with university health plans adding thousands more per year.

An F-1 student visa application carries the same $185 MRV fee. On top of that, F and M students must pay the $350 SEVIS I-901 fee (2026, per ice.gov) after receiving Form I-20 from a SEVP-certified school and before the visa interview.

Budget beyond the application too: most universities auto-enroll international students in a health plan costing roughly $2,400–$3,900 per year (2026–27 published figures from American University, Stony Brook and the University of Oregon). The US sets no federal dollar minimum for proof of funds, so the school’s published cost of attendance — the figure on the I-20 — is what counts. See the United States student visa guide for the full sequence.

Transit: the $185 C-1 visa

Transiting the US is not free: non-VWP travellers need a C-1 transit visa at the $185 MRV fee, even for airside-only connections.

The United States has no sterile international transit: every traveller who changes planes on US soil clears immigration, even without leaving the airport. Travellers from Visa Waiver Program countries may transit on an approved ESTA ($40.27). Everyone else needs a C-1 transit visa at the same $185 MRV fee as of 2026 — even for an airside-only connection. Treat a US transit as a full entry in your budget, not a free layover.

Work routes: the $100,000 H-1B proclamation payment

Work routes carry the largest US visa costs in 2026, including a $100,000 proclamation payment on most new H-1B petitions and $2,965 premium processing.

A September 2025 presidential proclamation imposed a $100,000 payment on most new H-1B petitions for workers outside the United States, covering the 2026 cycle — on top of standard USCIS fees. Because legal challenges were reported, verify current enforceability at state.gov/h-1b-faq before making plans or payments.

Where timing is critical, I-129 premium processing costs $2,965 for a 15-business-day decision (from 1 March 2026). And remember the category boundary: a B-1/B-2 visa never authorizes employment — not even remote work for a foreign employer while physically in the US. Reciprocity: the extra issuance fee some nationalities owe

Some nationalities owe an extra reciprocity issuance fee after approval — check before arranging passport return.

After a visa is approved, some nationalities owe an additional visa issuance (reciprocity) fee before the passport is returned. Whether this applies depends on your nationality — check the State Department’s reciprocity schedule for your country before arranging passport return, so the final total holds no surprises.

Fee scams to avoid

Only esta.cbp.dhs.gov charges the real $40.27 ESTA fee — copycat sites mark it up, and no one can sell approvals or interview slots.

The only official ESTA site is esta.cbp.dhs.gov. Domains combining “esta” with words like “apply-online” or “visa-service” are private businesses marking up a $40.27 application. No one can guarantee visa approval or sell an earlier interview slot — anyone promising either is running a scam. MRV fees are paid only through the embassy’s designated channels; never to a personal bank account or crypto wallet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a US tourist visa in 2026?

The B-1/B-2 visitor visa application fee (MRV) is $185 as of 2026, per the State Department’s fee schedule. Some nationalities may also owe a reciprocity issuance fee after approval.

How much does ESTA cost?

$40.27 total: a $10.27 application charge plus $30 on approval (2026). Apply only at esta.cbp.dhs.gov, at least 72 hours before departure.

What does a US student visa cost in total?

The F-1 visa application is $185, plus the $350 SEVIS I-901 fee for F/M students. Universities also typically require a health plan of roughly $2,400–$3,900 per year.

Is there a fee to transit through the US?

VWP nationals transit on an approved ESTA ($40.27). Everyone else needs a C-1 transit visa at the $185 MRV fee, even for airside-only connections.

What is the $100,000 H-1B fee?

A September 2025 presidential proclamation imposed a $100,000 payment on most new H-1B petitions for workers outside the US (2026 cycle), on top of standard USCIS fees. Legal challenges were reported — verify enforceability at state.gov/h-1b-faq.
