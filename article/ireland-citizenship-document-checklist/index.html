This guide distinguishes temporary status, permanent residence and citizenship, then shows which records are worth preserving before the final application becomes urgent.

Map the path from temporary status to long-term status

In Ireland, long-term residence or other residence pathways depending on history/status is not simply a longer version of a temporary visa. The qualifying route may depend on years of lawful residence, type of permission, absences, employment, language/integration, taxes or other conditions. Some temporary categories count differently from others.

Build a residence timeline now, not at the end. Record every entry/exit, permit type, address and major status change.
