Schengen visa fee for Indian citizens 2026

Schengen visa fee for Indian citizens in 2026: the €90 EU-wide fee, child discounts, and the 15-day processing standard — identical in every Schengen state.

Quick Answer

The Schengen visa fee for Indian citizens in 2026 is €90 for adults, €45 for children aged 6–11, and free for under-6s — set at EU level and identical in every Schengen state. Standard processing is about 15 days, and travel medical insurance of at least €30,000 is mandatory.

How this Schengen visa fee for Indian citizens 2026 guide works

The Schengen Type C visa fee is harmonised across all member states by EU regulation — the same €90 whether you apply through France, Italy, Germany or any other Schengen country. You apply at the mission or VFS centre of your main destination (the country where you will spend the most days). The fee covers processing of the application only.

What Indian citizens actually pay

Indian citizens apply at the consulate or VFS centre of their main Schengen destination in India. Alongside the €90 fee, the application must include travel medical insurance of at least €30,000 covering the entire Schengen area for the whole trip — policies are checked, not just asked about. Biometrics are taken at submission (stored in the Visa Information System), and standard processing is about 15 calendar days.

What the quoted fee does not include

The €90 covers the application only. It does not include the VFS or application-centre service fee, passport courier charges, the mandatory €30,000 travel medical insurance, or document translations. Children aged 6–11 pay €45 and under-6s pay nothing — the reduced fees apply per child.

Where and how to apply

Identify your main destination, book through its mission or VFS centre, submit biometrics, documents and the €30,000 insurance, and pay the €90 fee at submission. You can apply up to 6 months before travel; standard processing is about 15 calendar days. The visa allows up to 90 days' stay in any 180-day period across the whole Schengen area.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

Official fees are paid at the mission or VFS counter with a receipt — never to a personal account or a social-media 'agent'. Because the fee is identical everywhere, anyone offering a 'discounted Schengen fee' or a guaranteed visa for an extra payment is running a scam.
