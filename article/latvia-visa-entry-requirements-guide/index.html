How to use this Latvia visa and entry guide

Latvia is Schengen-standard for short stays (€90 C visa, no e-Visa or VOA) and PMLP-run for long stays — with some of the most specific student economics in the batch. The student residence-permit examination fee is speed-tiered: €160 / €280 / €560 by processing speed, and the official proof-of-funds figure is €780/month from 1 April 2026.

The 2026 legal change: Immigration Law amendments in force — including an A2 Latvian language requirement at the permanent-residence stage and tightened employer obligations. Students at accredited institutions are exempt from the separate invitation-approval step. This guide was verified in October 2026.

Passport validity and document condition

Standard Schengen rule for C visas: passport issued within the last 10 years, valid 3+ months beyond departure. For residence permits, longer validity is prudent — the permit process rewards documents that outlast the first permit year.

Identify the correct visa or entry route

EU-harmonised rules for short stays: US and UK nationals visa-free 90/180; India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh need the €90 Schengen C visa. Long stays go through the PMLP regardless of nationality.

Transit and onward travel

Riga connections follow Schengen transit logic: airside transit without entering Schengen needs no visa for most, with airport-transit-visa requirements for listed nationalities. Any landside step is an entry needing the C visa or visa-free status.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Whatever Latvia's official rules say on paper, the officer at the desk decides your admission. Carry a return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, and evidence you can fund the trip — bank statements or a card in your own name, not someone else's screenshots. Keep the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) approval or visa printout accessible offline; airport Wi-Fi is not a document strategy. Visiting friends or family? Their full address and a contact number answer the two most common questions before they're asked.

Answer questions directly and consistently with your application. If anything changed since you applied — new passport, different flight, longer stay — say so upfront rather than letting the officer discover the mismatch.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Latvia requires no vaccination certificate for ordinary visitors. No health document is checked at Latvian border control for short stays.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

The Schengen C application requires €30,000-minimum travel medical insurance. For students, the insurance must cover the full residence-permit period — and note the speed-tiered examination fee (€160/280/560) is separate from any insurance cost. No official annual student-insurance figure was verified.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

Schengen admission: 90 days per 180 across all member states. Latvian border officers may ask for the return ticket, accommodation, insurance and funds. Keep documents accessible.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Study: the examination fee is €160 / €280 / €560 by processing speed, with official proof of funds at €780/month from 1 April 2026 — one of the clearest student figures in the batch. Students at accredited institutions skip the separate invitation-approval step. The 2026 Immigration Law amendments add an A2 Latvian requirement at permanent residence — plan language learning early if PR is the goal. Work: employer-sponsored residence permits with tightened employer obligations under the 2026 amendments. A visitor visa never authorises work.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every child needs their own passport and their own Latvia permission — there is no family visa or shared authorization. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter from the non-travelling parent(s); it is not always a statutory requirement, but officers routinely ask, and a missing letter can delay or derail entry.

Dual nationals should enter and leave on the same passport, and check whether Latvia restricts dual nationality — some countries treat entry on a foreign passport as a declaration with consequences. Travellers on refugee travel documents or laissez-passers face different rules everywhere: verify with the mission, not with forums.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

Schengen C route: consulate/centre appointment, €90 fee, €30,000-minimum insurance. Student route: 1) Secure admission at an accredited institution (invitation-approval step waived). 2) Show €780/month funds (from 1 Apr 2026). 3) Pay the speed-tiered fee (€160/280/560). 4) Carry insurance for the full permit period. 5) Note the A2 Latvian requirement at PR stage (2026 amendments). Work route: employer-led under the tightened 2026 obligations.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Paying the wrong speed tier. €160/280/560 depends on processing speed — choose deliberately.
  • Budgeting old funds figures. €780/month is the official figure from 1 April 2026.
  • Ignoring the A2 requirement. The 2026 amendments add Latvian at PR stage — start learning early.
  • Arranging a separate invitation approval. Waived for accredited-institution students.
  • Counting 90 days per visit. The 90/180 rule spans all Schengen states.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Caseworkers and officers are testing two things: will you leave, and can you afford the trip. The strongest files show stable, genuine funds over several months — a single large unexplained deposit reads as funds parking and hurts more than it helps. Tie the itinerary to the money: the trip you're describing should be one your documented income plausibly supports.

Show a reason to return — employment with approved leave, enrolment, family, property — and keep the story consistent across every document. If you've been refused before, address the refusal reasons directly with new evidence; resubmitting the same file invites the same answer.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Latvia has no e-Visa — the PMLP website (pmlp.gov.lv) publishes requirements, and applications go through missions or the PMLP. Schengen C visas remain consular/in-centre with in-person biometrics. 'Latvia e-Visa' sellers are not official.

Translations, certifications and document format

Any document not in Latvia's accepted language(s) needs a proper translation — check the mission or the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) for whether certified translation is required, and by whom. Bank statements should be official downloads or stamped originals, not screenshots; employment letters should be on letterhead with a verifiable contact.

Submit scans well before any appointment so there's time to fix rejections. Keep originals for the appointment itself, and keep a full copy of everything you submitted — if the file is queried months later, you'll need to know exactly what the officer saw.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Where Latvia requires in-person biometrics — fingerprints and a digital photograph — book the appointment early; centres fill up in peak seasons and the decision clock usually starts at the appointment, not the application. The photograph is captured there, so check whether a separate photo upload is even needed before paying for passport photos.

Interviews are the exception rather than the rule for short stays, but they happen where credibility needs testing. Answer from your actual plans, not from what you think the officer wants to hear — memorised scripts are easy to spot and hard to recover from.

Families and group travel

Each traveller — including every child — needs their own permission; there is no family authorization that covers a group. Apply in the same window so decisions land together, but understand each application is decided on its own merits: one refusal doesn't cancel the others, though it should prompt the rest of the group to re-check their own files.

For organised groups, keep a shared folder with every member's approval, the full itinerary, and the group leader's contact details. If one member is pulled aside at the border, the group should know where the documents are — not just that they exist somewhere in someone's email.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Cash of €10,000+ entering or leaving must be declared. Standard EU duty-free allowances apply. Keep the visa, insurance and return ticket with your passport.

Departure and future travel

Schengen C visas are not extendable in-country except in narrow cases. Residence holders extend via the PMLP before expiry — and the 2026 amendments' A2 Latvian requirement now sits at the PR stage, not the extension stage.

How to research a route without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) pmlp.gov.lv — the PMLP's own fee, funds and amendment pages. The three 2026 facts that matter: €780/month (1 Apr 2026), €160/280/560 speed tiers, and the A2-at-PR amendment.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid per Latvia's rule, with blank pages, matching the application exactly
  • Visa / e-Visa / authorization approval saved offline and printed — verify it's linked to the current passport
  • Return or onward ticket and accommodation details accessible without internet
  • Proof of funds and ties-to-home evidence packed
  • Travel insurance documents with emergency numbers
  • Any required arrival forms (health, customs, digital entry cards) completed in advance
  • Certified translations for documents not in the accepted language
  • Emergency contacts: your embassy, your insurer, and someone at home who has your itinerary
  • Previous refusals or complications declared honestly wherever asked

Bottom Line
