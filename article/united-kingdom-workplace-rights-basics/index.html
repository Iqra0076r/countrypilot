Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

For the Skilled Worker route, GOV.UK currently states that the standard application fee ranges from £819 to £1,865 depending on circumstances. The immigration health surcharge is usually £1,035 per year, and applicants who must show maintenance normally need at least £1,270 held under the financial-evidence rules. Job eligibility and salary are separate checks: the applicable going rate and route salary rules must both be checked for the occupation.

Separate the job search from immigration eligibility

Search for jobs and immigration eligibility in parallel. Employer sponsorship is formal: applicants should verify the sponsor and the exact occupation/salary rules on GOV.UK. Create a one-page route summary before applying: route name, sponsor requirement, occupation rule, salary/skill requirement, language rule, professional registration and whether dependants can accompany.

This prevents a common failure: investing months in a job that cannot support the intended immigration route. How to verify an employer or sponsor

Use official sponsor/accreditation registers where they exist, the company's own careers site, business registry details and a professional email domain. Compare the legal employer name on the offer with the entity in the sponsor register. A recruitment agency may be legitimate, but it should be clear who the actual employer is. Red flags include payment for a job offer, requests to reimburse prohibited sponsor costs, interviews conducted only through anonymous messaging apps, contracts with a different company name, and salary promises that are inconsistent with the written offer. Build an overseas-ready application

A useful international CV is evidence-led: role, employer, dates, quantified responsibilities, tools/technologies, regulated credentials and language ability. In the cover letter, answer the employer's risk questions directly: Are you eligible for the occupation? Do you need sponsorship? Are your qualifications recognised? When can you relocate? If the employer uses a formal sponsorship programme, say which route you believe applies without pretending to give legal advice. Offer-stage checks

Before resigning or paying relocation costs, verify salary, hours, probation, location, remote-work expectations, relocation support, visa costs, health insurance and family benefits. Check whether the contract is conditional on immigration approval. For readers using United Kingdom Workplace Rights Basics, this is a “Offer-stage checks” issue rather than a stand-alone rule; verify it against UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office before committing money or documents.

A genuine offer can still be unsuitable if the salary fails the immigration threshold, the job title does not match the actual duties, or the employer is not eligible to sponsor. From job offer to lawful work

The employer and applicant may have separate steps. In some systems the employer first obtains accreditation, sponsorship approval, labour-market approval or a job check; only then can the worker apply. In others, the processes are linked. Use https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa to map the actual sequence.

Do not start work until the status authorises it. Entry as a visitor or student does not automatically permit full-time employment.
