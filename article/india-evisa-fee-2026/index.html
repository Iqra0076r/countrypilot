What an India eVisa costs in 2026: official e-Tourist fees by duration and season, nationality exceptions, transaction charges, and how to pay without overpaying on lookalike sites.

2026 eVisa fees at a glance

India's e-Tourist visa fee in 2026 ranges from US$10 to US$80, set by the duration you apply for and the season of travel. The India visa and entry requirements guide covers the full entry system; this page focuses on what you pay and how to avoid paying more than the official rate. All figures below are portal-standard amounts from the official e-Visa system at indianvisaonline.gov.in, and a 3-4% transaction charge is added at payment. Fees and rules change - confirm the live figure on the official portal before you pay.

India e-Tourist fees, 2026 (portal-standard)
DurationFeeNotes
30 days (April-June)US$10Low-season rate
30 days (July-March)US$25Standard-season rate
1 yearUS$40Multiple entry within validity
5 yearsUS$80Multiple entry; nationality exceptions apply

The fee you pay is determined by duration and season, not by how long you actually stay - a one-year e-Visa costs the same whether you use it once or ten times. Per-visit stay limits still apply within the granted validity, so read the conditions on your approval rather than assuming the headline duration equals continuous stay.

Nationality exceptions: US, UK and fee-free passports

There is no single "India visa fee". Around 166 nationalities can use the e-Visa, but several passports are charged at their own rates, and roughly 22 nationalities are free of charge. The two big exceptions to know:

  • United States - e-Visa at the US$160 nationality exception rate.
  • United Kingdom - the 5-year e-Tourist visa costs US$484, a sharp premium over the standard US$80.
  • Japan and Sri Lanka - a flat US$25 regardless of duration.

If your passport is not US or UK, check the portal's eligibility and fee tool for your exact nationality before budgeting - assuming the headline US$10-80 range applies to everyone is one of the most common reasons travellers are surprised at checkout.

Paying the fee and transaction charges

Payment happens on the official portal during the e-Visa application, and the 3-4% transaction charge is added to every fee - so the amount on your card statement will be slightly higher than the table above. Budget this in rather than discovering it at checkout. Apply a minimum of 4 days before arrival: standard processing runs about 72 hours, but that is a typical timeframe, not an official guarantee, so last-minute applications carry real risk. Use a card in your own name where possible and keep the payment confirmation alongside your e-Visa approval.

Avoid lookalike sites and inflated fees

indianvisaonline.gov.in is the only official online channel for the e-Visa - e-Visa applications and the E-Arrival Card both live there. The lookalike sites on .org/.com domains are agencies that charge multiples of the government fee for filing the same form. Type the domain yourself rather than clicking ads, and verify the address bar reads exactly indianvisaonline.gov.in before entering passport details or card information. If you have already paid an inflated agency fee, you have not paid the Indian government anything extra - the visa itself still costs the official amount.

Other 2026 entry costs to budget

Travellers who cannot use the e-Visa - nationalities outside the ~166 eligible, or anyone needing a long-stay category - pay the regular consular visa fee schedule of the mission they apply through, plus any VFS service fee. As an example, the Brussels mission schedule effective 1 April 2026 charges €110 / €183 / €274 for Employment (E) visas (up to 6 months / 1 year / 5 years) and €73 for Student (S) visas, each plus a €3 ICWF surcharge - but fees vary by mission and nationality, so confirm with your own post. Remember too that the e-Visa is valid only at designated airports and seaports, not at land borders, and every arriving passenger must complete the mandatory E-Arrival Card from 1 April 2026 - a registration step, separate from the visa fee.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

India's e-Visa fee in 2026 is US$10-80 by duration and season, plus a 3-4% transaction charge - unless your passport is a nationality exception like the US (US$160) or the UK (US$484 for 5 years). Pay only at indianvisaonline.gov.in, apply at least 4 days before arrival, and confirm the live fee on the portal before paying.
