Schengen Visa for Pakistani Citizens 2026: Requirements, Fee, and Timeline

Pakistani passport holders need a Schengen visa for every short trip to Europe. This is the complete 2026 picture: fee, documents, funds proof, and the timeline to plan around.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20268 min read

Pakistani citizens need a Schengen visa for every short stay in the Schengen area — tourism, business, conferences, family visits — issued before travel, with no visa-free or on-arrival route. The rules are the same EU-wide rules that bind every applicant, but scrutiny lands hardest on three parts of a Pakistani file: lawful funds, home ties, and document reliability. This guide covers the 2026 requirements end to end.

Quick Answer

Pakistani citizens need a Schengen short-stay visa for any short visit. The fee is €90 (children 6–12: €45), the decision takes 15 days normally and up to 45 days, and you apply at the consulate of your main destination (most nights) — or the first country entered if nights tie. You may apply from 6 months before travel, no later than 15 days before.

Fee €90 · Decision 15–45 days · Stay up to 90 days in 180 · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 8 minutes

The document list for Pakistani applicants

The Commission's baseline: application form, photo, passport (issued within the last 10 years, valid at least 3 months after you leave), travel medical insurance of at least €30,000 covering all Schengen states for the entire stay, plus documents on purpose, means, accommodation, and intention to return. Every document must be genuine and internally consistent — contradictions between the form, bookings, and employment evidence are a standalone refusal ground, and anything forged follows you in the shared Visa Information System. Run the list through our visa document checklist before the appointment.

Funds: lawful, demonstrably yours, built over time

"Insufficient means" and money that cannot be shown to be lawfully available are two separate refusal grounds, and Pakistani files are judged closely on both. Each member state sets daily reference amounts — France works on €65 per day where prepaid accommodation is proven — and the consulate measures your balance against your real trip cost. What convinces: bank statements showing regular income over several months. What damages: a large, sudden, unexplained deposit that reads as money parked for the application. If someone else funds the trip, declare it with their statements and a signed sponsorship letter. Total your true cost first with our Schengen visa cost calculator.

Home ties: the most common refusal tick

Doubt that you will leave before the visa expires is the most frequent refusal ground overall — it is a judgment about ties at home, not character. A strong Pakistani file documents a life that pulls you back: an employer's leave letter with a return date, property or a lease, family in Pakistan, ongoing business or study commitments, and a record of leaving other countries on time. Salaried applicants should bring employment letters and salary slips; business owners should bring registration and tax records.

Which consulate to apply at

Jurisdiction follows your main destination — the Schengen state where you spend the most nights — not whichever consulate has free slots. Applying at the wrong consulate is an established refusal ground. Count the nights, let the count choose the consulate, and if two countries tie, apply at the one you enter first.

Timeline and fee

Fee: €90 per adult, €45 for children 6–12, non-refundable. Decision: 15 days standard, up to 45 days where further scrutiny — including consultation with other member states — is needed. Lodging window: 6 months to 15 days before travel. Lodge early: the extended window exists precisely for files that need a second look. Details in our fee guide and processing-time guide.

If you are refused

A refusal arrives on the standard EU form with the grounds ticked — most often funds, ties, or document reliability. It is not a ban: you may appeal under the refusing country's law or reapply at any time, declaring the refusal (the consulate sees it anyway). Do not reapply unchanged — fix each ticked ground first. Our refusal guide walks through all 12 grounds and their fixes.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Pakistanis get a Schengen visa on arrival?

No. Pakistani citizens must hold the visa before travel; there is no on-arrival or e-visa route for the Schengen area.

How much bank balance do Pakistanis need?

No fixed figure exists. States set daily reference amounts (France: €65/day with prepaid accommodation), judged against your real trip cost and months of genuine statements.

How long is a Schengen visa valid for Pakistanis?

The short-stay visa allows up to 90 days in any 180-day period; the exact validity and entries granted are decided per case.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Pakistani applicants the Schengen visa is an evidence exercise: €90 per application, months of genuine bank statements, documented ties pulling you home, €30,000 of insurance, one consistent story across every page — lodged early, at the right consulate.
