New Zealand Visa Fee 2026

Every official 2026 fee for entering New Zealand — NZeTA, Visitor Visa, work and student visas — with the $100 IVL, fee bands, processing times and how to pay safely.

Quick Answer

In 2026, entering New Zealand costs NZD $17–23 plus the $100 IVL for the NZeTA, $441 total for a Visitor Visa, $1,540 for the Accredited Employer Work Visa, and $750 plus the $100 IVL for a student visa. Confirm every figure on Immigration New Zealand's official fees tool before paying.

2026 fee overview

Immigration New Zealand (INZ) sets all visa and entry fees, and publishes them with a fees, decision times and where to apply tool at immigration.govt.nz. That tool is the live source — the figures below are representative of 2026, verified October 2026, and should be re-checked before you pay anything.

Nearly every fee sits on top of the $100 International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy (IVL), charged per traveller — including infants. Budget it as a line item on every route.

NZeTA cost

The New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority is mandatory for travellers from around 60 visa-waiver nationalities — including the US and UK — before departure. It costs NZD $17 through the official app or $23 through the website, plus the $100 IVL, and is valid for 2 years. That makes the real total $117 (app) or $123 (web) per traveller.

Apply well before departure — INZ advises allowing up to 72 hours, though approvals are usually faster. See our dedicated New Zealand NZeTA 2026 guide for who needs it, how to apply and the transit rules.

Visitor Visa cost

Travellers from non-waiver nationalities — including India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh — apply for a Visitor Visa online at immigration.govt.nz. For most applicants (Band C, applying outside New Zealand), the total is NZD $441: $300 application fee + $41 immigration levy + $100 IVL.

Two extras sit outside that figure: the VAC (visa application centre) service fee, which increased on 1 January 2026, and a temporary 12-month reduction to NZD $161 for eligible Pacific nationals from 1 June 2026. There is no visa on arrival — the visa must be approved before travel.

Work visa cost

The main work route is the Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV), and the worker's application costs NZD $1,540 — raised from $750 in October 2024 and unchanged since. Many older guides still quote $750; they are wrong.

The employer pays separately before the worker can apply: $775 for employer accreditation and $735 for the job check.

From 9 March 2026, the immigration median wage rose to NZD $35.00/hour, which still anchors Green List pay rates and family support thresholds — even though the general AEWV median-wage requirement was removed in March 2025.

Read our New Zealand skilled worker pathway guide for how the sequence works.

Student visa cost

The Fee Paying Student Visa costs NZD $750 plus the $100 IVL (from October 2024, current through 2026). But the application fee is the smallest line item: applicants must also evidence NZD $20,000 per year in living costs for tertiary courses of a year or more ($1,667 per month for shorter courses), with tuition and outward travel as separate additional requirements.

Comprehensive medical and travel insurance is a mandatory visa condition — typically NZD $500–900 per year (2026), not a recommendation. See our New Zealand tuition and budgeting guide and the travel insurance and health guide for the full cost picture.

The $100 IVL explained

The International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy (IVL) is a NZD $100 charge applied per traveller — adults and children alike — on the NZeTA and most visa applications. It funds conservation and tourism infrastructure, and it is separate from the application fee itself.

Because it applies per person, a family of four pays $400 in IVL alone on top of their application fees. Any quote that omits the IVL is understating the true cost.

Processing times

INZ's official wait-times tool (2026) gives these representative figures — not guarantees: Visitor Visas average ~1 week, with most completed within 2; the AEWV averages 1.5–3 weeks, most within 3–8.5; student visas run around 20 working days online baseline, 4–8 weeks typical in practice.

The tool updates weekly, so treat these as planning figures and check the live numbers for your category before committing to a departure date. Fees do not buy faster processing.

2026 fee changes

  • 1 January 2026: VAC service fees increased for visa applications lodged through visa application centres.
  • From 9 March 2026: immigration median wage rose to NZD $35.00/hour (from $33.56) — relevant to work-visa thresholds, not a fee, but it shifts cost planning.
  • From 1 June 2026: temporary 12-month reduction of the Visitor Visa fee to NZD $161 for eligible Pacific nationals.
  • Still current: the October 2024 increases — AEWV $1,540 and student visa $750 — remain the 2026 figures.

Paying safely and avoiding scams

Apply and pay only through INZ's official channels at immigration.govt.nz. The NZeTA costs $17/$23 plus the $100 IVL — copycat sites charge multiples of that for submitting the same form.

No agent can influence INZ processing times or sell an earlier decision; anyone promising either is running a scam. Never pay a 'visa fee' outside INZ's official payment flow, and ignore any guide still quoting the old $750 AEWV fee or omitting the IVL — both errors have been wrong since October 2024.

Fee checklist before you apply

  • Check INZ's fees/decision-times tool for your exact visa — it overrides every static figure
  • Budget the $100 IVL per traveller (including infants) on every route
  • Visitor Visa: NZD $441 total ($161 Pacific reduction from 1 Jun 2026), plus VAC service fee
  • AEWV: worker $1,540; employer-side $775 accreditation + $735 job check complete first
  • Student visa: $750 + $100 IVL, plus $20,000/yr funds and $500–900/yr mandatory insurance
  • Confirm the VAC service fee for your lodgement location (increased 1 Jan 2026)
  • Pay only through immigration.govt.nz — copycat NZeTA sites multiply the real price
