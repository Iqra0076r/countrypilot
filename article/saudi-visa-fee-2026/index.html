How to use this Saudi visa fee guide

Passport validity and document condition

Most destinations require a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your stay, with a blank page — airlines enforce this at boarding. Confirm the exact rule on the official portal or via Timatic; emergency passports may face extra scrutiny.

Which visa route each fee applies to

The standard 2026 fee stack covers the visitor routes: the tourist eVisa (about 66 nationalities via ksavisa.sa), visa on arrival for eligible nationalities, and the transit/stopover visa. The Package Visa pilot is priced separately at SAR 402.21 including insurance, bundled with an approved-agency package — it replaces the USD fee stack for pilot travellers.

Transit and onward travel

Transit is a separate immigration question from visiting. Whether you need a transit visa depends on nationality, airport, and whether you leave the transit area. Check your exact itinerary on the official portal or via Timatic before booking — airlines deny boarding without the right permission.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

A visa lets you travel to the border; the officer decides entry. Carry your passport with the visa, a return or onward ticket, your accommodation address, proof of funds, and ties-to-home evidence such as an employment letter. Answer directly and consistently.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Health entry rules usually follow where you have recently been — yellow-fever certificates are the classic example for arrivals from endemic countries. Check current requirements on the official portal close to departure.

Insurance as part of the fee

Insurance appears twice in the fee stack: a $10.50 insurance digital services line item plus the policy itself, which is provider-based (no fixed 2026 figure is published — collect quotes from two or three insurers). Travelling without medical cover is a serious financial risk.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

The officer sets your permitted stay at entry — visa validity and admitted stay are different things. Check the admission record immediately. Overstaying can void the visa, trigger fines, and complicate future applications.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work needs an employer-sponsored permit — never work on a visitor entry, even remotely for a foreign employer. Study needs a student visa and enrollment; no student-visa fee could be verified for 2026 — confirm it on the portal.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Each child needs their own passport, application, and fee — there is no family visa or family e-Visa. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter plus the birth certificate. Dual nationals should check which passport to use at entry.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

1) Confirm your route — eVisa, VOA, or Package Visa pilot. 2) Apply only at https://ksavisa.sa/. 3) Budget the verified fee (USD $80 visa fee + $10.50 digital visa services + $10.50 insurance digital services + insurance (provider-based) (2026), or SAR 402.21 for the pilot) and pay through official channels. 4) Allow the full processing window (1 minute to 3 business days for the eVisa). 5) Gather documents, originals plus copies. 6) Verify visa details after approval.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Trusting third-party ‘official’ visa sites that add markups or harvest data.
  • Paying a ‘fee’ to a personal bank account or crypto wallet — official fees go through the portal only.
  • Reading visa validity as permitted stay — each admission is decided separately.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the visa is issued.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Most refusals come down to two doubts: will the applicant return home, and can they fund the trip? Answer both with a leave-approval letter, property or lease documents, family ties, prior compliant travel, and bank statements. Declare previous refusals honestly.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Start every step at https://ksavisa.sa/ — phishing sites copy government portals closely. No one can guarantee approval or sell earlier appointments. Never pay a ‘visa fee’ to a personal account or crypto wallet; track your case on the official system.

Translations, certifications and document format

Bring originals plus one copy: passport, specification photos, 3–6 months of bank statements, an employment letter with leave dates, and the itinerary. Non-English documents need a translation with a signed accuracy statement.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Missions may interview applicants and capture fingerprints and a digital photograph. Answer questions on the trip briefly and truthfully, matching the form.

Families and group travel

Every traveller needs their own authorization and pays their own fee — including infants. There is no family visa or e-Visa. A refusal for one member does not affect the others — address the refusal reason first.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Cash above a threshold (commonly USD 10,000 equivalent) must be declared — it is not taxed, but undeclared cash can be seized. Declare all food, plants and animal products. Carry medications in original packaging with prescriptions.

Departure and future travel

Keep every entry record — clean histories strengthen future applications. Seek extensions before the permitted stay expires. Report lost visas to the issuing mission promptly.

How to research a fee without getting misled

Hierarchy: 1) the official portal (https://ksavisa.sa/) — it overrides everything. 2) The official tourism fee-schedule page. 3) The embassy site for local procedures. Treat forums and agent blogs as leads, not answers.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid well beyond the stay, with a blank page
  • Visa/e-Visa approved and verified before departure
  • Fee receipt saved — paid through the official channel only
  • Return ticket and accommodation address available offline
  • Funds and ties-to-home evidence packed
  • Insurance policy with adequate medical coverage

What is the Package Visa pilot fee?

From 6 Jul 2026, travellers from Jordan, Egypt, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Mexico and Pakistan can use the Package Visa pilot: SAR 402.21 for the visa including insurance, bundled with an approved-agency package (minimum SAR 4,000/adult for the first 2 days).

Do children pay the visa fee?

Are visa fees refundable if my application is refused?

No official refund policy could be verified for 2026. Assume the fee pays for processing, not approval, and confirm the current policy on the official portal.

How long does Saudi visa processing take?
