Thailand Tourist Visa Requirements 2026: Documents, Fees, and Process

Thailand's tourist visa is a THB 1,000 advance visa for a 60-day stay — applied for online before you fly. Here are the 2026 requirements, documents, and the alternatives.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20267 min read

Thailand's tourist visa is an advance visa: applied for online before travel, issued before departure, good for a 60-day stay. Since the 27 April 2026 fee revision it costs THB 1,000 single-entry. This guide covers the requirements, the documents, the application channel, and the two alternatives travellers compare it against.

Quick Answer

The single-entry tourist visa costs THB 1,000 (since 27 April 2026) and allows a 60-day stay; the multiple-entry variant costs THB 5,000. Applications go through thaievisa.go.th, the official e-visa channel, before travel. Alternatives: visa on arrival (THB 2,000, 15 days, eligible nationalities at designated checkpoints) and the DTV (THB 10,000, 5-year multiple-entry, 180 days per entry).

Tourist single THB 1,000 · Stay 60 days · Channel thaievisa.go.th · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 7 minutes

The requirements

The tourist-visa file follows the standard pattern: a passport valid for the trip, a photo, proof of travel (return or onward ticket), accommodation details, and evidence of funds sufficient for the stay. The application is lodged online at thaievisa.go.th before departure — the approval exists before you fly, which is the point of the category. Apply with margin; the visa must be in hand before the travel date.

The April 2026 fee revision

The revised consular fee structure effective 27 April 2026 set the current prices: single-entry tourist THB 1,000, multiple-entry tourist THB 5,000, and Non-ED / Non-B single-entry THB 2,000. Any guide quoting the pre-April table is out of date. The live thaievisa site — not a remembered figure — is the price to pay.

Single vs multiple entry

The single-entry (THB 1,000) covers one 60-day stay — the standard holiday. The multiple-entry (THB 5,000) suits travellers entering Thailand repeatedly within its validity. Choose by itinerary: the multiple-entry is five times the price, so it only pays when the travel pattern actually uses it.

The alternatives: VoA and DTV

Visa on arrival: THB 2,000 for a 15-day stay at designated checkpoints, for eligible nationalities — the simplest door for a short trip, but shorter and less flexible than the advance visa. Destination Thailand Visa (DTV): base fee THB 10,000, 5-year multiple-entry, 180 days per entry, with ~THB 500,000 in proven funds — the long-stay remote-work product, priced for years of access rather than one trip.

Applying correctly

Use thaievisa.go.th — the official channel — and pay the government's published price. Third-party "Thai visa" sites add markups to fixed fees. Keep the e-visa approval accessible for the journey; airlines check it before boarding. And run the documents through our visa document checklist before submitting.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Thailand tourist visa requirements in 2026?

Passport, photo, return/onward ticket, accommodation details, and funds evidence — applied for online at thaievisa.go.th before travel, THB 1,000 single-entry for a 60-day stay.

How long can I stay on a Thailand tourist visa?

60 days on the single-entry tourist visa.

Is visa on arrival better than the tourist visa?

For short eligible-nationality trips (15 days, THB 2,000), the VoA is simpler; for longer stays, the advance tourist visa is the correct product.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

THB 1,000 online before you fly for 60 days — or THB 2,000 at the counter for 15, or THB 10,000 for five years of remote-work access: match the product to the trip, apply through thaievisa, and pay the official price.
