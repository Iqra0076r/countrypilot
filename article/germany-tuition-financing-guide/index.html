Build the funding stack before borrowing

List scholarships/grants, family contribution, savings, expected earnings and the remaining funding gap. Borrow only for the gap. A loan should solve a defined financing need, not substitute for a realistic total-cost budget. use WeMakeScholars to confirm the live requirement where it can change.

Eligibility, co-signers and security

Private international education lenders may restrict schools, degrees, nationalities or countries. Some require a local co-signer; others underwrite without one at selected institutions.
