How to use this Australia visa fee guide

On 1 July 2026, Australian visa application charges rose by roughly 25% across nearly all categories, applied with no advance announcement as a 2026–27 Budget measure. This guide lists what Australia charges in 2026 — verified against the Department of Home Affairs in October 2026. Older guides quoting AUD $200 for the Visitor 600 or AUD $2,000 for the Student 500 are out of date.

Fee table: what Australia charges in 2026

From the 1 July 2026 schedule: Visitor 600 (Tourist, outside AU) from AUD $250 (was $200); ETA 601 AUD $20 via the official app; eVisitor 651 free; Transit 771 free; Student 500 from AUD $2,500 (was $2,000); Skills in Demand 482 from AUD $4,015 (was $3,210); Temporary Graduate 485 from AUD $5,750 (was $4,600). Charges are ‘from’ figures for the main applicant — confirm the live schedule before paying.

Visitor and electronic routes: 600, 601, 651, 771

The ETA (subclass 601) costs AUD $20 via the official app; the eVisitor (subclass 651) is free via ImmiAccount for eligible European passports. The Visitor 600 is for everyone else — Tourist stream from AUD $250 since 1 July 2026 (was AUD $200); onshore Tourist applications cost AUD $630. The Transit 771 is free.

Student and graduate routes: 500 and 485

The Student visa (subclass 500) costs from AUD $2,500 for the main applicant — the fourth increase in three years, with the charge rising from AUD $710 to AUD $2,500 over that period, on top of tuition and mandatory OSHC health cover. The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) costs from AUD $5,750, up from AUD $4,600.

Work route: Skills in Demand 482

The Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482) — which replaced the old Temporary Skill Shortage visa on 7 December 2024 — costs from AUD $4,015 for the main applicant, up from AUD $3,210 on 1 July 2026.

Health cover cost: OSHC for students

OSHC is mandatory for the entire stay on a Student visa, from a government-authorised provider — a separate cost from the visa fee. Single cover runs roughly AUD $630–$800 per year on 2026 comparisons (ahm about $631, nib about $632, Allianz Care about $665, Bupa about $698); couples and family cover costs several thousand dollars a year.

Funds students must be able to show

Home Affairs requires proof of living costs of AUD $29,710 per year for the primary applicant, plus tuition and travel — in force since 10 May 2024. Budgeting for a student visa means proving funds, not just paying the fee.

What the headline fee does not cover

The application charge buys the lodgement of your application. Around it sit the real budget items: OSHC, certified English translations of non-English documents, travel to a biometrics collection centre if requested, and the funds evidence itself. Plan the total, not the fee.

How the routes compare on cost

A short electronic visit costs AUD $20 or nothing; a Visitor 600 costs from AUD $250; study costs from AUD $2,500 plus OSHC and the funds requirement; a Graduate 485 costs from AUD $5,750; employer-sponsored work on the 482 costs from AUD $4,015. The longer the stay Australia grants, the higher the charge.

Children and family members

Every traveller needs their own grant — children cannot ride on a parent’s ETA or visa. Each application carries its own charge, so a family multiplies the fee.

Where to pay: the official channels

Most visas are lodged and paid through ImmiAccount; the ETA (601) is paid in the official Australian ETA app.

Common fee mistakes to avoid

  • Quoting pre-July-2026 fees — the 600 moved from AUD $200 to $250, the student visa to AUD $2,500.
  • Paying a third-party site for an ETA or eVisitor — the 651 is free and the 601 costs AUD $20; intermediaries add large markups.
  • Budgeting the fee but forgetting OSHC, translations and travel.
  • Treating OSHC as optional — it is mandatory for the whole student stay.

Budgeting the whole application

A realistic budget adds the charge, a year of OSHC (roughly AUD $630–$800 single), translations and travel.

Processing times and planning lead times

Home Affairs’ August 2026 monthly publication: Visitor visas (600/601/651 combined) under 1 day; Student visas 22 days; Skilled (temporary) 134 days. Lodge 600 applications 6–8 weeks before travel in busy periods.

Fee scams to avoid

The 651 is free and the 601 costs AUD $20, so any site charging far more for ‘ETA processing’ is a markup intermediary. No one can expedite a Home Affairs decision for a fee. Never pay a ‘visa fee’ to a personal bank account.

Translations and document costs

Documents not in English need certified English translations — NAATI-accredited translators are the standard in Australia — a separate cost from the fee. Upload clear colour scans: poor-quality images are a common cause of delays.

Interviews and biometrics

Visitor applicants are not interviewed as a matter of course; biometrics are collected only if requested.

Family and group applications

Family members lodge separate applications through one ImmiAccount; each is decided and charged individually. The Sponsored Family stream adds the sponsor’s undertaking (and possibly a security bond) on top of the charge.

Nothing to pay at the border

Declaring AUD$10,000 or more in cash is free; non-declaration risks seizure. Biosecurity fines apply for undeclared goods.

Onshore applications cost more

Where condition 8503 (‘no further stay’) is not imposed on a Visitor 600, an onshore Tourist-stream application is possible — at AUD $630, against from AUD $250 offshore. Overstaying can trigger a 3-year exclusion period.

How to research fees without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) the Home Affairs fee schedule for charges; 2) the monthly visa-processing-times publication for timelines; 3) VEVO for your own grant. The 1 July 2026 round invalidated a large share of older guides — any page quoting AUD $200 for the 600 or AUD $2,000 for the 500 is out of date.

Fee checklist before you lodge

  • Current charge confirmed on the Home Affairs fee schedule (post–1 July 2026)
  • OSHC arranged for the full stay if applying for a student visa
  • Certified English translations ready for non-English documents
  • ImmiAccount credentials retained; receipt of payment kept
  • Funds evidence meeting the AUD $29,710-a-year student figure where applicable

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is an Australian visitor visa in 2026?

The Visitor visa (subclass 600, Tourist stream) costs from AUD $250 for applications lodged outside Australia since 1 July 2026 (up from AUD $200). The ETA (subclass 601) costs AUD $20 via the official app, the eVisitor (subclass 651) is free, and the Transit visa (subclass 771) is free.

How much is the Australian student visa in 2026?

The Student visa (subclass 500) costs from AUD $2,500 — the fourth increase in three years. Students must also hold OSHC health cover for the entire stay (roughly AUD $630–$800 a year single) and prove living costs of AUD $29,710 a year plus tuition and travel.

Why did Australian visa fees jump in 2026?

On 1 July 2026, visa application charges rose roughly 25% across nearly all categories as a 2026–27 Budget measure, applied with no advance announcement. Older guides quoting the pre-July figures are out of date as of October 2026.

Is the Australian ETA free?

The ETA (subclass 601) costs an AUD $20 service charge through the official Australian ETA app. The eVisitor (subclass 651) is free via ImmiAccount, and the Transit visa (subclass 771) is free. Third-party sites charging far more are markup intermediaries.

Do I need health insurance on top of the visa fee?

For students, yes: OSHC is mandatory for the entire stay on a Student visa and is a separate cost from the application charge. Travel insurance is not an entry condition for the ETA, eVisitor or Visitor 600.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Australia in 2026, budget from the post–1 July fee schedule: from AUD $250 for a Visitor 600, AUD $20 for an ETA, nothing for the eVisitor or transit visa, from AUD $2,500 for a Student 500, from AUD $4,015 for a Skills in Demand 482 and from AUD $5,750 for a Temporary Graduate 485. The fee is only the first line of the budget — OSHC, translations and the student funds test sit alongside it. Confirm every figure on Home Affairs before you pay.
