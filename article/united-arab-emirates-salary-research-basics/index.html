Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

The UAE uses several residence/work arrangements: standard employer-sponsored employment, self-sponsored Green Visa categories and a residence option for people working remotely for an organisation outside the UAE. These are legally different; a tourist entry does not substitute for employment/residence permission.

Official page: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/Types-of-visas/Residence-visa

Separate the job search from immigration eligibility

Search for jobs and immigration eligibility in parallel. Employment residence usually depends on an employer or an eligible self-sponsored category; emirate-specific channels may differ. Create a one-page route summary before applying: route name, sponsor requirement, occupation rule, salary/skill requirement, language rule, professional registration and whether dependants can accompany.

This prevents a common failure: investing months in a job that cannot support the intended immigration route. How to verify an employer or sponsor

Use official sponsor/accreditation registers where they exist, the company's own careers site, business registry details and a professional email domain. Compare the legal employer name on the offer with the entity in the sponsor register. A recruitment agency may be legitimate, but it should be clear who the actual employer is. Red flags include payment for a job offer, requests to reimburse prohibited sponsor costs, interviews conducted only through anonymous messaging apps, contracts with a different company name, and salary promises that are inconsistent with the written offer. Build an overseas-ready application

A useful international CV is evidence-led: role, employer, dates, quantified responsibilities, tools/technologies, regulated credentials and language ability. Offer-stage checks

Before resigning or paying relocation costs, verify salary, hours, probation, location, remote-work expectations, relocation support, visa costs, health insurance and family benefits. Check whether the contract is conditional on immigration approval. From job offer to lawful work

The employer and applicant may have separate steps. In some systems the employer first obtains accreditation, sponsorship approval, labour-market approval or a job check; only then can the worker apply. In others, the processes are linked. Use https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/residence-visas/residence-visa-for-working-in-the-uae to map the actual sequence.

Do not start work until the status authorises it. Entry as a visitor or student does not automatically permit full-time employment. For readers using United Arab Emirates Salary Research Basics, this is a “From job offer to lawful work” issue rather than a stand-alone rule; verify it against UAE Government / ICP / GDRFA before committing money or documents.

How to research salary properly

Use several sources: official occupation data where available, major job boards, professional associations and real vacancies in the same city/region. Compare base salary separately from bonus, overtime, housing and pension.

For immigration, use the salary definition in the visa rules rather than a market-salary website. Some routes exclude allowances or use an hourly/annualised calculation.
