This How To Verify A Sponsor guide shows how to verify those three layers before sending documents or paying anyone.

Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

Japan's system is based on a defined status of residence matching the activity, not a generic work visa. The Certificate of Eligibility process covers multiple long-term statuses including Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, Highly Skilled Professional and Specified Skilled Worker. A CoE supports the process but is not itself entry permission.

Official page: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/procedures/16-1

Separate the job search from immigration eligibility

Search for jobs and immigration eligibility in parallel. Japan uses defined statuses of residence, so the proposed job and activities must fit the granted status; a Certificate of Eligibility is commonly part of long-term entry processing. Create a one-page route summary before applying: route name, sponsor requirement, occupation rule, salary/skill requirement, language rule, professional registration and whether dependants can accompany.

This prevents a common failure: investing months in a job that cannot support the intended immigration route. How to verify an employer or sponsor

Use official sponsor/accreditation registers where they exist, the company's own careers site, business registry details and a professional email domain. Compare the legal employer name on the offer with the entity in the sponsor register. A recruitment agency may be legitimate, but it should be clear who the actual employer is. For readers using Japan How To Verify A Sponsor, this is a “How to verify an employer or sponsor” issue rather than a stand-alone rule; verify it against Immigration Services Agency of Japan / Ministry of Foreign Affairs before committing money or documents.

Red flags include payment for a job offer, requests to reimburse prohibited sponsor costs, interviews conducted only through anonymous messaging apps, contracts with a different company name, and salary promises that are inconsistent with the written offer. Build an overseas-ready application

A useful international CV is evidence-led: role, employer, dates, quantified responsibilities, tools/technologies, regulated credentials and language ability. Remove vague objective statements and focus on the requirements in the vacancy. In the cover letter, answer the employer's risk questions directly: Are you eligible for the occupation? Do you need sponsorship? Are your qualifications recognised? When can you relocate? If the employer uses a formal sponsorship programme, say which route you believe applies without pretending to give legal advice. Offer-stage checks

Before resigning or paying relocation costs, verify salary, hours, probation, location, remote-work expectations, relocation support, visa costs, health insurance and family benefits. Check whether the contract is conditional on immigration approval. For this Japan How To Verify A Sponsor research path, the practical checkpoint is “Offer-stage checks”; use Immigration Services Agency of Japan / Ministry of Foreign Affairs to confirm the live requirement where it can change.

A genuine offer can still be unsuitable if the salary fails the immigration threshold, the job title does not match the actual duties, or the employer is not eligible to sponsor. From job offer to lawful work

The employer and applicant may have separate steps. In some systems the employer first obtains accreditation, sponsorship approval, labour-market approval or a job check; only then can the worker apply. In others, the processes are linked. Use https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/long/ to map the actual sequence.

Do not start work until the status authorises it. Entry as a visitor or student does not automatically permit full-time employment.
