Singapore Visa Processing Time 2026: 3–5 Days for Tourists, Weeks for Passes

Singapore's tourist visa is decided in 3–5 working days in 2026 — but it must go through an authorized agent. Employment and Student's Passes run on separate, slower clocks. Here is each timeline.

Singapore's timelines split cleanly by purpose. The tourist visa is decided in 3–5 working days in 2026 — but it is agent-mediated: there is no self-service eVisa portal for individuals, and applications go through an authorized visa agent. Work and study passes are decided by the Ministry of Manpower and ICA on longer clocks measured in weeks.

This guide sets out the 2026 timelines for each route, the agent system that controls the tourist clock, and the eligibility-first discipline that matters for passes — because all pass fees are non-refundable.

The tourist clock: 3–5 working days via an agent

Singapore's tourist visa is electronic but agent-mediated — individuals cannot apply directly to ICA. An authorized visa agent submits on your behalf; ICA typically decides in 3–5 working days in 2026. The official S$30 processing fee is fixed and non-refundable; the agent adds a service charge on top.

Because the agent controls submission timing, choose one that files the same day — a slow agent adds invisible days before ICA's clock starts. Agents adding large 'express' surcharges for the standard 3–5-day service are padding: there is no official express tier to buy.

The pass clocks: Employment and Student's

Employment Pass (application + issuance S$330: S$105 + S$225, 2026, plus S$30 for a Multiple Journey Visa if needed): the employer files through the myMOM Portal. For Singapore-registered companies the decision takes about 10 business days; for overseas companies without a local presence it stretches to up to 8 weeks, with 3+ weeks typical. The company's registration status is the main variable.

Student's Pass (processing + issuance S$105: S$45 + S$60, 2026): filed through SOLAR+/ICA e-Service after the institution's acceptance. Decisions take 2–4 weeks — 1–2 weeks for Institutes of Higher Learning, longer during peak intake when ICA processes in volume.

All pass fees are non-refundable — get the eligibility assessment right before the employer or institution files, because a refused application costs the fee and the weeks.

The SG Arrival Card: free and separate

The SG Arrival Card is mandatory for all travellers but it is not a visa — it is a free electronic arrival declaration submitted via ICA within 3 days before arrival. Anyone charging a fee to submit it is running a markup scam.

Do not confuse the Arrival Card's instant submission with visa processing: visa-required nationalities still need the 3–5-day tourist visa plus the Arrival Card. They are two separate steps.

What slows a Singapore file down

  • Slow agents (tourist). The 3–5 days is ICA's clock — the agent's submission speed is the hidden variable. Confirm same-day filing.
  • Overseas-company EP filings. Employers without Singapore registration face the 8-week end of the range; factor it into start dates.
  • Peak-intake Student's Pass volume. Apply the moment the institution issues the SOLAR+ application number.
  • Eligibility misjudgement. With non-refundable fees, a file that was never eligible wastes both money and weeks — assess first, file second.
  • Document mismatches. Passport bio-page, photograph and form details must align exactly; agents submit what you give them.

When to apply: the planning formula

Tourist visa: apply through the agent 2–3 weeks before travel — the 3–5-day decision leaves comfortable margin for one query round.

Employment Pass: the employer should file 8–10 weeks before the intended start date for overseas companies, 4–6 weeks for Singapore-registered ones.

Student's Pass: file immediately on receiving the institution's acceptance package — 6–8 weeks before term for non-IHL institutions, 4 weeks for IHLs outside peak intake.

Tracking and the live picture

Tourist-visa applicants track through their agent; Employment Passes through the employer's myMOM Portal; Student's Passes through SOLAR+/ICA e-Service. ICA and MOM are the only authorities that decide — no one can guarantee pass approval, and no agent can accelerate it.

Confirm current timelines on ica.gov.sg and mom.gov.sg before planning; pass criteria and processing loads shift with policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Singapore tourist visa take in 2026?

Typically 3–5 working days from submission by an authorized agent (S$30 ICA fee plus the agent's charge). There is no self-service eVisa portal — it must go through an agent.

How long does the Singapore Employment Pass take?

About 10 business days for Singapore-registered companies; up to 8 weeks for overseas companies (3+ weeks typical). The employer files via the myMOM Portal.

How long does the Singapore Student's Pass take?

2–4 weeks in 2026 — 1–2 weeks for Institutes of Higher Learning, longer at peak intake.

Is the SG Arrival Card a visa?

No. It is a free, mandatory electronic arrival declaration submitted via ICA — not a visa, and anyone charging for it is running a scam.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Three to five working days for the tourist visa, ~10 business days to 8 weeks for the Employment Pass, 2–4 weeks for the Student's Pass — with the agent's speed and the employer's registration status as the hidden variables. File through the official channels, assess eligibility before paying non-refundable fees, and confirm timelines on ICA and MOM.
