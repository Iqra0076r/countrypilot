UAE Visa Fee 2026: Tourist, Transit and Residence Costs

UAE visa fees in 2026 explained: tourist visa prices, visa-on-arrival costs, Green Visa and student residence fees — with the official GDRFA schedule and what the quotes leave out.

How this UAE visa fee guide works

The UAE runs a sponsor-based visa system: almost every visa is issued through a sponsor — an airline, hotel, travel agency, employer, university or family member — via the ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security) or GDRFA Dubai.

The figures below come from GDRFA Dubai's 2026 fee schedule and 2026 policy reporting, verified in October 2026.

The exact fee depends on the channel and the emirate, so always confirm the live figure on u.ae or the GDRFA/ICP site before paying.

2026 UAE tourist visa fees

Pre-arranged tourist visas are applied for inside the UAE by your sponsor — typically an airline (Emirates, Etihad, flydubai), your hotel or a licensed travel agency. GDRFA Dubai's 2026 schedule lists four standard tiers:

Visa-on-arrival fees

Around 50+ nationalities — including the US, UK, EU states, Japan and Singapore — receive 30 to 90 days on arrival depending on nationality, free of charge.

Since June 2026, visa-on-arrival was expanded to nationals of Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, Kenya and South Africa who hold valid residence permits of the US, EU, UK, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand or Canada: 14 days for AED 100 or 60 days for AED 250.

If your nationality qualifies for visa-free arrival, that is the cheapest route by far — no sponsor fee, no application. If it does not, budget from AED 250 plus VAT for the 30-day single-entry visa above.

Residence visa fees: the Green Visa route

The notable self-sponsored route is the Green Visa (5-year residence).

The government fee components are AED 200 for the residence permit + AED 10 Knowledge Dirham + AED 10 Innovation Dirham (plus AED 500 if applied for inside the UAE), with a base of about AED 740 including delivery.

But total first-year all-in costs — medical fitness test, Emirates ID, health insurance — typically run AED 3,500–6,000 (2026).

Employment residence visas, processed in 5–15 working days in 2026, are employer-sponsored with their own fee structure. Residence-visa holders must complete post-arrival steps (medical fitness test, Emirates ID biometrics) within the entry permit's validity — usually 60 days.

Student residence: what the visa really costs

There is no single officially published student-visa fee — the university sponsors the entry permit and residence visa.

Expect all-in costs of roughly AED 3,560–5,560 per year (2026), covering the medical fitness test (~AED 300–500), Emirates ID (~AED 270/year) and permit fees.

Renewals typically run AED 2,000–3,500 per year (2026).

Health insurance is mandatory for student residence: basic plans run about AED 600–1,200 per year, comprehensive cover up to about AED 3,000 per year (2026).

Any source quoting one 'official' student-visa fee as fact is guessing — budget the all-in range instead.

What the quoted fee does not include

Read any quote carefully.

The GDRFA figures above exclude 5% VAT, and the exact government fee depends on the channel and emirate — fees processed in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah can differ, as can fees filed through different sponsor types.

Your sponsor may also charge a service fee on top of the government figure, so ask for an itemised receipt showing the government component separately.

As a rule: if a quote bundles everything into one number with no breakdown, get the breakdown before paying.

When you pay and how long issuance takes

You pay through the sponsor when the application is submitted — for a tourist visa that means the airline, hotel or agency portal.

Tourist e-visas are typically issued within ~48 working hours (2026).

There are no embassy interviews for tourist visas; the process is entirely sponsor-driven and online.

Employment residence visas take 5–15 working days; student residence via university sponsorship 2–4 weeks after document submission (2026).

Because fees move by emirate and channel, verify the live figure on u.ae or GDRFA at the time you apply, not from a months-old quote.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

Legitimate UAE visa fees go through the sponsor's official channel with a receipt — never pay a 'visa fee' to an individual's bank account or crypto wallet.

Be sceptical of agents quoting rules with no government source.

The prime example is the persistent agency rumour of a $4,000 bank-balance requirement for tourist visas: no UAE government source publishes such a rule, and no fee schedule includes a deposit by that name.

The ICP and GDRFA websites, plus u.ae, are the only authorities — if a fee or rule isn't there, treat it as unverified.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a UAE tourist visa in 2026?

From AED 250 for a 30-day single-entry visa, plus 5% VAT (GDRFA Dubai 2026 schedule). The other tiers are AED 690 (30-day multiple), AED 600 (90-day single) and AED 1,740 (90-day multiple) — all plus VAT, and the exact fee can vary by channel and emirate.

Is UAE visa on arrival free?

For around 50+ nationalities (US, UK, EU, Japan, Singapore and others) it is free: 30–90 days on arrival. Nationals of Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, Kenya and South Africa holding qualifying residence permits (US/EU/UK etc.) pay AED 100 for 14 days or AED 250 for 60 days under the June 2026 expansion.

Is there an official UAE student visa fee?

No — there is no single officially published student-visa fee. The university sponsors the entry permit and residence visa; all-in costs run roughly AED 3,560–5,560 per year (2026), plus mandatory health insurance of AED 600–3,000 per year.

Why does the price I see differ from the GDRFA schedule?

The GDRFA figures exclude 5% VAT, and the exact fee depends on the channel and emirate. Your sponsor may also add a service fee on top of the government component — ask for an itemised receipt.

Is there really a $4,000 bank balance requirement for a UAE tourist visa?

No UAE government source publishes such a rule. The claim circulates through visa agencies only. Carry normal evidence of funds for your stay and disregard the figure.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

In 2026, budget from AED 250 + 5% VAT for a 30-day UAE tourist visa, or nothing at all if your nationality qualifies for visa-free arrival — checking the June-2026-expanded list costs nothing and can save the whole sponsor process.

For residence, think in all-in terms: Green Visa AED 3,500–6,000 for the first year, student residence AED 3,560–5,560 per year, neither of which is a single 'official' fee.

Always confirm the live figure on u.ae or GDRFA for your emirate and channel, and never pay a visa fee outside an official channel.
