LMIA Jobs in Canada: Find and Verify Real Opportunities

Learn how to use Canada's official Job Bank route, understand what an LMIA actually means, and verify an employer before relying on a job offer.

By CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated 7 October 2026
CountryPilot Research GuideCanadaJobs & Work Permits
Scope: This page focuses on LMIA-linked employer/job verification. For the worker-side permit categories and application process, use the Canada Work Permit Guide.

“LMIA jobs” is a popular search phrase, but it can be misunderstood. A Labour Market Impact Assessment is primarily an employer-side process. It does not create an automatic visa for a worker and it does not make every advertisement carrying the letters “LMIA” genuine.

Start with Canada's official Temporary Foreign Worker Job Bank

The Government of Canada’s Job Bank has a dedicated Temporary Foreign Workers section. Job Bank describes these vacancies as coming from Canadian employers who want to recruit temporary foreign workers and who have already obtained or applied for an LMIA.

Open Job Bank: Temporary Foreign Workers →

Use its filters for occupation and location rather than relying on copied job lists on third-party sites. Job availability changes constantly, so a static CountryPilot page should teach you how to search and verify instead of reproducing listings that may expire.

LMIA Canada: verify the decision, employer and expiry

A positive LMIA is employer- and job-specific evidence; it is not a transferable “LMIA visa.” Government of Canada guidance says positive LMIA decisions for applications received from 1 May 2024 are valid for up to six months for the worker to use in the relevant process. Always check the expiry date on the actual decision rather than relying on a screenshot or recruiter message.

Canada also publishes quarterly data on employers that received positive LMIAs. That dataset can help with employer research, but historical inclusion does not prove that a current vacancy has an approved LMIA or that a worker will receive a work permit.

What a positive LMIA establishes

IRCC explains that a positive LMIA generally shows that there is a need for a foreign worker and that no Canadian citizen or permanent resident is available to fill the job. For an employer-specific work permit, the employer normally must complete the employer-side steps and give the worker the documents required for the application.

Use a six-step employer verification workflow

  1. Find the job independently. Prefer the employer’s own site or Job Bank rather than an unsolicited social-media message.
  2. Match the legal employer name. The name on the offer, Job Bank record and immigration documents should make sense together.
  3. Check the job terms. Record occupation, location, duties, wage, hours and start date.
  4. Ask which immigration route applies. “We sponsor visas” is not a route. Ask whether the position is LMIA-based, LMIA-exempt or intended for someone who already has work authorization.
  5. Do not buy an LMIA. Treat demands for money to purchase a job offer or an LMIA as a serious warning sign.
  6. Verify before sharing sensitive documents. Passport scans and financial data should not be sent to an unverified recruiter merely to “reserve” a vacancy.

What about city-specific searches?

For city-specific searches such as Edmonton or Calgary, use Job Bank’s location filters to research current vacancies. A city itself does not “sponsor” a worker; sponsorship and LMIA steps belong to a specific employer and job.

LMIA expiry and timing

Service Canada says positive LMIAs issued under current rules are generally valid for a limited period in which the worker needs to submit the work-permit application. Because timing rules can change, use the date and instructions on the actual LMIA letter rather than a screenshot or old forum answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does an LMIA job guarantee a Canadian work permit?

No. An LMIA is part of the employer/job side of some employer-specific work-permit cases. The worker still needs to meet the applicable immigration and work-permit requirements.

Where can I search for LMIA jobs in Canada?

Canada's official Job Bank Temporary Foreign Workers section is a strong starting point because it specifically surfaces employers that have obtained or applied for an LMIA.

Is 'LMIA requested' the same as 'LMIA approved'?

No. Requested indicates a process/application status, not a positive decision. Verify the actual documentation before relying on the job for a work-permit application.

Should I pay a recruiter for an LMIA?

Do not treat an offer to sell an LMIA or job as normal recruitment. Independently verify the employer, the offer and the immigration process before making any payment.
