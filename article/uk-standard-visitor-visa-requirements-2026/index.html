UK Standard Visitor Visa Requirements 2026: Documents and Rules

The UK visitor visa is decided on three questions — purpose, funds, and ties. Here are the 2026 requirements, the £135 fee, and the evidence that answers each question.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20267 min read

The UK Standard Visitor visa is the route for tourism, family visits, and permitted business activities — and it is decided on genuine purpose, sufficient funds, and intention to return. The 2026 requirements add the April fee change to that timeless trio. This guide sets out the documents, the rules, and the traps.

Quick Answer

The Standard Visitor visa (up to 6 months per visit) costs £135 since 8 April 2026 (was £127), applied for online before travel with a biometrics appointment. The decision standard is about 3 weeks outside the UK from biometrics. The file must prove genuine purpose, adequate funds, and ties pulling you home. The passport must be valid for the whole stay — the UK applies no six-month passport rule.

The application process

Apply online before travel through the official GOV.UK route: complete the form, pay the £135 fee, then attend the biometrics appointment. The 3-week service standard is measured from biometrics — and it is a target, not a guarantee, so in busy periods 6–8 weeks before travel is the safer application point. Fees and standards change by regulation; the April 2026 round (£127→£135, ETA £16→£20) is the live reminder to verify before paying.

Requirement 1: genuine purpose

Documents showing why you are going and that the purpose is genuine: a clear itinerary, accommodation arrangements, and the purpose evidence — family details and invitation for family visits, business invitation or conference details for business visits. Vague purpose with nothing behind it is the first credibility failure.

Requirement 2: adequate funds

Evidence you can support yourself for the trip without working or accessing public funds: bank statements showing regular income over months, employment and salary evidence. The test is consistency — statements must match the employment story, and sudden unexplained deposits damage the file. Total the real trip cost first; the balance is judged against it, not against a fixed figure.

Requirement 3: intention to return

Evidence of ties at home: employment with approved leave, property or tenancy, family, ongoing commitments, and a history of complying with other countries' visas. This is the requirement that refuses the most applications — it is a judgment about your life pulling you back, and it must be documented, not asserted.

What the visa allows — and what it does not

Up to 6 months per visit for tourism, family visits, and permitted business activities. It does not allow work, long-term study, or living in the UK through repeated visits. Planning to "live" in the UK on successive visitor visas is the misuse the requirements are designed to catch.

Common refusal traps

The classic failures: inconsistent documents (dates or employment that do not line up), funds that appeared this month, weak ties with nothing documented, and applying on an outdated fee guide (£127 belongs to the pre-April world). Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the UK visitor visa requirements in 2026?

Online application, £135 fee, biometrics, and evidence of genuine purpose, adequate funds, and intention to return — decided in about 3 weeks from biometrics.

How long can I stay on a UK visitor visa?

Up to 6 months per visit.

Does my passport need 6 months validity for the UK?

No — the passport must be valid for the whole of your stay; the UK applies no six-month rule.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

£135 since April 2026, online plus biometrics, decided in about 3 weeks: the visitor visa is won on three documented answers — a genuine purpose, funds that tell a consistent story, and ties that pull you home.
