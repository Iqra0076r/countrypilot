The four parts of transfer cost

Compare: (1) sender fee, (2) exchange rate, (3) intermediary deductions, and (4) receiver fee. Providers often advertise only the first. The most useful comparison is the amount the recipient receives for the same amount sent. verify it against Immigration New Zealand (INZ) before committing money or documents.

Check whether the quoted exchange rate is locked and for how long. A small spread on a large tuition payment can cost more than a visible transfer fee. compare it with the current Immigration New Zealand (INZ) guidance linked below.

Bank wire versus fintech transfer

A bank wire can be appropriate for large documented payments and institutions that require SWIFT details. Fintech providers can be faster or cheaper for common currency corridors. Neither is automatically better. Consider regulation, transfer limits, recipient method, refund process and support. the live Immigration New Zealand (INZ) reference below should be consulted before action.

For tuition, verify whether the university has an approved payment partner; sending to the wrong account can be difficult to reverse. the live Immigration New Zealand (INZ) reference below should be consulted before action.

Compliance and source-of-funds checks

Large or unusual transfers can trigger anti-money-laundering checks. Keep bank statements, salary records, scholarship letters, loan documents or sale agreements that explain the money. A compliance review is not necessarily an accusation; providers are required to understand certain transactions. the live Immigration New Zealand (INZ) reference below should be consulted before action.

Do not split a lawful transfer into many smaller transfers merely to avoid reporting or verification. verify it against Immigration New Zealand (INZ) before committing money or documents.

Speed, cut-off times and failed transfers

Quoted speed often begins after the provider receives cleared funds and passes verification. Weekends, public holidays, intermediary banks, name mismatches and compliance reviews can add time. For deadlines, transfer early and keep proof of payment. verify it against Immigration New Zealand (INZ) before committing money or documents.

If a transfer fails, ask for the exact status: rejected, returned, pending compliance or awaiting beneficiary information. The remedy differs. compare it with the current Immigration New Zealand (INZ) guidance linked below.

Scam prevention

Treat urgent requests to change bank details as high risk. Confirm new beneficiary details by calling a known number. Never pay an immigration 'deposit' or scholarship fee to a personal account unless the official provider clearly instructs it. the live Immigration New Zealand (INZ) reference below should be consulted before action.

If a payment is induced by fraud, contact the sending bank/provider immediately; recovery becomes harder once funds move onward. re-check Immigration New Zealand (INZ) before relying on it.
