How to use this Azerbaijan eVisa guide

For Azerbaijan the answer is unusually simple: almost everyone who is not visa-exempt enters on the ASAN eVisa at evisa.gov.az — fee, timeline, documents and traps are all below. Apply only on the official portal; copycat sites resell the same application at a markup. For work, study or long stays, see the full Azerbaijan Visa & Entry Requirements Guide.

Passport validity and document condition

The portal states that the passport must be valid for at least three months beyond the eVisa's expiry; six months is the safer standard airlines and officers expect. Carry at least one blank page and check the document is undamaged — frayed covers and loose pages give officers reasons to look closer.

The eVisa route, by nationality

The ASAN eVisa at evisa.gov.az is the standard route: USD 29 total (USD 20 state fee + USD 9 service), decided within up to 3 working days (2026) — a clean, official, state-agency system, so apply nowhere else. There is no general visa on arrival. An urgent eVisa tier is reported (higher fee, faster decision) — confirm it on the portal, and check visa-free eligibility for your nationality before paying.

Transit and onward travel

Heydar Aliyev (Baku) airside transit on a through ticket normally needs no visa. Leaving the airport requires the eVisa or an exemption — the $29 eVisa is cheap certainty.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

No official proof-of-funds figure is published for the eVisa. Carry bank statements, an onward or return ticket, proof of accommodation and evidence you can support yourself — printed, not only on a phone.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

No vaccination certificate is required for entry from most countries. Keep standard travel immunizations up to date.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Travel insurance is reported as not mandatory for the eVisa per the official portal — but medical costs fall on the visitor, so a policy with medical and evacuation cover is plain common sense. See the Azerbaijan Travel Insurance & Health Guide for what to look for.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

The eVisa is normally issued for a 30-day stay — read the entry stamp and know your exit date. Overstays in Azerbaijan can mean fines, detention or a future entry ban.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

The eVisa covers visits only. Work and study need separate permits with Azerbaijani sponsorship — category fees are not published on the portal, so confirm with the embassy.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Each traveller needs their own eVisa application — children included. Minors travelling with one parent should carry a birth certificate and a parental consent letter.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

  1. Check visa-free eligibility for your nationality on evisa.gov.az.
  2. Apply online — USD 29 total (2026).
  3. Upload the passport scan and a compliant photograph; have accommodation details ready.
  4. Allow up to 3 working days.
  5. Print the eVisa approval and carry it with your passport.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using a copycat site. The official portal is evisa.gov.az (ASAN) — lookalikes charge markups or harvest passport data.
  • Expecting visa on arrival. There is no general VoA — arrive with the eVisa.
  • Trusting the urgent tier blindly. It is reported, not confirmed — verify on the portal rather than a blog.
  • Skipping insurance. Not mandatory, but medical costs are yours.

Building a stronger eVisa application

A strong eVisa file is complete and consistent: a legible passport scan, the photograph to the portal's specification, names spelled identically everywhere, accommodation details ready. Most problems come from rushing the application the night before a flight.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Only use the official channel — ASAN Visa portal. Copycat sites charge markups or harvest passport data. Carry the approval with your name, passport number and permitted entries on it.

Translations, certifications and document format

The eVisa needs a passport scan and a compliant photograph on the ASAN portal; the form also asks for accommodation details. Bank statements should be recent (typically within 3 months) if funds evidence is ever requested.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Biometric capture is not documented for the eVisa. Expect standard passport control on arrival — photograph and possibly fingerprints at the immigration counter.

Families and group travel

Each family member applies separately, children included. With minors, bring birth certificates or consent documentation in addition to passports.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

A visa is not customs clearance. Declare cash above the local reporting threshold and check restricted items before packing — medications, drones, satellite equipment and certain foods are common flashpoints. For safety context, see the Azerbaijan Travel Safety & Security Guide.

Departure and future travel

Confirm your exit is recorded — keep boarding passes and stamps. Note any immigration incident: future applications to Azerbaijan and other countries ask about it.

How to research a route without getting misled

Rank sources by authority: Azerbaijan's immigration authority and foreign ministry first, then Timatic/IATA, then consular posts, then recent reputable reporting. Treat aggregator visa sites as leads, not answers — they go stale.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Azerbaijan eVisa in 2026?

USD 29 total — USD 20 state fee plus USD 9 service charge (2026, ASAN portal at evisa.gov.az).

How long does the Azerbaijan eVisa take?

Up to 3 working days (reported). An urgent tier is reported but not confirmed — apply at least a week ahead and verify the urgent option on the portal if needed.

Is there visa on arrival in Azerbaijan?

No general visa on arrival. Use the eVisa or a qualifying visa exemption.

Do I need to print the Azerbaijan eVisa?

Yes — carry the printed approval with your passport, and check it shows your name, passport number and permitted entries.

Can I work in Azerbaijan on the eVisa?

No — visits only. Work and study need separate sponsored permits.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Azerbaijan, the ASAN eVisa is the whole story: USD 29 (2026), up to 3 working days, only at evisa.gov.az. No visa on arrival, no copycat sites — apply a week ahead and carry the printed approval.
