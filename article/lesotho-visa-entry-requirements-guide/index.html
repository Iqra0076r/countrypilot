How to use this Lesotho visa and entry guide

Lesotho's 2026 system is defined by a suspension: the e-Visa portal is SUSPENDED — applications now go by email to visa-applications.immigration@gov.ls, with at least 5 business days needed for processing. Do not trust any site still offering a Lesotho e-Visa.

The verified fees (UK High Commission schedule): US$150 single-entry / US$250 multiple-entry visitor visas. Many nationalities are visa-exempt — verify your status on gov.ls before applying. This guide was verified in October 2026.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond arrival with blank pages. Email the visa application at least 2 weeks before travel — 5 business days is the stated minimum, not a guarantee.

Identify the correct visa or entry route

Check visa-exempt status first — many nationalities skip the system. Everyone else emails the immigration address; there is no portal, no VOA, no alternative.

Transit and onward travel

Lesotho's transit follows the entry rule — visa-exempt nationalities transit freely. There is no transit-visa category and no VOA; visa-required travellers email the application regardless of transit purpose. Most overland transit goes via South Africa — which has its own visa regime.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Whatever Lesotho's official rules say on paper, the officer at the desk decides your admission. Carry a return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, and evidence you can fund the trip — bank statements or a card in your own name, not someone else's screenshots. Keep the Lesotho e-Visa portal (SUSPENDED) approval or visa printout accessible offline; airport Wi-Fi is not a document strategy. Visiting friends or family? Their full address and a contact number answer the two most common questions before they're asked.

Answer questions directly and consistently with your application. If anything changed since you applied — new passport, different flight, longer stay — say so upfront rather than letting the officer discover the mismatch.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

No vaccination certificate is a standard Lesotho entry requirement for ordinary tourists. Check current advisories — requirements can be added during health events.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Travel medical insurance is not a Lesotho visa condition, but comprehensive cover is strongly recommended — facilities are limited. No official student-insurance figure is published.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

Have the email visa approval accessible — print it. Keep the return ticket and accommodation details ready. Lesotho's land borders with South Africa are the usual entry points; have documents ready for both sides.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work and study permits run through Lesotho's immigration authorities — no official fee schedules were verified for 2026. A visitor visa never authorises work. Verify every figure with the mission or immigration department before budgeting.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every child needs their own passport and their own Lesotho permission — there is no family visa or shared authorization. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter from the non-travelling parent(s); it is not always a statutory requirement, but officers routinely ask, and a missing letter can delay or derail entry.

Dual nationals should enter and leave on the same passport, and check whether Lesotho restricts dual nationality — some countries treat entry on a foreign passport as a declaration with consequences. Travellers on refugee travel documents or laissez-passers face different rules everywhere: verify with the mission, not with forums.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

Visa route: 1) Verify you're not visa-exempt on gov.ls. 2) Email the application to visa-applications.immigration@gov.ls with supporting documents ≥2 weeks ahead. 3) Pay US$150 single / US$250 multiple. 4) Travel with the printed approval (≥5 business days' processing). There is no portal, no VOA, no shortcut.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using the 'e-Visa portal'. It's suspended — email is the only channel.
  • Paying a third-party 'Lesotho e-Visa' site. Not official — the portal is down.
  • Emailing days before travel. 5 business days minimum — allow 2+ weeks.
  • Skipping the exemption check. Many nationalities don't need the visa at all.
  • Forgetting South Africa's rules. Most itineraries transit South Africa — check its visa regime too.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Caseworkers and officers are testing two things: will you leave, and can you afford the trip. The strongest files show stable, genuine funds over several months — a single large unexplained deposit reads as funds parking and hurts more than it helps. Tie the itinerary to the money: the trip you're describing should be one your documented income plausibly supports.

Show a reason to return — employment with approved leave, enrolment, family, property — and keep the story consistent across every document. If you've been refused before, address the refusal reasons directly with new evidence; resubmitting the same file invites the same answer.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Lesotho's e-Visa portal is SUSPENDED — there is currently no official online application channel. Applications go by email to visa-applications.immigration@gov.ls. Any site currently selling a 'Lesotho e-Visa' is not official.

Translations, certifications and document format

Any document not in Lesotho's accepted language(s) needs a proper translation — check the mission or the Lesotho e-Visa portal (SUSPENDED) for whether certified translation is required, and by whom. Bank statements should be official downloads or stamped originals, not screenshots; employment letters should be on letterhead with a verifiable contact.

Submit scans well before any appointment so there's time to fix rejections. Keep originals for the appointment itself, and keep a full copy of everything you submitted — if the file is queried months later, you'll need to know exactly what the officer saw.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Where Lesotho requires in-person biometrics — fingerprints and a digital photograph — book the appointment early; centres fill up in peak seasons and the decision clock usually starts at the appointment, not the application. The photograph is captured there, so check whether a separate photo upload is even needed before paying for passport photos.

Interviews are the exception rather than the rule for short stays, but they happen where credibility needs testing. Answer from your actual plans, not from what you think the officer wants to hear — memorised scripts are easy to spot and hard to recover from.

Families and group travel

Each traveller — including every child — needs their own permission; there is no family authorization that covers a group. Apply in the same window so decisions land together, but understand each application is decided on its own merits: one refusal doesn't cancel the others, though it should prompt the rest of the group to re-check their own files.

For organised groups, keep a shared folder with every member's approval, the full itinerary, and the group leader's contact details. If one member is pulled aside at the border, the group should know where the documents are — not just that they exist somewhere in someone's email.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Declare currency and restricted goods as required. Keep the printed email visa approval, return ticket and accommodation details with your passport.

Departure and future travel

Extensions go through the immigration authorities before expiry — start early. Overstaying draws fines and future-entry consequences.

How to research a route without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) gov.ls and the immigration department's email channel. 2) The UK High Commission's fee schedule. Distrust any 'Lesotho e-Visa' site — the portal is suspended, and anyone selling access to it is not official.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid per Lesotho's rule, with blank pages, matching the application exactly
  • Visa / e-Visa / authorization approval saved offline and printed — verify it's linked to the current passport
  • Return or onward ticket and accommodation details accessible without internet
  • Proof of funds and ties-to-home evidence packed
  • Travel insurance documents with emergency numbers
  • Any required arrival forms (health, customs, digital entry cards) completed in advance
  • Certified translations for documents not in the accepted language
  • Emergency contacts: your embassy, your insurer, and someone at home who has your itinerary
  • Previous refusals or complications declared honestly wherever asked

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Lesotho is an email-only destination until the portal returns: visa-applications.immigration@gov.ls, 5+ business days, US$150/250 — no e-Visa, no VOA, no shortcuts. Check the exemption list first, apply 2+ weeks ahead, and don't pay any third-party 'e-Visa' site.
