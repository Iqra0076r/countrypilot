Does the country actually have a digital-nomad route?

Sweden does not list a dedicated general digital-nomad residence visa; a lawful residence/work basis is still required. Start with the official immigration portal and identify the legal name of the status. Private sites such as VisaGuide are useful for discovering programmes, but the government page controls whether the route is open and who qualifies.

If there is no dedicated route, do not relabel a tourist visa as a digital-nomad visa. Visitor status may restrict work even when the employer is abroad.
