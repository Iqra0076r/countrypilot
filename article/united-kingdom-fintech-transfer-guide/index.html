The four parts of transfer cost

Compare: (1) sender fee, (2) exchange rate, (3) intermediary deductions, and (4) receiver fee. Providers often advertise only the first. Bank wire versus fintech transfer

A bank wire can be appropriate for large documented payments and institutions that require SWIFT details. Fintech providers can be faster or cheaper for common currency corridors. Neither is automatically better. Consider regulation, transfer limits, recipient method, refund process and support. Compliance and source-of-funds checks

Large or unusual transfers can trigger anti-money-laundering checks. Keep bank statements, salary records, scholarship letters, loan documents or sale agreements that explain the money. Scam prevention

Treat urgent requests to change bank details as high risk. Confirm new beneficiary details by calling a known number. Never pay an immigration 'deposit' or scholarship fee to a personal account unless the official provider clearly instructs it.
