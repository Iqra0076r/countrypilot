Canada Visa for Indian Citizens 2026: TRV Requirements, Fee, and Timeline

Indian passport holders need a visitor visa for every trip to Canada — no eTA route. This is the complete 2026 picture: fee, documents, and realistic timing.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20267 min read

Indian citizens need a visitor visa (Temporary Resident Visa, TRV) for every trip to Canada — India is not eTA-eligible, so there is no lighter route. The application is online, the fee is fixed, and the timeline is the variable. This guide covers the 2026 requirements for Indian applicants end to end.

Quick Answer

Indian citizens need a TRV for tourism, family visits, or business: CAD $100 visa fee plus CAD $85 biometrics per person (2026), applied for online through an IRCC account, allowing stays of up to 6 months per entry. The service standard is 14 days (outside Canada); recent actuals ran around 27 days (September 2026) — check IRCC's processing-times tool for the live figure.

Why Indians need the TRV — not the eTA

Canada's eTA is for visa-exempt nationalities only (the UK among them). India is not on that list, so every Indian traveller — tourist, family visitor, business visitor — needs the full Temporary Resident Visa: online application, fee payment, biometrics at a visa application centre, then the decision wait. Pricing or planning around the eTA is the classic wrong-lane mistake.

The application, step by step

1) Create an IRCC account and complete the online TRV application. 2) Pay CAD $100 plus CAD $85 biometrics per person — families of 5+ applying together are capped at CAD $500 in visa fees. 3) Give biometrics (fingerprints and photo) at the visa application centre on the instruction letter. 4) Wait the decision — and check the processing-times tool rather than the calendar. Full fee detail in our Canada visitor fee guide.

What the file must show

The TRV is decided on purpose, funds, and ties: a genuine reason to visit, enough money for the trip, and reasons to return to India — employment, business, property, family, and a history of leaving countries on time. Funds evidence should be verifiable and consistent: bank statements with regular income over months, not a sudden balance assembled for the application. One consistent story must run through the form, the funds, and the employment evidence.

Timing: plan on the actuals

The 14-day service standard is the target; the ~27-day September 2026 actual is the planning number — and both move. Indian applicants should apply well ahead of fixed travel dates, keep plans refundable until the visa is issued, and re-check the tool rather than trusting a quoted figure. Students face the longer track: the 60-day study-permit standard against ~10-week actuals, with proof of funds at CAD $23,448/year from 1 September 2026 — see our proof-of-funds guide.

At the border

The TRV gets you to the port of entry; the officer there sets the actual stay length — up to 6 months per entry. Carry the documents that supported the application (return ticket, funds evidence, purpose documents) in case you are asked. The visa in the passport is permission to travel to Canada, not a guarantee of any particular admission period.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Canada?

Yes — a visitor visa (TRV) for every trip. India is not eTA-eligible.

How much is the Canada visa fee for Indians in 2026?

CAD $100 plus CAD $85 biometrics per person; families of 5+ applying together cap visa fees at CAD $500.

How long does it take for Indians?

The standard is 14 days; recent actuals were around 27 days — check IRCC's live tool.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Indian citizens Canada is a $100-plus-$85 online application with biometrics, decided on purpose, funds, and ties — planned on the live actuals, not the 14-day standard, with nothing non-refundable booked until the TRV is issued.
