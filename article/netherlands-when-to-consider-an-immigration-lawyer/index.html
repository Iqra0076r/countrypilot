When professional advice is worth paying for

A straightforward application with clear eligibility may be manageable without a lawyer or adviser. Professional help becomes more valuable when there is a refusal, allegation of misrepresentation, criminal/medical issue, complex family history, status violation, urgent deadline, employer compliance problem or legal interpretation that materially affects eligibility.
