Quick Answer

The Canada visitor visa fee for Pakistani citizens in 2026 is CAD $100 per person for the Temporary Resident Visa, plus CAD $85 biometrics at a Visa Application Centre — budget CAD $185 per adult. Applications are made online through an IRCC account.

How this Canada visa fee for Pakistani citizens 2026 guide works

Canada's fees are set by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) and are identical for every nationality. You apply online through an IRCC account, pay by card inside the account, and then give biometrics at a Visa Application Centre after receiving the biometrics instruction letter. The figures below are the 2026 IRCC schedule.

What Pakistani citizens actually pay

Pakistani applicants apply online through an IRCC account and give biometrics at the Visa Application Centre serving Pakistan. Processing times move with volume — check IRCC's live processing-times tool for the current visitor-visa figure before fixing travel dates.

What the quoted fee does not include

The CAD $100 + CAD $85 covers the government side only. It does not include the Visa Application Centre's own service charges, passport courier fees, document translations, or a medical exam if IRCC asks for one. Biometrics, once given, are generally reusable for later applications within their validity — you don't repay the CAD $85 every time.

Where and how to apply

Create an IRCC online account, complete the visitor-visa application, upload documents and pay CAD $100 by card. IRCC then sends a biometrics instruction letter — book the VAC appointment, pay CAD $85 there, and wait for the passport request if approved. Track the application in the same IRCC account throughout.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

IRCC fees are paid only inside your IRCC account or at the official VAC counter — never to an agent's personal account, and never in cash to someone who 'knows an officer'. No consultant can guarantee approval or buy a faster decision; anyone promising either is running a scam.

How much is the Canada visa fee for Pakistani citizens in 2026?

Do I pay biometrics every time I apply?

No — biometrics are generally reusable for later applications within their validity period.
