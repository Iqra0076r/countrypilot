UK Visa Processing Time 2026: Service Standards by Route and Location

The UK publishes service standards, not guarantees. Here is what the 2026 timelines actually promise for each major route — and how to plan around them.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20266 min read

The UK Home Office publishes service standards for visa decisions — targets it aims to meet, not promises. Knowing the standard for your route and where you apply from is the difference between a calm timeline and a missed flight. This guide sets out the 2026 standards as GOV.UK states them.

CountryPilot Research Guide · United Kingdom · Visas & Immigration

Source-led planning guide · Updated October 14, 2026

Quick Answer

For the main routes — Visitor, Student, Skilled Worker — the 2026 service standard is a decision in 3 weeks when applying outside the UK, measured from the biometrics appointment. Applying inside the UK, the standard is 8 weeks. The standard is a target, not a guarantee: in busy periods, apply 6–8 weeks before travel.

Outside UK 3 weeks · Inside UK 8 weeks · Measured from biometrics · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 6 minutes

The 3-week standard: what it covers

The 3-week standard applies outside the UK to the routes most readers use: the Standard Visitor visa (£135), the Student visa (£558 + £776/year IHS), and the Skilled Worker visa (£819–£1,618 outside the UK, plus the £525 Certificate of Sponsorship). The clock starts at the biometrics appointment, not at the online submission — the days between paying online and attending biometrics are outside the standard. Straightforward, complete files are the ones that meet it.

Inside the UK: 8 weeks

Applications made inside the UK — extensions, switching routes — run to an 8-week service standard (2026). The longer window reflects the different casework pipeline for in-country applications. If your leave is expiring, the timing of the application itself (made before expiry, under the right conditions) matters more than the decision date — but that is a separate legal question to confirm on GOV.UK, not something a timeline guide can settle.

"Target, not a guarantee": what that means in practice

Service standards describe what UKVI aims to do, and most straightforward applications land inside them. The files that do not are the ones needing further checks: unverifiable employment, funds needing confirmation, or high-volume periods like the autumn student intake and summer travel season. The practical response is not to hope for the fast end of the range but to apply 6–8 weeks before travel in busy periods, so a slow file still lands in time.

What slows a UK file down

The usual delays: incomplete evidence (the 28-day maintenance window misjudged on a Student application is a classic), documents needing verification, and interviews where credibility needs testing. Each is a file problem, not a queue problem — which means each is fixable before submission. Run the documents through our visa document checklist and, for students, our 28-day rule explainer before the biometrics appointment.

Planning the timeline

Work backwards from travel: subtract the 3-week standard, add 2–3 weeks of margin in busy periods, then place the biometrics appointment — and remember the online submission and fee payment come before it. Fees and service standards change by regulation, so re-check GOV.UK before paying: the 8 April 2026 fee round (£127→£135 visitor, £16→£20 ETA) is a live reminder that last year's numbers expire.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a UK visa take in 2026?

3 weeks outside the UK (Visitor, Student, Skilled Worker) from biometrics; 8 weeks for in-country applications — service-standard targets.

When does the 3-week clock start?

At the biometrics appointment, not at the online submission or payment.

Should I book flights before the decision?

No — not non-refundable ones. The standard is a target, and a delayed file plus fixed flights is the expensive combination.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Three weeks from biometrics outside the UK, eight weeks inside, both as targets: plan 6–8 weeks ahead in busy periods, submit a file that needs no follow-up, and re-check the live standards before you pay.
