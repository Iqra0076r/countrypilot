Quick Answer

$185 — the machine-readable visa (MRV) fee for the B-1/B-2 visitor visa, paid before you book the consular interview. It is non-refundable and covers the application only, not a guaranteed visa. B-1/B-2 visas are often issued for up to 10 years multiple-entry.

How this USA visa fee for Filipino citizens 2026 guide works

The United States charges one flat government fee for a visitor visa regardless of nationality: the $185 machine-readable visa (MRV) fee. You complete Form DS-160 online, pay the fee, and then schedule the interview at a US embassy or consulate. The fee covers processing of the application — it does not buy a visa, and it is not refunded if the visa is refused. Figures here follow the State Department fee schedule as of 2026.

What Filipino citizens actually pay

Filipino applicants interview at the US Embassy in Manila. The $185 MRV fee is the same for all nationalities — check the live Global Visa Wait Times tool for the current Manila wait and apply well ahead of any fixed travel date.

What the quoted fee does not include

The $185 covers the application only. It does not include passport courier or return-delivery fees charged by the local delivery partner, the cost of travelling to the embassy or consulate city for the interview, or any optional help you hire. There is no US-government 'priority processing' upgrade for B-1/B-2 — anyone selling one is selling nothing.

Where and how to apply

Apply through the State Department's system: complete Form DS-160 online, pay the $185 MRV fee via the official payment options, then schedule the interview at the embassy or consulate serving your place of residence. Bring the DS-160 confirmation page, the fee receipt, your passport and supporting documents. Decisions are usually given at the interview; the passport is returned by courier a few working days later.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

The MRV fee is paid only through the official channels shown after your DS-160 — never to a personal bank account, mobile-money number or social-media 'agent'. No one can guarantee or sell a US visa, and refusal does not refund the $185. Treat any quote of a 'new 2026 fee' far above $185 as a scam: the State Department schedule is the only number that counts.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the US visa fee for Filipino citizens in 2026?

The B-1/B-2 visitor visa fee is $185 — the same MRV application fee for every nationality.

Is the $185 US visa fee refundable?

No. The MRV fee is non-refundable, whether the visa is refused or you cancel the interview.

How long is a US B-1/B-2 visa valid?

It is often issued for up to 10 years multiple-entry; each admission is typically up to 6 months (I-94).

How long is the US visa interview wait in 2026?

Waits at Manila move with demand — check the State Department's live Global Visa Wait Times tool before making plans.

Where do I pay the US visa fee?

Through the official payment options presented after you complete Form DS-160. Keep the receipt — it is valid for one year from payment.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Budget exactly $185 for the government fee — and budget far more time than money. In 2026 the interview wait is the real constraint, running many months at South Asian posts. Pay only through official channels, start the DS-160 early, and confirm the live wait on the State Department's tool before booking anything non-refundable.
