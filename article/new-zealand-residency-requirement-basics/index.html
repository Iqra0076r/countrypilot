Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

Temporary work status and residence are separate. New Zealand has resident pathways including Skilled Migrant Category and Green List routes where eligibility is met; holding an AEWV can support a career path but does not automatically become residence.

Official page: https://www.immigration.govt.nz/live/

Map the path from temporary status to long-term status

In New Zealand, resident visas such as Skilled Migrant Category or Green List pathways where eligible is not simply a longer version of a temporary visa. The qualifying route may depend on years of lawful residence, type of permission, absences, employment, language/integration, taxes or other conditions. Some temporary categories count differently from others.

Build a residence timeline now, not at the end. Record every entry/exit, permit type, address and major status change.
