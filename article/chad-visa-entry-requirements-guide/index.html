Transit and onward travel

Do not treat a Chad layover as automatically visa-free. Some travellers who need no visa to visit still need permission to transit, and vice versa. Confirm the transit rule for your nationality on the official e-Visa platform (evisa.td) when the itinerary is ticketed.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Border officers in Chad, like everywhere, are assessing two things: that you will leave when you say you will, and that you can support yourself while there. Have ready: passport and visa/e-Visa printout, onward travel proof, accommodation details, and funds evidence.

Keep the story identical to your application. If someone else funds the trip, carry evidence of that arrangement too — but know that officers weigh your ties to home most heavily.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

No Chad health document for visitors was verifiable in our October 2026 research beyond standard requirements. Health rules are among the fastest-moving entry requirements; re-check the official e-Visa platform (evisa.td) close to travel rather than relying on this guide alone.

Student health insurance: not found (as of 2026)

If your trip later extends to study, confirm whether the student route has its own insurance requirement — several countries in this group do, and the minimum cover is specified by the authorities, not by you.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

Separate two things people constantly confuse: the visa's validity (the window in which you may travel) and the permitted stay (the period stated on your visa or entry stamp). The officer sets the second at entry.

Note the admitted-until date immediately. Extensions, where the system allows them, must be filed before expiry — overstay is a separate violation with its own penalties.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Three different questions, three different permissions: visiting, working, studying. Chad treats them separately, and using the wrong one — especially working on a visitor entry — is the single most common serious violation we see described in refusal accounts.

Arrange the correct status before travel. Switching from visitor to worker or student inside the country is restricted or impossible in most systems.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

For family travel to Chad: one passport per person, one application per person where applications are required. Dual nationals should enter and exit on the same passport throughout the trip and check the official e-Visa platform (evisa.td) for any nationality-specific guidance.

A parental consent letter for minors travelling with one parent or alone is cheap insurance at the border — carry it with contact numbers even when not strictly required.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

Run this checklist for Chad — in this order: 1) Confirm your nationality's route in the table above. 2) Book the consulate appointment early — slots are the usual bottleneck. 3) Assemble the file: passport, photos to specification, funds evidence, itinerary, and purpose documents. 4) Attend, then track the case; do not book non-refundable travel until the visa is in hand.

Build in buffer: consular queues and e-Visa approvals both slip, especially in peak season.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Budgeting from a stale fee guide. The Chad visitor figure is EUR 100 (single entry, 30 days); EUR 150 (multiple entry, 30 days); EUR 200 (multiple entry, 90 days) (as of 2026) — older pages quote superseded amounts.
  • Travelling on last year's rules. Chad changed entry rules in 2025–2026 — check the policy notes in this guide before assuming the old process still works.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the visa is decided. Processing slips everywhere; the approval comes first, the flights second.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

For Chad, as everywhere, officers look for rootedness: a job to return to, a home, family, and a travel history of coming back on time. Assemble those four evidences before you apply, not after a refusal.

Previous refusals must be declared honestly. A prior refusal explained beats a concealed one discovered — misrepresentation findings are far harder to overcome than the original refusal.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Scam avoidance for Chad is mostly one habit: type the official the official e-Visa platform (evisa.td) (https://evisa.td) yourself instead of clicking ads or social-media links. The lookalike sites are sophisticated — same colours, similar URLs, live-chat ‘agents’.

No one can sell you an approval or a faster decision. Anyone promising either is running a scam; report the site and walk away.

Translations, certifications and document format

Assemble the Chad file as the officer will read it: identity (passport, photos), purpose (itinerary, bookings, invitation if any), means (bank statements, employment letter), and roots (property, family, prior compliant travel).

Translations need a signed accuracy statement. Keep a full second copy of the entire file separate from the originals — lost documents mid-process are miserable to reconstruct.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Biometrics (fingerprints, photo) are now routine in Chad visa processes that involve an interview or enrolment appointment. They are an identity check, not an accusation — the appointment itself is usually short.

Prepare for the standard questions — purpose, job, family, previous trips, funding — with answers that match the form word for word. Rehearsed-sounding is fine; contradictory is fatal.

Families and group travel

Family applications to Chad are individual applications that happen to travel together: separate forms, separate fees, separate decisions. A refusal for one person does not automatically affect the others, but the underlying weakness usually does — fix it first.

Children's applications need the same care as adults': complete documents, consistent story, proper photos.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

At Chad customs: cash over the declaration threshold must be reported (it is not taxed, but undeclared cash can be seized), food and plant products are the most commonly confiscated items, and controlled medications need prescriptions.

When in doubt, declare. The penalty for an undeclared item always exceeds the inconvenience of declaring it.

Departure and future travel

Leave Chad on time, every time. The departure record matters as much as the entry — it is what the next officer will look at. If you must stay longer, extend through the official channel before expiry; overstaying ‘just a few days’ is never worth it.

Archive the trip file (approvals, stamps, tickets) for a year. Future-you applying for the next visa will be grateful.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Chad tourist visa or e-Visa in 2026?

EUR 100 (single entry, 30 days); EUR 150 (multiple entry, 30 days); EUR 200 (multiple entry, 90 days) (as of 2026). Confirm the live figure on the official e-Visa platform (evisa.td) before paying — fees move by regulation.

Does Chad offer visa on arrival or an e-Visa?

Chad runs a mandatory online visa system: since 11 May 2026 every visa application must go through evisa.td, and visas issued outside the platform are invalid.

What changed for Chad visas in 2025–2026?

e-Visa became mandatory for all travelers from May 11, 2026 per official notice issued May 8, 2026 by Chad's E-Visa Sub-Directorate (Ministry of Public Security and Immigration), backed by Decree No. 2299/PR/PM/MSPI/2025 and Joint Order No. 033/PR/PMFBEPC/MSPI/2026; manual/paper visa processing abolished; visas issued outside evisa.td invalid from May 21, 2026 (diplomatic/courtesy visas excepted).

What most commonly goes wrong at the Chad border?

Mismatched stories between the application and the interview, missing onward-travel proof, and — where no visa on arrival exists — showing up without a visa at all. The fixes are all boring: consistent paperwork, a return ticket, and the right permission secured in advance.

Do I need travel insurance for Chad?

Student health insurance: not found (as of 2026) Even where it is not a formal entry condition, buy real medical cover — visitors have no access to subsidised care.
