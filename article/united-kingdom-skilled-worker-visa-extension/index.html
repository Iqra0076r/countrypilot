UK Skilled Worker Visa Extension

This guide separates a Skilled Worker visa extension from a change-of-employer update and points you to the current GOV.UK rules, so you can identify the correct process before preparing an application.

By CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated 6 October 2026
CountryPilot Research GuideUnited KingdomVisas & Immigration
Scope: This page is for extending existing Skilled Worker permission. For first-time route eligibility, sponsor, occupation and salary planning, use the UK Skilled Worker Visa Guide.
Choose the correct process first: Extending existing Skilled Worker permission is different from changing employer or making another route change. Confirm which GOV.UK process matches your circumstances before paying fees or requesting new sponsorship documents.

When a Skilled Worker extension is the right process

GOV.UK says you can usually apply to extend a Skilled Worker visa or eligible legacy Tier 2 (General) permission when you remain in the same job, the job uses the same occupation code as before and you are still working for the employer that issued your current Certificate of Sponsorship.

Check your Certificate of Sponsorship and salary again

An extension is not simply a renewal payment. Your employer still needs to provide the appropriate sponsorship information and the job must meet the Skilled Worker requirements that apply to your circumstances. GOV.UK explains that salary rules depend on the occupation, the date of the Certificate of Sponsorship and whether transitional/lower-rate provisions apply.

The general Skilled Worker page currently states that the standard salary threshold is normally the higher of £41,700 a year or the applicable going rate, although lower or different rules can apply in defined circumstances, including some extension situations and healthcare/education roles.

Medium-skilled jobs and transitional rules

GOV.UK currently says a person in a job classified as medium skilled can extend in certain circumstances, including where the first Certificate of Sponsorship was issued before 22 July 2025 and the person has continuously held qualifying Skilled Worker permission since then. These transitional details are highly fact-specific, so verify the current extension page rather than relying on an old eligibility list.

Apply before your current visa expires

The extension application is made online. GOV.UK says you must apply before your current visa expires. It also warns that travel outside the UK, Ireland, Channel Islands or Isle of Man while the application is pending can cause the application to be withdrawn.

How long a decision usually takes

GOV.UK currently states that an in-country extension decision is usually made within 8 weeks after the application date, although cases can take longer where documents need verification, an interview is required or personal circumstances require further checks.

Extension costs

Skilled Worker costs include the application fee and, for most applicants, the Immigration Health Surcharge. GOV.UK currently displays the standard application-fee bands and says the healthcare surcharge is usually charged per year. Because fee schedules change, use the CountryPilot Skilled Worker Visa Cost Calculator as a planning tool and verify the final amount at GOV.UK before payment.

Your partner and children do not extend automatically

A partner or child’s permission does not automatically extend just because the main Skilled Worker extends. They can apply with the main applicant or before their own permission expires. Their applications and maintenance requirements need to be planned separately.

Extension checklist

  • Confirm your current visa expiry date.
  • Confirm whether this is truly an extension rather than a change-of-job/employer update.
  • Check your occupation code and current job details.
  • Obtain the sponsorship information required for the new application.
  • Re-check the salary/going-rate rule that applies to your circumstances.
  • Budget application fees, healthcare surcharge and dependant costs.
  • Plan dependant applications separately.
  • Keep evidence and submission confirmations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extend a UK Skilled Worker visa with the same employer?

Usually yes if the extension requirements are met, including the sponsored job and occupation-code conditions that apply to your case. Check the current GOV.UK extension page.

Do I need an extension if I change employer?

A change of employer normally means you need to update your Skilled Worker visa rather than simply extend the existing sponsored arrangement.

How long does a Skilled Worker extension decision take?

GOV.UK currently says in-country extension decisions are usually made within 8 weeks, although individual cases can take longer.

Do my dependants extend automatically?

No. A partner or child's permission does not automatically extend with the main applicant. They need their own application before their permission expires.
