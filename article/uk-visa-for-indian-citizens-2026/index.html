UK Visa for Indian Citizens 2026: Visitor, Student, and Work Routes

Indian passport holders need a UK visa for every visit — no ETA route. This is the complete 2026 picture across visitor, student, and work routes.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20268 min read

India is not a visa-exempt country for the UK, so Indian citizens need a visa for every visit — the ETA does not apply. The three routes that matter are the Standard Visitor, the Student, and the Skilled Worker visas, each with its own 2026 fee and evidence bar. This guide covers all three.

Quick Answer

Indian citizens need a UK visa before travel. The Standard Visitor visa (6 months) costs £135 (from 8 April 2026, was £127), decided in about 3 weeks outside the UK from biometrics. The Student visa costs £558 plus the £776/year Immigration Health Surcharge, with maintenance of £1,529/month in London or £1,171/month elsewhere held 28 days. The Skilled Worker route needs a licensed sponsor and a £525 Certificate of Sponsorship, with application fees of £819–£1,618 outside the UK.

Visitor £135 · Student £558 + £776/yr IHS · Decision ~3 weeks · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 8 minutes

Do Indians need a UK visa — or an ETA?

A visa, always. The ETA (£20) is for visa-exempt nationalities only and is irrelevant to Indian passport holders. Every Indian traveller — tourist, family visitor, business visitor, student, worker — goes through a full visa application: online submission, fee payment, biometrics appointment, then the decision wait. There is no shortcut lane.

Route 1: Standard Visitor (£135)

For tourism, family visits, and permitted business activities: £135 since 8 April 2026, up to 6 months per visit, decided in about 3 weeks from biometrics. The file must show genuine purpose, sufficient funds, and intention to return — employment in India, property or family ties, and a travel history of compliance. The passport needs to be valid for the whole stay; the UK applies no six-month passport rule. The current fee and the April 2026 changes are detailed in our United Kingdom visa guide.

Route 2: Student (£558 + IHS + maintenance)

For degree study: a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed sponsor, the £558 application fee, the £776/year Immigration Health Surcharge paid upfront, and maintenance funds of £1,529/month (London) or £1,171/month (elsewhere) for up to 9 months — held for 28 days ending within 31 days of application. The 28-day rule is the most failed detail in Indian student files; the maintenance rules are covered in our student fee guide.

Route 3: Skilled Worker (sponsored)

For sponsored employment: a licensed sponsor, a Certificate of Sponsorship (£525 per certificate, 2026), and an application fee of £819–£1,618 outside the UK depending on circumstances, plus the health surcharge. Note the July 2025 policy change that raised the Skilled Worker skill level to degree level — the route is narrower than older guides describe. Verify current eligibility on GOV.UK before an employer invests in sponsorship.

Timing and planning

The 3-week service standard is measured from the biometrics appointment and is a target, not a guarantee — in busy periods, 6–8 weeks before travel is the safer application point. Fees and standards change by regulation (the April 2026 round is the proof), so confirm the live figures on GOV.UK before paying. And run the documents through our visa document checklist before biometrics.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Indians travel to the UK on an ETA?

No. Indian citizens need a full visa; the ETA is for visa-exempt nationalities only.

How much is the UK visitor visa for Indians in 2026?

£135 for the 6-month Standard Visitor visa (from 8 April 2026).

How long does the UK visa take for Indians?

About 3 weeks outside the UK from the biometrics appointment — a service-standard target, not a guarantee.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Indian citizens the UK has no shortcuts: £135 for the visitor, £558 plus surcharge and 28-day-held maintenance for the student, sponsorship for the worker — each applied for online with biometrics, each decided in about 3 weeks, each verified on GOV.UK before you pay.
