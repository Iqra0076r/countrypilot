Build the funding stack before borrowing

List scholarships/grants, family contribution, savings, expected earnings and the remaining funding gap. Borrow only for the gap. A loan should solve a defined financing need, not substitute for a realistic total-cost budget. re-check WeMakeScholars before relying on it.

International students should also check whether the visa requires funds to be held in a specific way; a loan approval letter may or may not satisfy the immigration rule. verify it against Australia Home Affairs before committing money or documents.

Eligibility, co-signers and security

Private international education lenders may restrict schools, degrees, nationalities or countries. Some require a local co-signer; others underwrite without one at selected institutions. Understand whether the co-signer is jointly liable and what happens if either borrower misses payments.
