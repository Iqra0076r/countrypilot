Does the country actually have a digital-nomad route?

The Netherlands does not list a broad digital-nomad visa; self-employment and employment residence routes have their own conditions. Start with the official immigration portal and identify the legal name of the status. Private sites such as VisaGuide are useful for discovering programmes, but the government page controls whether the route is open and who qualifies.

If there is no dedicated route, do not relabel a tourist visa as a digital-nomad visa. Visitor status may restrict work even when the employer is abroad.
