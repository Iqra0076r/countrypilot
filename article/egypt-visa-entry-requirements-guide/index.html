How to use this Egypt visa and entry guide

Egypt controls visitor entry through the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg). Egypt runs three parallel tourist channels: the e-Visa at visa2egypt.gov.eg for ~75 nationalities, visa on arrival for ~46, and — since 1 August 2026 — a digital VoA pilot at Cairo airport with QR-code payment.

What changed most recently: Tourist e-Visa fees raised April 27, 2026: single-entry $25 to $30, multiple-entry $60 to $65 (reflected on official portal). Visa-on-arrival fee raised $25 to $30 on March 1, 2026.…

Passport validity and document condition

Most countries — Egypt included — expect your passport to be valid well beyond your trip; six months beyond your planned stay is the common airline-enforced standard. Because the exact rule for Egypt was not verifiable from an official source in our October 2026 check, confirm the requirement on the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) or via the IATA Timatic database your airline uses before you book.

Keep at least one blank page for stamps and make sure the document is undamaged — a frayed passport can be refused at the counter regardless of its expiry date.

Transit and onward travel

Transit is its own immigration question in Egypt, separate from entry. If you change planes on Egypt soil, check whether the regime above requires a transit permission even without leaving the airport — being denied boarding at origin is the common failure mode, not refusal at the Egypt airport.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

A visa or visa-free entitlement gets you to the Egypt border; the officer there decides admission. Carry the passport (with the visa or e-Visa approval), a return or onward ticket, the address where you will stay, and evidence you can fund the trip.

Expect questions on purpose, length of stay and funding. Answer directly and consistently with whatever you declared in the application — contradictions between the form and the interview are the fastest route to secondary inspection.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Our October 2026 check found no Egypt-specific vaccination certificate required for ordinary visitors beyond the standard international rules. That can change with outbreaks — check the current health-entry page on the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) and the WHO destination guidance within two weeks of departure.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Our October 2026 research could not verify whether Egypt mandates travel health insurance for visitors. Buy it anyway: a policy with solid medical limits and emergency evacuation costs little against the price of an uninsured hospital stay abroad.

If your trip later extends to study, confirm whether the student route has its own insurance requirement — several countries in this group do, and the minimum cover is specified by the authorities, not by you.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

The permission you receive on arrival to Egypt — the period stated on your visa or entry stamp — is what counts, not the visa's validity window. Check the stamp or electronic record before leaving the counter; the date there is your legal limit.

Overstaying, even briefly, can void the visa and trigger re-entry bans. If you need longer, apply for an extension through the proper channel before the permission expires — do not simply remain.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

A visitor entry to Egypt never authorizes employment — not remote work for a foreign employer while physically in Egypt, not unpaid help at a family business. Work and study each need their own permission with work route around $32 (as of 2026) and study route around EGP 6,877 - 7,097 (as of 2026).

Do not enter as a visitor intending to ‘sort out’ work or study later; misrepresentation at entry is a separate ground for refusal or removal.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every traveller to Egypt needs their own travel document — children included, with no sharing of passports. Where a visa or e-Visa is required, each child needs their own application.

Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter from the non-travelling parent(s) with contact details, plus the birth certificate when surnames differ. It is not always a legal requirement, but officers may ask and having it avoids delays.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

A reliable sequence for Egypt: 1) Confirm your nationality's route in the table above. 2) Apply on the official portal (the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) — https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg) and pay only there. 3) Save the approval offline as well as on your phone. 4) Verify the approval is linked to the passport you will actually travel on.

Build in buffer: consular queues and e-Visa approvals both slip, especially in peak season.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming visa on arrival means no preparation. For Egypt, have the fee in the right currency and the documents ready — the counter is not the place to discover a missing printout.
  • Budgeting from a stale fee guide. The Egypt visitor figure is $30 (as of 2026) — older pages quote superseded amounts.
  • Travelling on last year's rules. Egypt changed entry rules in 2025–2026 — check the policy notes in this guide before assuming the old process still works.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the visa is decided. Processing slips everywhere; the approval comes first, the flights second.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Most visitor-visa refusals worldwide come down to one doubt: will this person leave? A strong Egypt file answers it directly — stable employment with a leave letter, property or lease documents, family ties, prior compliant travel, and bank statements showing you can fund the trip.

Keep the story identical across the form, the interview and the documents. Vague itineraries read as risk; dated plans with bookings and a return ticket read as a genuine visit.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

For Egypt, start every application at the official the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) (https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg) — the only channel whose outcome counts. Copycat sites buy ads on the portal's own name and charge large markups for submitting the same form, or harvest passport scans.

Red flags anywhere: ‘guaranteed approval’, fees far above the official schedule, payment to personal accounts or crypto wallets, and pressure to decide today. Real immigration fees go through official channels only.

Translations, certifications and document format

For a Egypt application, bring originals plus one copy of everything: bank statements (3–6 months), an employment letter with approved leave dates, property or lease documents, prior visas and entry stamps, and the trip itinerary with bookings.

Documents in another language need an English (or locally accepted language) translation with a translator's statement of accuracy. Photographs must meet the specification published for Egypt — wrong-size photos are a needless reason for delay.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Where Egypt requires an in-person step, expect identity checks — fingerprints and a digital photograph are standard at interviews and ports of entry. Answer questions on purpose, employment, family, prior travel and funding briefly and truthfully, matching the application exactly.

At the border, devices can be inspected under border-search authority in many countries — travel with a device you are comfortable having examined, and remain calm and direct if sent to secondary inspection.

Families and group travel

Every traveller to Egypt needs their own authorisation — each family member, including infants, needs a separate application or e-Visa where one is required. There is no family visa.

Book appointments as close together as the system allows, and if one member is refused, address the refusal reason before anyone re-applies — do not try to ‘cover’ it with reworded facts.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Customs in Egypt follows the usual pattern: declare cash above the reporting threshold, declare food and agricultural products, and keep medications in original packaging with prescriptions for anything controlled.

Check the current allowances for alcohol and tobacco per adult traveller before you pack — and never carry anything across a border for someone else, no matter how innocent it looks.

Departure and future travel

A clean Egypt travel history — entries and timely departures — is an asset that compounds: future applications get easier, not harder. An overstay does the opposite, and can void the visa plus trigger multi-year bars.

If plans change mid-trip, use the proper extension or status-change channel before the permission expires. And keep copies of every approval after the trip; they are evidence for the next application.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Egypt tourist visa or e-Visa in 2026?

$30 (as of 2026). Confirm the live figure on the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) before paying — fees move by regulation.

Does Egypt offer visa on arrival or an e-Visa?

Egypt runs three parallel tourist channels: the e-Visa at visa2egypt.gov.eg for ~75 nationalities, visa on arrival for ~46, and — since 1 August 2026 — a digital VoA pilot at Cairo airport with QR-code payment.

What changed for Egypt visas in 2025–2026?

Tourist e-Visa fees raised April 27, 2026: single-entry $25 to $30, multiple-entry $60 to $65 (reflected on official portal). Visa-on-arrival fee raised $25 to $30 on March 1, 2026.

What most commonly goes wrong at the Egypt border?

Mismatched stories between the application and the interview, missing onward-travel proof, and — where no visa on arrival exists — showing up without a visa at all. The fixes are all boring: consistent paperwork, a return ticket, and the right permission secured in advance.

Where is the official Egypt application site?

the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) — https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg. Bookmark it and type it yourself rather than clicking ads; copycat sites buy ads on the portal's own name and charge markups for the same form.
