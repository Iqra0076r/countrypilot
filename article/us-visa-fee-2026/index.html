US Visa Fee 2026: B-1/B-2, F-1, and What the $185 Covers

US visa fees are set by regulation and change by category. Here is what each major 2026 application costs — and what the headline fee does and does not include.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20266 min read

The US does not have one visa fee — it has a fee schedule, and the number that matters depends on the category you apply in. For most readers that means the $185 visitor-visa fee, but students pay a second fee on top, and some nationalities owe a further issuance fee after approval. This guide lays out the 2026 fees as the US State Department's schedule defines them.

Quick Answer

The B-1/B-2 visitor visa application fee is $185 (the machine-readable-visa fee, 2026) — paid with Form DS-160 before the embassy interview. F-1 students pay the same $185 plus the $350 SEVIS I-901 fee. C-1 transit is also $185. After approval, check whether your nationality owes a separate visa issuance (reciprocity) fee before arranging passport return.

B-1/B-2 $185 · F-1 $185 + $350 SEVIS · Transit $185 · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 6 minutes

The B-1/B-2 fee: $185

The visitor-visa application costs $185, the machine-readable-visa (MRV) fee as of 2026. It is paid before the interview, as part of the DS-160 process, and it covers the application itself — the interview, the checks, and the decision. It is not a payment for a visa: a refused application is not refunded. The fee is per applicant, so a family of four pays four times. When you apply, the steps run: complete Form DS-160, pay the $185 MRV fee, then schedule the interview at the embassy or consulate.

Student visas: $185 plus $350 SEVIS

Study requires F-1 status, and the fee stack is two layers: the $185 MRV fee for the visa application itself, plus the $350 I-901 SEVIS fee (as of 2026) that registers you in the Student and Exchange Visitor Information System. SEVIS registration happens against the Form I-20 issued by your school — and the school's published cost of attendance is the figure used for the separate financial-evidence requirement. Budget both fees together; they are not interchangeable and neither is waived.

Transit and other categories

Even an airside-only connection through the US needs a C-1 transit visa for most nationalities — at $185 (2026), the same MRV fee. Work routes sit on a different scale entirely: the headline to know is the $100,000 H-1B fee added after 21 September 2025, on top of standard USCIS fees — a figure that reshaped employer-sponsored planning for the 2026 cycle. Always verify the current schedule before paying: fees change by regulation.

The reciprocity fee: the second payment some nationalities owe

After a visa is approved, some nationalities owe an additional visa issuance (reciprocity) fee, set country by country to mirror what the US citizen pays in reverse. It is not part of the $185 and it is not owed by everyone — check whether your nationality owes one after approval, before you arrange passport return, so the final step is not held up.

Fees change — verify before paying

The State Department's fee schedule and the Global Visa Wait Times tool are the live sources for both what you pay and how long the interview queue is at your post. Interview wait times move weekly and fees change by regulation — re-check the live pages before paying anything or booking non-refundable travel. Older guides quoting earlier fee levels are out of date for the 2026 cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a US tourist visa in 2026?

The B-1/B-2 application fee is $185 (MRV fee, 2026), paid before the interview.

How much does an F-1 student visa cost in total?

$185 MRV fee plus $350 SEVIS I-901 fee (2026) — $535 in government fees before any other costs.

Is the $185 refunded if the visa is refused?

No. The MRV fee covers processing the application, which you received; refusals are not refunded.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

$185 for the visitor or transit application, $185 plus $350 SEVIS for students, a possible reciprocity fee after approval — and the live fee schedule checked before you pay a cent, because these numbers move by regulation.
