How to use this Indonesia visa and entry guide

Indonesia offers both routes: e-VOA and counter VOA for ~97 countries at IDR 500,000 (30 days, extendable once), plus visa-exempt entry (VEA) — 30 days, non-extendable — for 13 places (ASEAN members, Timor-Leste, Suriname, Colombia, Hong Kong). The official portal is evisa.imigrasi.go.id — it must end in .go.id; agency lookalikes charge multiples.

Two 2026 must-knows: the free All Indonesia Arrival Card is mandatory for all travellers before landing, and Bali adds a one-off IDR 150,000 tourist levy. A proposed fee hike (e-VOA Rp750,000 / counter Rp1,000,000) has no effective date as of August 2026 — the current fee remains Rp500,000. This guide was verified in October 2026.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond arrival with blank pages. For the e-VOA, apply at least 48 hours to a week before departure; have crisp US-dollar cash ready if using the counter VOA (cards can fail, and damaged notes are refused).

Identify the correct visa or entry route

Check the three lanes: visa-exempt (13 places), VOA/e-VOA (~97 countries), or consular visa for the rest. The ~97-country VOA list covers most Western and Asian passports — confirm yours on the official portal.

Transit and onward travel

Indonesia's VOA works for transit-style entries too — but only if you clear immigration. Airside transit without entering needs no visa; any landside step (hotel, terminal change) needs the VOA/e-VOA or exemption. The proposed fee changes do not affect the transit logic.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Whatever Indonesia's official rules say on paper, the officer at the desk decides your admission. Carry a return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, and evidence you can fund the trip — bank statements or a card in your own name, not someone else's screenshots. Keep the Directorate General of Immigration e-Visa portal approval or visa printout accessible offline; airport Wi-Fi is not a document strategy. Visiting friends or family? Their full address and a contact number answer the two most common questions before they're asked.

Answer questions directly and consistently with your application. If anything changed since you applied — new passport, different flight, longer stay — say so upfront rather than letting the officer discover the mismatch.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

No vaccination certificate is a standard Indonesian entry requirement for ordinary tourists. Complete the All Indonesia Arrival Card before landing — it is free and mandatory.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Travel medical insurance is not an Indonesian VOA condition, but it is strongly recommended. No official student-insurance figure is published.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

VOA is 30 days, extendable once for 30 more days (the extension must be processed before expiry — don't leave it to the last day). VEA (visa-exempt) entries are 30 days, non-extendable — choosing VEA when you need 60 days is a classic, expensive mistake. Bali arrivals also pay the IDR 150,000 tourist levy.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work runs on the KITAS system (employer-sponsored, with the US$100/month DPKK skills-development levy per foreign worker) and students on the VITAS C-316 → ITAS conversion — but current official PNBP fee figures were not verified for 2026, so confirm the fee schedule with the sponsor/employer before budgeting. A VOA/VEA entry never authorises work or study; working on one risks detention and deportation.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every child needs their own passport and their own Indonesia permission — there is no family visa or shared authorization. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter from the non-travelling parent(s); it is not always a statutory requirement, but officers routinely ask, and a missing letter can delay or derail entry.

Dual nationals should enter and leave on the same passport, and check whether Indonesia restricts dual nationality — some countries treat entry on a foreign passport as a declaration with consequences. Travellers on refugee travel documents or laissez-passers face different rules everywhere: verify with the mission, not with forums.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

VOA route: 1) Confirm your nationality is on the ~97-country list at evisa.imigrasi.go.id. 2) Either pre-apply the e-VOA online (at least 48h–1 week ahead) or pay IDR 500,000 at the counter in crisp USD cash (+US$1 overtime surcharge outside office hours). 3) Complete the free All Indonesia Arrival Card before landing. 4) Bali: pay the IDR 150,000 levy. 5) Extend once if needed — before expiry. Work/study route: sponsor-led KITAS/VITAS process; verify the 2026 fee schedule first.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing VEA for a 45-day trip. Visa-exempt entry is non-extendable — take the VOA if you need the extension.
  • Using lookalike e-VOA sites. The official portal ends in .go.id — agencies charge multiples.
  • Budgeting the proposed hike. Rp750,000/Rp1,000,000 has no effective date (Aug 2026) — current fee is Rp500,000.
  • Skipping the All Indonesia Arrival Card. Free but mandatory before landing.
  • Forgetting Bali's levy. IDR 150,000 one-off, separate from the visa.
  • Working on a VOA. Detention and deportation territory — KITAS only.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Caseworkers and officers are testing two things: will you leave, and can you afford the trip. The strongest files show stable, genuine funds over several months — a single large unexplained deposit reads as funds parking and hurts more than it helps. Tie the itinerary to the money: the trip you're describing should be one your documented income plausibly supports.

Show a reason to return — employment with approved leave, enrolment, family, property — and keep the story consistent across every document. If you've been refused before, address the refusal reasons directly with new evidence; resubmitting the same file invites the same answer.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

The evisa.imigrasi.go.id portal is the only official online channel — and the domain must end in .go.id. The free All Indonesia Arrival Card is filed online before landing. Agency lookalikes with similar names charge multiples of the IDR 500,000 government fee.

Translations, certifications and document format

Any document not in Indonesia's accepted language(s) needs a proper translation — check the mission or the Directorate General of Immigration e-Visa portal for whether certified translation is required, and by whom. Bank statements should be official downloads or stamped originals, not screenshots; employment letters should be on letterhead with a verifiable contact.

Submit scans well before any appointment so there's time to fix rejections. Keep originals for the appointment itself, and keep a full copy of everything you submitted — if the file is queried months later, you'll need to know exactly what the officer saw.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Where Indonesia requires in-person biometrics — fingerprints and a digital photograph — book the appointment early; centres fill up in peak seasons and the decision clock usually starts at the appointment, not the application. The photograph is captured there, so check whether a separate photo upload is even needed before paying for passport photos.

Interviews are the exception rather than the rule for short stays, but they happen where credibility needs testing. Answer from your actual plans, not from what you think the officer wants to hear — memorised scripts are easy to spot and hard to recover from.

Families and group travel

Each traveller — including every child — needs their own permission; there is no family authorization that covers a group. Apply in the same window so decisions land together, but understand each application is decided on its own merits: one refusal doesn't cancel the others, though it should prompt the rest of the group to re-check their own files.

For organised groups, keep a shared folder with every member's approval, the full itinerary, and the group leader's contact details. If one member is pulled aside at the border, the group should know where the documents are — not just that they exist somewhere in someone's email.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Bring crisp US-dollar notes for the counter VOA — damaged bills are refused. Declare large cash sums as required. Keep the VOA receipt, arrival card confirmation and Bali levy proof with your passport.

Departure and future travel

VOA extends once (30 + 30) — process the extension before the first 30 days expire. VEA cannot be extended at all. Overstaying draws daily fines and can mean detention; the extension system works if used in time.

How to research a route without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) evisa.imigrasi.go.id — the portal's own fee and country-list pages (checked October 2026). Treat the proposed fee hike as what it is — proposed, no date — and distrust any 'Indonesia e-Visa' domain not ending in .go.id.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid per Indonesia's rule, with blank pages, matching the application exactly
  • Visa / e-Visa / authorization approval saved offline and printed — verify it's linked to the current passport
  • Return or onward ticket and accommodation details accessible without internet
  • Proof of funds and ties-to-home evidence packed
  • Travel insurance documents with emergency numbers
  • Any required arrival forms (health, customs, digital entry cards) completed in advance
  • Certified translations for documents not in the accepted language
  • Emergency contacts: your embassy, your insurer, and someone at home who has your itinerary
  • Previous refusals or complications declared honestly wherever asked

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Indonesia visa on arrival?IDR 500,000 for both e-VOA and counter VOA (2026) — 30 days, extendable once. A proposed rise to Rp750,000/Rp1,000,000 has no effective date as of August 2026.
What is the All Indonesia Arrival Card?A free, mandatory digital arrival form all travellers complete before landing (allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id).
Do I pay the Bali levy on top of the visa?Yes — Bali's IDR 150,000 one-off tourist levy is separate from the IDR 500,000 VOA.
Can I extend the visa-free entry?No — the 30-day visa-exempt (VEA) entry is non-extendable. If you need up to 60 days, take the VOA (extendable once) instead.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Indonesia is a choose-the-right-lane destination: VEA for short fixed trips (free, non-extendable), VOA at IDR 500,000 when you might need 60 days, the official .go.id portal only, the free arrival card filed, and Bali's IDR 150,000 levy on top. Verify the country list and fees on evisa.imigrasi.go.id — and ignore the proposed hike until it has a date.
