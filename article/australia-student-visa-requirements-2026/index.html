How to use this Australia student visa guide

Study in Australia beyond 3 months needs the Student visa (subclass 500). The controlling source is the Home Affairs student-500 page, verified October 2026. The 1 July 2026 fee round lifted the charge to from AUD $2,500 — the fourth increase in three years.

Who needs the Student visa (subclass 500)

The 500 covers full-time study — universities, vocational and English-language courses. Study beyond 3 months needs the student visa; the stay is generally aligned to enrolment, up to six years.

The requirements at a glance

  • An offer of enrolment from an eligible Australian institution
  • Financial evidence — AUD $29,710 a year in living costs for the primary applicant, plus tuition and travel
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the entire stay, from an authorised provider
  • English language evidence, if requested
  • Proof the course and institution are eligible
  • Application charge from AUD $2,500 (1 July 2026)

Step 1: secure an offer from an eligible institution

Everything starts with an offer. After receiving one, review the Home Affairs student-500 page — authorities may ask for proof that the course and institution are eligible. Keep the visa timeline separate from the university timeline.

Step 2: financial evidence

Home Affairs requires proof of living costs of AUD $29,710 per year for the primary applicant, plus tuition and travel — in force since 10 May 2024. Consistent savings over months reads as genuine.

Step 3: OSHC health cover

OSHC is mandatory for the entire stay on a Student visa, from a government-authorised provider. Single cover runs roughly AUD $630–$800 per year on 2026 comparisons: ahm about $631, nib about $632, Allianz Care about $665, Bupa about $698. Couples and family cover costs several thousand dollars a year.

Step 4: English language evidence

Authorities may ask for language evidence. Note the trap: a university may accept TOEFL or PTE while an immigration route requires a specific approved test version. Confirm the exact accepted test with both the institution and Home Affairs before booking.

Step 5: lodge and wait

Applications are lodged online through ImmiAccount, with the from-AUD $2,500 charge paid at lodgement. The grant is electronic — verify it in VEVO before departure. Home Affairs’ August 2026 publication puts Student visas at a 22-day median; lodge well ahead of the course start.

Work rights on the Student 500

Students can generally work up to 48 hours a fortnight while the course is in session, with different treatment for research students. A visitor grant carries condition 8101 — no work at all.

Minors and family members

Every traveller needs their own authorisation — children need their own passport and visa.

A reliable application workflow

1) Confirm the offer and eligible course/institution. 2) Assemble financial evidence (AUD $29,710 a year + tuition + travel). 3) Arrange OSHC for the full stay. 4) Lodge through ImmiAccount and pay from AUD $2,500. 5) Verify the grant in VEVO before flying.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Treating the university offer as the visa — admissions and immigration are separate decisions.
  • Quoting pre-July-2026 fees — the charge is from AUD $2,500.
  • Sitting the wrong English test: the university’s accepted list and the immigration route’s test version can differ.
  • Booking non-refundable flights before the grant arrives.
  • Renewing the passport after the grant — the electronic visa is linked to the old number.

Building a stronger student file

A strong file answers two questions: are the funds real, and is the study plan genuine? Consistent savings over months and a course that follows your history strengthen the file.

Fees and processing time

Home Affairs’ August 2026 publication puts Student visas at 22 days median. Lodge as early as the intake allows.

Apply through official channels

Lodge through ImmiAccount and check the grant in VEVO — the only official channels. No one can expedite a Home Affairs decision for a fee.

Documents and translations

Typical documents: passport biodata scan, financial evidence, OSHC policy, English test results, enrolment evidence. Non-English documents need certified English translations — NAATI-accredited translators are the standard.

Interviews and biometrics

Student applicants are not interviewed as a matter of course; biometrics are collected only if requested.

Family members

Partners and children apply as their own applicants — each needs a separate application and their own passport.

Know your grant conditions

Before flying, read the conditions on the grant in VEVO — especially the 48-hours-a-fortnight work limit. Breaching a condition can lead to cancellation.

After the course: the Temporary Graduate visa

Graduates may have a post-study option: the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), from AUD $5,750 since 1 July 2026 (was AUD $4,600).

How to research without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) the Home Affairs student-500 page for requirements and charges; 2) the monthly processing-times publication; 3) VEVO for your own grant. The 1 July 2026 fee round invalidated older guides — any page quoting AUD $2,000 for the 500 is out of date.

Document audit before lodging

  • Offer from an eligible institution confirmed
  • Financial evidence: AUD $29,710 a year living costs + tuition + travel
  • OSHC arranged for the entire stay, from an authorised provider
  • Fee of from AUD $2,500 budgeted and paid via ImmiAccount
  • Clear colour scans; certified English translations for non-English documents

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Australia student visa cost in 2026?

The Student visa (subclass 500) costs from AUD $2,500 for the main applicant since 1 July 2026 — the fourth increase in three years. Budget OSHC health cover on top (roughly AUD $630–$800 a year single) plus the funds you must prove.

How much money do I need to show for an Australian student visa?

Home Affairs requires proof of living costs of AUD $29,710 per year for the primary applicant, plus tuition and travel — in force since 10 May 2024. Consistent savings over months reads as genuine; a sudden lump deposit just before lodgement reads as risk.

How long does the Australian student visa take?

Home Affairs’ August 2026 publication puts Student visas at a 22-day median. That is an aggregate — individual cases vary — so lodge well ahead of the course start and monitor ImmiAccount for requests.

Can I work in Australia on a student visa?

Yes. Students can generally work up to 48 hours a fortnight while the course is in session, with different treatment for masters-by-research and doctoral students. A visitor visa never authorizes work — the 500 is the route that lawfully combines study with part-time work.

Do I need health insurance for an Australian student visa?

Yes. OSHC is mandatory for the entire stay on a Student visa, from a government-authorised provider. Single cover runs roughly AUD $630–$800 a year; couples and family cover costs several thousand.
