How this Ireland visa fee for Indian citizens 2026 guide works

Ireland sets short-stay visa fees in two bands — €60 single-entry, €100 multiple-entry — the same for every nationality in 2026. The fee is paid per the instructions on the Irish immigration application system when you submit. Processing is the slow part: the standard timeline is 4–8 weeks, which makes the fee the easy half of the planning.

What Indian citizens actually pay

Indian citizens apply online through the Irish immigration application system and submit supporting documents per the instructions for India. With standard processing at 4–8 weeks, the realistic move is to apply at least two to three months before travel — the €60 fee is fixed, but a rushed timeline isn't fixable. Choose single (€60) or multiple (€100) based on whether you'll re-enter Ireland within the visa's validity.

What the quoted fee does not include

The €60/€100 covers the application only. It does not include document translations, courier or application-centre service charges, or travel insurance for the trip. Decide single vs multiple entry before paying — the band is chosen at application.

Where and how to apply

Apply online through the Irish immigration application system, pay the fee per the official instructions, and submit your documents at the designated centre or embassy. Standard processing is 4–8 weeks; track the application and don't book non-refundable travel until the visa is in hand.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

Pay the visa fee only through the official instructions on the Irish immigration site — never to a personal account or via a social-media 'agent'. No one can shorten the 4–8 week standard processing for a side payment, and a refusal doesn't refund the fee, so submit a complete application.
