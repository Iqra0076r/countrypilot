Quick Answer

The UK Standard Visitor visa fee for Filipino citizens in 2026 is £135 for the 6-month visa (raised from £127 on 8 April 2026). Long-term options cost £506 (2 years), £903 (5 years) or £1,128 (10 years). Decisions outside the UK take around 3 weeks, and visit visas are now issued as digital eVisas linked to your passport.

How this UK visa fee for Filipino citizens 2026 guide works

The UK charges visa fees by visa type, not by nationality — every Standard Visitor applicant pays the same fee. You apply online on gov.uk, pay there, then give biometrics at a visa application centre. Since 2026, visit visas are issued as digital eVisas linked to your passport rather than vignette stickers. What Filipino citizens actually pay

Filipino citizens need a Standard Visitor visa before travel — apply online on gov.uk, pay the £135, then give biometrics at the application centre serving the Philippines. The 2026 service standard for decisions outside the UK is around 3 weeks.

What the quoted fee does not include

The £135 covers the application and decision only. It does not include optional priority processing upgrades (which cost extra and only speed the decision, never the outcome), courier or document-return fees, or the cost of translations if your supporting documents are not in English or Welsh.

Where and how to apply

Apply on gov.uk: complete the online form, pay the £135 fee, and book a biometrics appointment at the visa application centre serving your country. Decisions outside the UK take around 3 weeks under the 2026 service standard. If granted, your visit visa is issued as a digital eVisa linked to your passport — check the link before you travel, since there is no sticker to inspect.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

UKVI fees are paid only on gov.uk — never to an agent's personal account or by cash at an unofficial office. The eVisa itself is digital and free once the visa is granted; anyone charging a separate 'eVisa fee' is running a scam. No agent can guarantee a decision or a faster outcome than the service standard you paid for.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the UK visa fee for Filipino citizens in 2026?

The Standard Visitor visa (6 months) costs £135 — raised from £127 on 8 April 2026.

How long can I stay on a UK Standard Visitor visa?

Up to 6 months per visit, including on the 2, 5 and 10-year long-term versions.

How long does a UK visitor visa take in 2026?

Around 3 weeks for decisions made outside the UK (2026 service standard).

Are UK visit visas still stickers in the passport?

No — since 2026 they are issued as digital eVisas linked to your passport.

What happens to the fee if my UK visa is refused?

The fee covers processing of the application, so treat it as spent once paid — check the current gov.uk refund policy before you apply.

Bottom Line

Budget £135 for the 6-month Standard Visitor visa — or £506/£903/£1,128 if you travel often enough to justify a long-term visa. Apply on gov.uk at least a month before travel, expect about 3 weeks for the decision, and verify your digital eVisa is linked to your passport before you fly.
