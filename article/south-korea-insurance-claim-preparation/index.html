Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

Korea's government Study in Korea guidance states that D-2/D-4 students need the required Korean-language level and immigration permission before part-time work. Current government guidance describes weekly limits that vary by programme/proficiency, with a general part-time framework up to 30 hours for many undergraduate cases and higher allowances for some graduate/accredited cases. Always verify the individual permission before starting work.

Official page: https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/ko/work/aboutForeignerEmploymentSystem.do?tab=part-time

First determine whether cover is legally mandatory

Check https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/en/main.do and the institution. Some destinations require a specific student health product or comprehensive private insurance before a visa is granted; others enrol eligible residents into a public system after arrival. Read the policy beyond the headline limit

Compare hospitalisation, outpatient treatment, emergency care, prescription drugs, mental health, maternity, dental/optical, ambulance, repatriation and pre-existing-condition rules. A high overall limit can coexist with low sub-limits or exclusions. re-check Korea Visa Portal / Hi Korea / Ministry of Justice before relying on it.

Check the deductible/excess and whether treatment requires pre-authorisation. verify it against Korea Visa Portal / Hi Korea / Ministry of Justice before committing money or documents.

Waiting periods, exclusions and pre-existing conditions

Policies can exclude treatment related to a known condition, impose waiting periods, or limit routine care. How to make a claim

Keep the policy number, insurer contact details, medical reports, invoices, prescriptions and proof of payment. For non-emergency planned treatment, contact the insurer first and ask whether a network provider or authorisation is required. verify it against Korea Visa Portal / Hi Korea / Ministry of Justice before committing money or documents.

If a claim is denied, request the policy clause and appeal process in writing. use Korea Visa Portal / Hi Korea / Ministry of Justice to confirm the live requirement where it can change.

Travel insurance is not always student health insurance

Travel insurance is designed mainly for unexpected events during a trip. Long-term student health requirements can be broader and may need to cover routine or local medical care.
