The four parts of transfer cost

Compare: (1) sender fee, (2) exchange rate, (3) intermediary deductions, and (4) receiver fee. Providers often advertise only the first. The most useful comparison is the amount the recipient receives for the same amount sent. compare it with the current German federal immigration authorities / Make it in Germany guidance linked below.

Check whether the quoted exchange rate is locked and for how long. A small spread on a large tuition payment can cost more than a visible transfer fee. re-check German federal immigration authorities / Make it in Germany before relying on it.

Bank wire versus fintech transfer

A bank wire can be appropriate for large documented payments and institutions that require SWIFT details. Fintech providers can be faster or cheaper for common currency corridors. Neither is automatically better. Consider regulation, transfer limits, recipient method, refund process and support. re-check German federal immigration authorities / Make it in Germany before relying on it.

For tuition, verify whether the university has an approved payment partner; sending to the wrong account can be difficult to reverse. use German federal immigration authorities / Make it in Germany to confirm the live requirement where it can change.

Compliance and source-of-funds checks

Large or unusual transfers can trigger anti-money-laundering checks. Keep bank statements, salary records, scholarship letters, loan documents or sale agreements that explain the money. A compliance review is not necessarily an accusation; providers are required to understand certain transactions. verify it against German federal immigration authorities / Make it in Germany before committing money or documents.

Do not split a lawful transfer into many smaller transfers merely to avoid reporting or verification. compare it with the current German federal immigration authorities / Make it in Germany guidance linked below.

Speed, cut-off times and failed transfers

Quoted speed often begins after the provider receives cleared funds and passes verification. Weekends, public holidays, intermediary banks, name mismatches and compliance reviews can add time. Scam prevention

Treat urgent requests to change bank details as high risk. Confirm new beneficiary details by calling a known number. If a payment is induced by fraud, contact the sending bank/provider immediately; recovery becomes harder once funds move onward. Confirm the current position using German federal immigration authorities / Make it in Germany in the source list below.
