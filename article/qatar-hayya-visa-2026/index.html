What the Hayya visa is

Hayya is Qatar's official electronic entry platform, run with the Ministry of Interior at https://www.hayya.qa/. It is the controlling source for Qatar visitor entry — the only Hayya channel that matters, and the site to treat as authoritative over any third-party summary.

The Hayya route exists alongside a free visa-on-arrival programme for around 102 nationalities, plus visa-free entry for GCC citizens. Many travellers never need Hayya at all; those who do apply online before departure and pay the official fee through the portal.

Hayya visa types: A1, A2, A3, A4

Hayya entry is split into four types. Pick yours before you apply:

  • A1 — tourist e-Visa. The standard visitor route: 30-day stay, QAR 100 (2026). The route for travellers from nationalities like India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh.
  • A2 — GCC resident visa. For residents of Gulf Cooperation Council countries.
  • A3 — ETA (electronic travel authorization). For eligible travellers meeting the ETA conditions.
  • A4 — GCC companion visa. For companions of GCC nationals.

Who needs Hayya — and who skips it

Check your nationality against the free visa-on-arrival list first: around 102 nationalities — including the UK and the US — get a free visa on arrival with a 30-day stay, and GCC citizens enter visa-free. If you are on that list, you do not need Hayya.

You likely need the Hayya A1 tourist e-Visa if your nationality is outside the visa-on-arrival programme — travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh, among others, use the A1 route at QAR 100. Confirm your own nationality's requirement on hayya.qa: entry regimes are nationality-specific and third-party lists go stale. How to apply on hayya.qa

  • Use hayya.qa only. It is the official Hayya platform; every other "Hayya" domain is a private business.
  • Confirm your route — A1 tourist, A2 GCC resident, A3 ETA or A4 GCC companion — before filling the form.
  • Complete the application on the passport you will travel with.
  • Pay the official fee (QAR 100 for A1) through the portal's own payment channel — never to a personal account.
  • Save the approval offline and printed: verify the dates, entries and name spelling immediately after issuance.

Processing time and application timeline

No official processing time is published for the Hayya visa. That is the fact to plan around: apply as early as your plans allow and avoid non-refundable bookings until the visa is approved. Do not trust third-party "48-hour approval" promises as official policy — the portal publishes no SLA.

The practical timeline: 1) confirm your nationality's route, 2) apply at hayya.qa with the travel passport, 3) pay QAR 100 for the A1, 4) wait for the decision, 5) verify the approval details before booking non-refundable travel.

2026 gotchas

  • The 2026 Discover Qatar hotel-booking rule. Nationals of India, Pakistan, Iran, Thailand and Ukraine must book accommodation via Discover Qatar under the 2026 rules — factor this into the budget and the timeline before applying.
  • Extension is possible but costs. The tourist visa can be extended 30 days for another QAR 100 — do not assume an extension is automatic or free.
  • Visa on arrival is not universal. The ~102-nationality free VOA programme is generous, but nationality is everything — apply on Hayya rather than hoping at the gate.
  • Work on a visitor entry is never allowed. Work routes run through employer-sponsored permits (QAR 100/year private-sector work permit per the 2025 fee revision), never the A1.
  • Fees move by regulation. Ministerial Decision No. 32 of 2025 (effective end September 2025) revised labour fees — re-check the live fee schedule before paying.

Copycat sites and scam avoidance

Hayya applications live or die on the domain you choose. The official portal is hayya.qa; lookalike domains charging multiples of QAR 100 or promising "guaranteed approval" are running a business on your anxiety, not an official channel. No one can guarantee a visa decision or sell an earlier approval.

Never pay a visa fee to a personal bank account, mobile-money number or crypto wallet. Official fees go through the portal's designated channels only, and the application status should be trackable on the official system — not through an agent's screenshots.

Pre-travel Hayya checklist

  • Eligibility confirmed on hayya.qa for your nationality — or VOA confirmed instead
  • Correct Hayya type selected (A1/A2/A3/A4); application on the travel passport
  • QAR 100 paid through the official portal; receipts saved
  • Approval verified (dates, entries, name spelling); saved offline and printed
  • Discover Qatar hotel booking completed if required (India, Pakistan, Iran, Thailand, Ukraine)
  • Passport comfortably valid with a blank page
  • Return ticket, accommodation address and funds evidence ready for the border

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official Qatar Hayya website?

The Hayya platform at https://www.hayya.qa/, run with Qatar's Ministry of Interior. Any other domain offering "Hayya visas" is a private business, not the authority.

How much does the Qatar Hayya visa cost in 2026?

The A1 tourist e-Visa costs QAR 100 (~US$27) under the 2026 official schedule. Around 90 nationalities are fee-exempt, and the free visa-on-arrival programme covers roughly 102 nationalities.

What is the difference between Hayya A1, A2, A3 and A4?

A1 is the standard tourist e-Visa, A2 is for GCC residents, A3 is the electronic travel authorization (ETA), and A4 is for companions of GCC nationals.

How long does the Hayya visa take?

No official processing time is published. Apply as early as possible and avoid non-refundable bookings until the visa is approved.

Can I extend a Qatar tourist visa?

The tourist visa can be extended by 30 days for another QAR 100. Arrange it before the permitted stay expires — do not simply overstay.

What is the 2026 Discover Qatar rule?

Under the 2026 rules, nationals of India, Pakistan, Iran, Thailand and Ukraine must book accommodation via Discover Qatar for their visit. Check the current requirement on the official portal before applying.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

The Qatar Hayya visa in 2026 is a straightforward online application at hayya.qa: A1 tourist entry at QAR 100 for a 30-day stay, with A2, A3 and A4 routes for GCC residents, ETAs and GCC companions. Check the free visa-on-arrival list first — you may not need Hayya at all. Pay only through the official portal, allow plenty of time since no official processing SLA is published, and watch the Discover Qatar hotel-booking rule if you travel on one of the five affected nationalities.
