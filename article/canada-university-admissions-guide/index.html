Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

A Canadian study permit normally requires acceptance by a designated learning institution (DLI), evidence of sufficient funds, admissibility and proof that the applicant will respect the temporary nature of the permit. IRCC currently lists the study-permit fee at CAD 150; processing time varies by country and application circumstances.

Official page: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit.html

Understand the education system before choosing a university

Start with the qualification structure in Canada: degree length, academic calendar, credit system, taught/research distinction and whether the programme leads to professional registration. Designated Learning Institution (DLI) lists and provincial/university admissions are useful starting points. Rankings can help with broad reputation, but programme accreditation, curriculum, graduate outcomes and location often matter more.

For regulated fields, check the professional body before applying. A university may be recognised while a specific qualification still does not grant the professional outcome the student expects. Intakes and application timeline

Map the intake backwards. A practical sequence is: 10–14 months out, research programmes and funding; 8–12 months out, tests and documents; 6–10 months out, submit university and scholarship applications; after admission, arrange finances, housing and immigration. Do not assume every programme has both autumn and spring entry. Competitive programmes and scholarships may have one annual intake. Admission is not immigration permission

After receiving an offer, review study permit requirements on https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit.html. Immigration authorities may ask for financial evidence, health insurance, language evidence or proof that the course and institution are eligible.

Keep the university and visa timelines aligned. A late admission decision can compress the visa window; a visa delay can affect enrolment. Know the institution's latest-arrival and deferral policy.
