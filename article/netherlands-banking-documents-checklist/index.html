What you usually need to open an account

Banks commonly verify identity, residential address, tax residency and source of funds. International students may use a passport, immigration document, university letter and local address evidence, but each bank sets its own onboarding rules. Cards, payments and credit history

A debit card spends money already in the account; a credit card creates borrowing. New arrivals may have limited local credit history, so credit limits or products can be restricted. Build history slowly and pay attention to interest, annual fees and cash-advance charges. Security and fraud prevention

Never share one-time passwords, PINs or remote-access codes because someone claims to be from a bank or immigration office. Verify calls through the bank's official number.
