Does the country actually have a digital-nomad route?

South Korea has operated a workation/digital-nomad category; applicants should verify whether the current F-1-D framework is open and the exact income, insurance and employment-abroad conditions on the Visa Portal/Hi Korea before applying. Start with the official immigration portal and identify the legal name of the status. Private sites such as VisaGuide are useful for discovering programmes, but the government page controls whether the route is open and who qualifies.

If there is no dedicated route, do not relabel a tourist visa as a digital-nomad visa. Visitor status may restrict work even when the employer is abroad. the live Korea Visa Portal / Hi Korea / Ministry of Justice reference below should be consulted before action.

Tax is separate from immigration

A visa that permits remote work does not automatically settle tax residence, social-security, permanent-establishment or employer-payroll questions. Days of presence, treaty rules and the location of employer/business activities can matter. the live Korea Visa Portal / Hi Korea / Ministry of Justice reference below should be consulted before action.

Dependants, renewal and local work

Check whether spouse/children can accompany, whether they can work or study, and whether extra income/insurance is required. Also verify maximum stay, renewal, path to longer residence and whether local clients/employers are prohibited. re-check Korea Visa Portal / Hi Korea / Ministry of Justice before relying on it.
