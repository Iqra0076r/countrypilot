Australia Visitor Visa for Pakistani Citizens 2026: 600 Requirements and Fee

Pakistani passport holders need the subclass 600 Visitor visa for every trip to Australia — no ETA route. Here is the 2026 fee, process, and timeline.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20266 min read

Pakistan is not ETA-eligible, so Pakistani citizens need the Visitor visa (subclass 600) for every trip to Australia — tourism, family visits, short business. The Tourist stream starts at AUD $250 since the 1 July 2026 increase, and decisions are fast. This guide covers the 2026 requirements for Pakistani applicants.

Quick Answer

Pakistani citizens need the subclass 600 Visitor visa, Tourist stream: from AUD $250 (since 1 July 2026, up from AUD $200), applied for online, usually allowing 3 months per stay (up to 12 months in some cases) with multiple entries possible. Home Affairs' August 2026 headline for visitor visas was a decision in under 1 day.

Fee from AUD $250 · Stay usually 3 months/visit · Decision under 1 day (Aug 2026) · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 6 minutes

Why Pakistanis need the 600 — not the ETA

The ETA (subclass 601) — AUD $20 in the official app — is for eligible passports only (the UK, the US, and Hong Kong among them). Pakistan is not on the list, so the ETA is irrelevant to Pakistani travellers: any "ETA" bought from a third-party site is money wasted. The 600 is the one and only door — applied for online through the official Home Affairs channels.

The fee after 1 July 2026

The Tourist stream costs from AUD $250 since the 1 July 2026 increase of roughly 25% across nearly all visa categories (up from AUD $200). The "from" matters: the Sponsored Family and ADS streams price differently. The charge pays for processing — not the visa — and is not refunded on refusal. Any guide quoting AUD $200 without a post-July date is out of date.

What the file must show

The 600 is decided on genuine purpose, sufficient funds, and intention to return: a clear itinerary, funds covering the trip, employment or business in Pakistan, family ties, and a travel history of compliance. One consistent story must run through the form and every supporting document. Run the documents through our visa document checklist before lodging, and total the funds honestly with our proof-of-funds calculator.

Stay rules and decision speed

When granted, the Tourist stream usually allows 3 months per stay — up to 12 months in some cases — with multiple entries possible; the exact stay period and entries are set on the grant. And the August 2026 Home Affairs headline put visitor decisions (600, 601, 651 combined) at under 1 day — among the fastest in the world. Apply ahead of fixed dates anyway: the headline describes the system, not your file.

Applying correctly

Apply through the official Home Affairs online channels only, and pay the published charge — third-party "visa lodgement" sites add markups to a process the government runs directly. The grant is linked to the passport: travel on the passport the grant is attached to, and check the stay period and entries on the grant letter rather than assuming them.
