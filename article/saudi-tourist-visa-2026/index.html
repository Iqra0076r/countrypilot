How to use this Saudi tourist visa guide

Saudi Arabia offers tourists several entry routes, and the right one depends on nationality. This guide covers the tourist eVisa, visa on arrival, the transit/stopover visa and the Package Visa pilot. Start at Ministry of Foreign Affairs (KSA Visa) (https://ksavisa.sa/), the controlling source.

Passport validity and document condition

Most destinations require a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your stay, with a blank page — airlines enforce this at boarding. Confirm the exact rule on the official portal or via Timatic; emergency passports may face extra scrutiny.

Identify the correct tourist entry route

Routes, lightest to heaviest: 1) Tourist eVisa (about 66 nationalities via ksavisa.sa; 1-year multiple-entry, 90 days total stay). 2) Visa on arrival (eligible nationalities incl. US/UK/Schengen visa holders, on Saudi carriers). 3) Transit/stopover visa. 4) Package Visa pilot (from 6 Jul 2026; 7 countries; visa SAR 402.21 with approved-agency package).

Transit and the stopover visa

Transit is its own immigration question, separate from visiting. Whether you need a transit visa depends on nationality, airport, and whether you leave the transit area. A transit/stopover visa can add a short stay to a flight itinerary. Check your itinerary on the official portal or via Timatic before booking.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

A visa lets you travel to the border; the officer decides entry. Carry your passport with the visa, a return or onward ticket, your accommodation address, proof of funds, and ties-to-home evidence such as an employment letter. Answer directly and consistently.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Health entry rules usually follow where you have recently been — yellow-fever certificates are the classic example for arrivals from endemic countries. Check current requirements on the official portal close to departure.

Insurance as part of the tourist visa

Insurance is part of the visitor fee stack: a $10.50 insurance digital services line item plus the policy itself, which is provider-based (no fixed 2026 figure is published — collect quotes from two or three insurers). Travelling without medical cover is a serious financial risk.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

The officer sets your permitted stay at entry — visa validity and admitted stay are different things. The eVisa allows 90 days total stay; each admission is decided separately. Overstaying can void the visa.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work needs an employer-sponsored permit — never work on a tourist entry, even remotely for a foreign employer. Study needs a student visa and enrollment at a recognized institution. No fixed proof-of-funds minimum could be verified for 2026.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Each child needs their own passport and application — there is no family visa or family e-Visa. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter plus the birth certificate. Dual nationals should check which passport to use at entry.

A reliable application workflow

1) Confirm your route above. 2) Apply at https://ksavisa.sa/. 3) Budget the official fee (USD $80 visa fee + $10.50 digital visa services + $10.50 insurance digital services + insurance (provider-based) (2026), or SAR 402.21 for the pilot) and pay through official channels. 4) Allow the full processing window (1 minute to 3 business days for the eVisa). 5) Gather documents, originals plus copies. 6) Verify visa details after approval.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Trusting third-party ‘official’ visa sites that add markups or harvest data.
  • Reading the 1-year visa validity as a 1-year stay — the eVisa allows 90 days total stay.
  • Assuming a friend’s passport means the same rules for you — regimes differ sharply by nationality.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the visa is issued.

Building a stronger tourist visa file

Most refusals come down to two doubts: will the applicant return home, and can they fund the trip? Answer both with a leave-approval letter, property or lease documents, family ties, prior compliant travel, and bank statements. Dated plans with bookings read as genuine visits.

Published processing: 1 minute to 3 business days (eVisa) — a target, not a guarantee. Apply well ahead of travel.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Start every step at https://ksavisa.sa/ — phishing sites copy government portals closely. No one can guarantee approval or sell earlier appointments. Never pay a ‘visa fee’ to a personal account or crypto wallet; track your case on the official system.

Translations, certifications and document format

Bring originals plus one copy: passport, specification photos, 3–6 months of bank statements, an employment letter with leave dates, and the itinerary. Non-English documents need a translation with a signed accuracy statement.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Missions may interview applicants and capture fingerprints and a digital photograph. Answer questions on the trip briefly and truthfully, matching the form.

Families and group travel

Every traveller needs their own authorization — each family member requires a separate application, including infants. There is no family e-Visa.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Cash above a threshold (commonly USD 10,000 equivalent) must be declared — it is not taxed, but undeclared cash can be seized. Declare all food, plants and animal products. Carry medications in original packaging with prescriptions.

Departure and future travel

Keep every entry record — clean histories strengthen future applications. Seek extensions before the permitted stay expires. A valid visa in an expired passport often remains usable with both passports — confirm this first.

How to research a route without getting misled

Hierarchy: 1) the official portal (https://ksavisa.sa/). 2) The embassy site for local procedures. 3) Timatic for boarding requirements.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid well beyond the stay, with a blank page
  • Visa/e-Visa approved and verified before departure
  • Return/onward ticket and accommodation address available offline
  • Funds and ties-to-home evidence packed
  • Insurance policy with adequate medical coverage
  • Visa details checked (dates, entries, name spelling) immediately after issuance

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a tourist visa for Saudi Arabia?

Most travellers do. The main 2026 routes are the tourist eVisa (about 66 nationalities via ksavisa.sa), visa on arrival for eligible nationalities, the transit/stopover visa, and the Package Visa pilot. Confirm your nationality’s route on the official portal close to departure.

How long can I stay on the Saudi tourist eVisa?

The eVisa is 1-year multiple-entry with 90 days total stay. Validity and admitted stay differ — the immigration officer sets your stay at each entry.

How fast is the Saudi tourist eVisa processed?

The published figure is 1 minute to 3 business days (2026) — a planning guide, not a guarantee. Apply well ahead of non-refundable bookings.

Can I get a visa on arrival in Saudi Arabia?

Visa on arrival is available for eligible nationalities, including US/UK/Schengen visa holders travelling on Saudi carriers. If you are not in the eligible set, apply for the tourist eVisa via ksavisa.sa before travel.

How much does the Saudi tourist visa cost?

The verified 2026 fee is USD $80 visa fee + $10.50 digital visa services + $10.50 insurance digital services + insurance (provider-based) (2026). The Package Visa pilot charges a separate SAR 402.21 including insurance, bundled with an approved-agency package (minimum SAR 4,000/adult for the first 2 days).
