Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

France-Visas states that long-stay student-visa applications submitted from 1 August 2026 must show resources of at least €877.50 per month. France-Visas also uses a five-stage application flow. Applicants should still check whether the Études en France/Campus France procedure applies in their country and which consular service receives the file.

Official page: https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/

Understand the education system before choosing a university

Start with the qualification structure in France: degree length, academic calendar, credit system, taught/research distinction and whether the programme leads to professional registration. Études en France/Campus France where applicable and universities are useful starting points. Rankings can help with broad reputation, but programme accreditation, curriculum, graduate outcomes and location often matter more.

For regulated fields, check the professional body before applying. A university may be recognised while a specific qualification still does not grant the professional outcome the student expects. Intakes and application timeline

Map the intake backwards. A practical sequence is: 10–14 months out, research programmes and funding; 8–12 months out, tests and documents; 6–10 months out, submit university and scholarship applications; after admission, arrange finances, housing and immigration. Admission is not immigration permission

After receiving an offer, review long-stay student visa/residence formalities requirements on https://www.france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/student. Immigration authorities may ask for financial evidence, health insurance, language evidence or proof that the course and institution are eligible.

Keep the university and visa timelines aligned. A late admission decision can compress the visa window; a visa delay can affect enrolment. Know the institution's latest-arrival and deferral policy. For France International Student Checklist, connect this point to the “Admission is not immigration permission” decision and re-check EU Immigration Portal before relying on it.

After admission: the practical handover

Confirm acceptance, deposits, accommodation, visa documents, travel dates, orientation and registration. Keep digital and paper copies of key documents in separate places. Before departure, understand work rights, health-care access and any address-registration requirement. These post-arrival rules can be as important as the visa itself.
