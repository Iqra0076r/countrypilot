Australia ETA vs Visitor Visa 2026: The $20 App Route Explained

Australia's ETA costs $20 and takes minutes — if your passport qualifies. If it does not, you need the $250 visitor visa. Here is how to tell which lane you are in.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20266 min read

Australia runs two completely different doors for short visits: the ETA (subclass 601) — an AUD $20 app-based authorisation for eligible passports — and the Visitor visa (subclass 600) from AUD $250 for everyone else. Choosing the wrong door wastes money, time, or both. This guide maps the two lanes for 2026.

Quick Answer

The ETA (subclass 601) costs AUD $20 as a service charge, applied for in the Australian ETA app: 12-month validity, up to 3 months per visit. It is available to eligible passports — the UK and the US among them. Nationals of India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, and Bangladesh cannot use the ETA and need the subclass 600 Visitor visa from AUD $250 instead.

ETA AUD $20 · Validity 12 months · Stay 3 months/visit · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 6 minutes

The ETA lane: $20 in the app

The ETA is applied for through the Australian ETA app — the official channel — with an AUD $20 service charge and no other government fee. Once granted, it is valid for 12 months with stays of up to 3 months per visit. Eligible nationalities include the United Kingdom and the United States (and Hong Kong among others). For these travellers the ETA is the whole process: no interview, no paper form, no consulate.

The visitor-visa lane: subclass 600 from $250

If your passport is not ETA-eligible — India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, Bangladesh among them — the route is the Visitor visa (subclass 600), applied for online, costing from AUD $250 for the Tourist stream since the 1 July 2026 increase. It usually allows 3 months per stay (up to 12 months in some cases) with multiple entries possible. Full detail in our Australia tourist visa fee guide.

Which lane are you in?

The test is nationality, nothing else: your passport decides the lane, not where you live, how often you travel, or how much you earn. Check the eligibility list on homeaffairs.gov.au before paying anything. An ineligible traveller buying an "ETA" from a third-party site has bought nothing usable — and a needless 600 application from an ETA-eligible traveller is $230 overspent.

The markup trap

The ETA's simplicity attracts intermediaries: sites charging far more than AUD $20 for "ETA processing" are markup intermediaries, not government channels — the subclass 601 costs $20 in the official app, period. The same warning applies to the 600: the official charge is the charge. Wherever a site's price exceeds the government's published figure by multiples, close the tab and use the official channel.

After the grant

Both the ETA and the 600 are permission to travel to Australia, linked to the passport — carry the passport the grant is attached to, and check its validity covers the trip. The grant letter states your stay period and entries; read it, because "12-month validity" (the ETA's life) and "3 months per visit" (each stay's length) are different numbers that travellers routinely confuse.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Australia ETA in 2026?

AUD $20 service charge via the official Australian ETA app — no other government fee.

Can Indians or Pakistanis get the Australia ETA?

No. They need the subclass 600 Visitor visa from AUD $250; the ETA is for eligible passports only.

How long is the ETA valid?

12 months, with stays of up to 3 months per visit.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

$20 in the official app for eligible passports, $250 online for everyone else: the ETA question is answered by your nationality alone — check eligibility first, pay the government's price, and ignore every intermediary selling the process back at a markup.
