Check what “online” means

Some programmes are fully asynchronous; others require live attendance, exams at test centres, placements, laboratories or short campus residencies. Time-zone and residency requirements can make a nominally online course impractical. use Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) to confirm the live requirement where it can change.

Ask for the assessment method and attendance policy before enrolling. re-check Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) before relying on it.

Employer and immigration recognition

If the qualification will be used for migration, credential assessment or a regulated job, check that purpose specifically. An immigration authority or credential evaluator may apply rules different from an employer. the live Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) reference below should be consulted before action.

Do not assume a popular certificate carries the same weight as an accredited degree. re-check Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) before relying on it.

Cost and return on investment

Include tuition, exam fees, proctoring, software, textbooks, travel for residencies and time away from work. Compare the programme to lower-cost alternatives and ask whether it creates a credential, portfolio, licence or measurable skill that the target employer values. verify it against Finnish Immigration Service (Migri) before committing money or documents.

How to shortlist

Compare curriculum depth, faculty, student support, career services, completion rates where published, alumni outcomes, accreditation and refund policy.
