Australia Tourist Visa Fee 2026: Subclass 600 Cost After the July Rise

Australia raised nearly all visa fees on 1 July 2026 — and the tourist visa was not spared. Here is the current subclass 600 price and what it buys.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20265 min read

Australia increased visa charges by roughly 25% across nearly all categories on 1 July 2026 — which means every older guide quoting AUD $200 for the visitor visa is out of date. The Tourist stream of the subclass 600 now starts higher. This guide states the current 2026 fee and the rules around it.

Quick Answer

The Visitor visa (subclass 600), Tourist stream costs from AUD $250 since the 1 July 2026 fee increase (up from AUD $200). It is applied for online, usually allows 3 months per stay (up to 12 months in some cases), and can carry multiple entries. Home Affairs' August 2026 processing headline for visitor visas was under 1 day.

What changed on 1 July 2026

The 1 July 2026 increase lifted charges by about 25% across nearly all visa categories. For the subclass 600 Tourist stream that moved the base charge from AUD $200 to AUD $250 — and "from" matters, because the Sponsored Family and ADS streams price differently. The practical rule: any Australian visa figure quoted without a post-July-2026 date is suspect. The visa listing pages on homeaffairs.gov.au carry the current charge; check the live page before paying.

Who needs the subclass 600

Nationals of India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines, and Bangladesh — among others — need the subclass 600 for tourism, because they cannot use the ETA. (The UK, the US, and Hong Kong among others use the ETA subclass 601 instead: AUD $20 via the app.) The 600 is applied for online, and the Tourist stream is the lane for holidays, family visits, and short non-work trips.

What the $250 buys

The charge pays for processing the application — assessment, checks, and the decision — not for the visa itself, and it is not refunded on refusal. When granted, the Tourist stream usually allows 3 months per stay, with up to 12 months in some cases and multiple entries possible. The exact stay period and entries are set on the grant, not chosen at payment.

How fast the decision comes

Home Affairs publishes monthly processing-time headlines, and the August 2026 figure for visitor visas (600, 601, and 651 combined) was under 1 day — among the fastest visitor decisions in the world. That headline describes straightforward online applications; files needing further checks take longer. It is still worth applying ahead of fixed travel dates rather than relying on the headline.

Applying correctly

Apply through the official Home Affairs online channels only. A persistent trap: third-party sites charging far more for "ETA processing" or "visa lodgement assistance" — the subclass 601 ETA costs AUD $20 in the official app, and any site charging far more for it is a markup intermediary, not a government channel. The same discipline applies to the 600: the official charge is the charge; everything above it is someone else's margin.
