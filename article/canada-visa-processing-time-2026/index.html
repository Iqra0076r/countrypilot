Canada Visa Processing Times 2026

IRCC service standards for 2026

IRCC publishes service standards for complete applications filed outside Canada: visitor visa 14 days, study permit 60 days, work permit 60 days (2026), targeted at 80% of complete applications. Work permits filed inside Canada carry a longer standard of 120 days.

Treat these as targets: they describe what IRCC aims to hit for complete files. The weekly actuals run longer at busy posts — for the week of 22 September 2026, visitor visas for India averaged around 27 days, and study permits ran 5 weeks (India), 7 weeks (Pakistan) and 10 weeks (Nigeria). Always check IRCC's live 'check processing times' tool before planning around a date.

Processing time by applicant country

IRCC's processing data is post- and country-specific. The clearest recent signals: visitor visas for India averaged around 27 days in late September 2026 — roughly double the 14-day standard. Study-permit actuals ran 5 weeks for India, 7 weeks for Pakistan and 10 weeks for Nigeria. British citizens and other visa-exempt nationals skip this queue entirely with an eTA (CAD $7 on canada.ca, usually approved within minutes), while nationals of visa-required countries — including India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh — should apply 3–4 months before travel, since VAC biometrics appointments add their own lead time in peak season.

eTA timing

The eTA is the fastest route into the timeline conversation: apply on canada.ca (CAD $7, 2026) with the passport you will travel on, and most approvals arrive within minutes, though some take days. Do not fly until approved — carriers verify eTA status before boarding. Renew the passport and you must apply for a new eTA (CAD $7), since it is linked to the passport document.

Work permits: inside vs outside Canada

The location of the application changes the standard. A work permit filed outside Canada carries a 60-day standard (2026); filed inside Canada, the standard is 120 days. The fee stack for that work permit is CAD $155 per person plus a CAD $100 open-work-permit holder fee — see our Canada visa fee 2026 guide for the full fee table.

The biometrics lead time

The clock on your application is not the only clock running: biometrics appointments at VACs add their own lead time in peak season. Most applicants aged 14–79 give biometrics (CAD $85 individual / CAD $170 family maximum), and the appointment must be booked and attended before the file is complete. In busy periods, the gap between paying the fee and sitting for biometrics can be the longest part of the process — book the appointment the moment it is available.

Planning your timeline backwards from travel

For visitor visas from high-demand countries, apply 3–4 months before travel. Work the workflow in order: 1) Create an IRCC secure account (GCKey or sign-in partner). 2) Complete the forms (IMM 5257 and family information for visitors). 3) Pay the fee — CAD $100 for the visitor visa (family cap CAD $500 for 5+). 4) Book biometrics at a Visa Application Centre (CAD $85 individual / CAD $170 family maximum). 5) Track the application and check IRCC's processing-times tool. 6) Submit the passport only when IRCC requests it — the counterfoil is placed inside.

For study permits, budget the financial test ahead of time: permit CAD $150 plus proof of funds of CAD $23,448 per year (single applicant outside Quebec, from 1 September 2026), plus first-year tuition and travel. Why published times keep moving

Three forces move the numbers: weekly actuals (which shift with post workloads), file completeness (the standards describe complete applications), and VAC capacity in peak season. Control what you can: submit a complete file — the Canada visa document checklist keeps it that way — and book biometrics early. For the rest, check the live tool close to application and again before booking non-refundable travel.

Common timeline mistakes to avoid

  • Planning on the 14-day standard alone — India visitor actuals ran ~27 days in late September 2026.
  • Ignoring the VAC lead time. Biometrics appointments stretch in peak season and the file is not complete without them.
  • Booking non-refundable travel before the visa is in hand — especially from high-demand countries where TRVs need 3–4 months.
  • Submitting the passport early. Submit it only when IRCC requests it; the counterfoil goes in then.
  • Treating one week's actuals as a forecast. They move weekly — re-check the live tool before committing to dates.
