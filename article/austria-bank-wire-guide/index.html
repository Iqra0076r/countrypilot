Bank wire versus fintech transfer

A bank wire can be appropriate for large documented payments and institutions that require SWIFT details. Fintech providers can be faster or cheaper for common currency corridors. Neither is automatically better. Consider regulation, transfer limits, recipient method, refund process and support. Scam prevention
