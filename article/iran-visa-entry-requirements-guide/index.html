How to use this Iran visa and entry guide

Iran's system is an e-Visa plus visa-on-arrival hybrid: the e-Visa via evisa.mfa.ir (the MFA states it is the only official visa-issuance website) covers 180+ nationalities, and a visa on arrival of up to 30 days (extendable twice, to 90 days) is then issued at international airports. But nine nationalities — Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Canada, Colombia, Jordan, Pakistan, Somalia, the UK and the US — are ineligible for the e-Visa and must obtain visas in advance at an embassy.

Fees are reciprocity-based (EUR 20–150 by nationality) — there is no single universal fee, so distrust any site quoting one. And since 22 November 2025, Iran suspended visa-free entry for Indian nationals — Indians need a visa before travel as of 2026. This guide was verified in October 2026.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond arrival with blank pages. Note: Israeli stamps/visas in the passport can cause refusal — check current policy for your travel history before applying.

Identify the correct visa or entry route

First check the ineligibility list: nine nationalities (including the UK, US, Pakistan and Bangladesh) cannot use the e-Visa at all. Everyone else chooses between the e-Visa (before travel) and the airport VOA.

Transit and onward travel

Iran's VOA works for transit-style entries at international airports, but the e-Visa authorization code (3–10 days) is the safer instrument for tight itineraries — the airport VOA takes 1–3 hours and is not guaranteed during disruptions. Ineligible nationalities must hold the consular visa regardless of transit purpose.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Whatever Iran's official rules say on paper, the officer at the desk decides your admission. Carry a return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, and evidence you can fund the trip — bank statements or a card in your own name, not someone else's screenshots. Keep the MFA e-Visa system approval or visa printout accessible offline; airport Wi-Fi is not a document strategy. Visiting friends or family? Their full address and a contact number answer the two most common questions before they're asked.

Answer questions directly and consistently with your application. If anything changed since you applied — new passport, different flight, longer stay — say so upfront rather than letting the officer discover the mismatch.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

No vaccination certificate is a standard Iranian entry requirement for ordinary tourists. Travel health insurance has been mandatory for visa applicants since May 2024 (Iran Insurance Company policy issued with the visa) — but no verified annual student cost figure exists.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Travel health insurance is mandatory with the visa application (May 2024 rule) — the policy is issued alongside the visa. No verified annual international-student cost figure was found; do not budget from third-party tables.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

VOA is issued at international airports for up to 30 days, extendable twice for 30 days each (90 days total). Have the e-Visa authorization code or the VOA fee ready — VOA costs more than embassy collection (e.g. Europeans EUR 75 at VOA vs EUR 60 at embassy). Keep the return ticket accessible.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work visas run about EUR 250 basic tariff (India-post table; varies by nationality/validity) and student/educational visas about EUR 60 — but fees are reciprocity-based (EUR 20–150+), so confirm your nationality's exact fee rather than budgeting a universal figure. Financial-support documents are required for student admission, but no official minimum amount is published. A tourist VOA never authorises work.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every child needs their own passport and their own Iran permission — there is no family visa or shared authorization. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter from the non-travelling parent(s); it is not always a statutory requirement, but officers routinely ask, and a missing letter can delay or derail entry.

Dual nationals should enter and leave on the same passport, and check whether Iran restricts dual nationality — some countries treat entry on a foreign passport as a declaration with consequences. Travellers on refugee travel documents or laissez-passers face different rules everywhere: verify with the mission, not with forums.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

e-Visa route: 1) Apply at the official evisa.mfa.ir (the MFA's only official site). 2) Wait for the authorization code (3–10 calendar days). 3) Collect the visa at the embassy (7–14 working days) or take the VOA at the airport (1–3 hours, higher fee). VOA route: arrive at an international airport with the fee — 30 days, extendable. Ineligible nationalities (UK, US, PK, BD + 5): consular application only, in advance.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Quoting a single 'Iran visa fee'. Fees are reciprocity-based (EUR 20–150) — the universal figure is a myth.
  • Indians assuming visa-free entry. Suspended 22 November 2025 — a visa is now required.
  • Ineligible nationalities trying the e-Visa. UK, US, Pakistani and Bangladeshi passports are excluded — embassy only.
  • Skipping the mandatory insurance. Required with the application since May 2024.
  • Trusting agency fee/time marketing. Third-party '$170, 12-day' figures are unverified marketing.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Caseworkers and officers are testing two things: will you leave, and can you afford the trip. The strongest files show stable, genuine funds over several months — a single large unexplained deposit reads as funds parking and hurts more than it helps. Tie the itinerary to the money: the trip you're describing should be one your documented income plausibly supports.

Show a reason to return — employment with approved leave, enrolment, family, property — and keep the story consistent across every document. If you've been refused before, address the refusal reasons directly with new evidence; resubmitting the same file invites the same answer.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

evisa.mfa.ir is the MFA's stated only official visa-issuance website — e-Visa applications and authorization codes run there. Consulates handle the ineligible nationalities. Third-party 'Iran visa' agencies quoting flat fees and timelines are marketing, not official channels.

Translations, certifications and document format

Any document not in Iran's accepted language(s) needs a proper translation — check the mission or the MFA e-Visa system for whether certified translation is required, and by whom. Bank statements should be official downloads or stamped originals, not screenshots; employment letters should be on letterhead with a verifiable contact.

Submit scans well before any appointment so there's time to fix rejections. Keep originals for the appointment itself, and keep a full copy of everything you submitted — if the file is queried months later, you'll need to know exactly what the officer saw.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Where Iran requires in-person biometrics — fingerprints and a digital photograph — book the appointment early; centres fill up in peak seasons and the decision clock usually starts at the appointment, not the application. The photograph is captured there, so check whether a separate photo upload is even needed before paying for passport photos.

Interviews are the exception rather than the rule for short stays, but they happen where credibility needs testing. Answer from your actual plans, not from what you think the officer wants to hear — memorised scripts are easy to spot and hard to recover from.

Families and group travel

Each traveller — including every child — needs their own permission; there is no family authorization that covers a group. Apply in the same window so decisions land together, but understand each application is decided on its own merits: one refusal doesn't cancel the others, though it should prompt the rest of the group to re-check their own files.

For organised groups, keep a shared folder with every member's approval, the full itinerary, and the group leader's contact details. If one member is pulled aside at the border, the group should know where the documents are — not just that they exist somewhere in someone's email.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Declare currency and restricted goods as required. Keep the e-Visa authorization code, insurance policy and return ticket with your passport. Note the Israeli-stamp sensitivity before travel.

Departure and future travel

VOA stays extend twice (30 → 60 → 90 days) — process extensions before expiry. Overstaying in Iran carries serious consequences; the extension system works if used in time.

How to research a route without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) evisa.mfa.ir — the MFA's own system. 2) The ineligibility list (nine nationalities) — check it before anything else. Treat any universal fee figure, the old India visa-free rule, and agency marketing timelines as what they are — wrong or unverified.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid per Iran's rule, with blank pages, matching the application exactly
  • Visa / e-Visa / authorization approval saved offline and printed — verify it's linked to the current passport
  • Return or onward ticket and accommodation details accessible without internet
  • Proof of funds and ties-to-home evidence packed
  • Travel insurance documents with emergency numbers
  • Any required arrival forms (health, customs, digital entry cards) completed in advance
  • Certified translations for documents not in the accepted language
  • Emergency contacts: your embassy, your insurer, and someone at home who has your itinerary
  • Previous refusals or complications declared honestly wherever asked

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Iran is a check-the-list-first destination: nine nationalities are e-Visa-ineligible, everyone else uses evisa.mfa.ir or the 30-day VOA, fees are reciprocity-based (no universal figure), and insurance has been mandatory since May 2024. Indians note: the visa-free era ended 22 November 2025. Verify your nationality's exact fee — don't budget someone else's.
