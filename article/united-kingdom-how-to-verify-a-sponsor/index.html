A vacancy in United Kingdom is not a sponsorship opportunity merely because the advertisement says ‘visa available’. A credible route requires a real employer, a real role and an immigration category that accepts that employer/occupation/pay arrangement. This How To Verify A Sponsor guide shows how to verify those three layers before sending documents or paying anyone.

Current official context — checked 6 October 2026

For the Skilled Worker route, GOV.UK currently states that the standard application fee ranges from £819 to £1,865 depending on circumstances. The immigration health surcharge is usually £1,035 per year, and applicants who must show maintenance normally need at least £1,270 held under the financial-evidence rules. Job eligibility and salary are separate checks: the applicable going rate and route salary rules must both be checked for the occupation.

Official page: https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/how-much-it-costs

Separate the job search from immigration eligibility

Search for jobs and immigration eligibility in parallel. Employer sponsorship is formal: applicants should verify the sponsor and the exact occupation/salary rules on GOV.UK. Create a one-page route summary before applying: route name, sponsor requirement, occupation rule, salary/skill requirement, language rule, professional registration and whether dependants can accompany.

This prevents a common failure: investing months in a job that cannot support the intended immigration route. re-check UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office before relying on it.

How to verify an employer or sponsor

Use official sponsor/accreditation registers where they exist, the company's own careers site, business registry details and a professional email domain. Compare the legal employer name on the offer with the entity in the sponsor register. A recruitment agency may be legitimate, but it should be clear who the actual employer is. verify it against UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office before committing money or documents.

Red flags include payment for a job offer, requests to reimburse prohibited sponsor costs, interviews conducted only through anonymous messaging apps, contracts with a different company name, and salary promises that are inconsistent with the written offer. use UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office to confirm the live requirement where it can change.

Build an overseas-ready application

A useful international CV is evidence-led: role, employer, dates, quantified responsibilities, tools/technologies, regulated credentials and language ability. Remove vague objective statements and focus on the requirements in the vacancy. the live UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office reference below should be consulted before action.

In the cover letter, answer the employer's risk questions directly: Are you eligible for the occupation? Do you need sponsorship? Are your qualifications recognised? When can you relocate? If the employer uses a formal sponsorship programme, say which route you believe applies without pretending to give legal advice. verify it against UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office before committing money or documents.

Offer-stage checks

Before resigning or paying relocation costs, verify salary, hours, probation, location, remote-work expectations, relocation support, visa costs, health insurance and family benefits. Check whether the contract is conditional on immigration approval. Confirm the current position using UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office in the source list below.

A genuine offer can still be unsuitable if the salary fails the immigration threshold, the job title does not match the actual duties, or the employer is not eligible to sponsor. re-check UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office before relying on it.

From job offer to lawful work

The employer and applicant may have separate steps. In some systems the employer first obtains accreditation, sponsorship approval, labour-market approval or a job check; only then can the worker apply. In others, the processes are linked. Use https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa to map the actual sequence.

Do not start work until the status authorises it. Entry as a visitor or student does not automatically permit full-time employment. re-check UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office before relying on it.

Practical action checklist

  • Open the current official UK Visas and Immigration / Home Office page and confirm the rule still applies on 2026-10-05 or later.
  • Identify the exact visa/residence route and confirm that your current status allows you to use it.
  • Write down any fee, threshold, deadline or processing estimate with the date it was checked.
  • Keep the official application link separate from private explanatory articles.
  • Save copies of forms, evidence and confirmations before submitting anything.
  • Do not pay an unverified recruiter, adviser, landlord, scholarship agent or lender because of urgency.

UK sponsor licence check: the exact verification workflow

The Home Office worker sponsor register is the controlling starting point for checking whether an organisation is licensed to sponsor workers on listed routes. The register is updated frequently, so use the live GOV.UK version rather than an old spreadsheet, screenshot or recruiter-supplied list.

StepWhat to verifyWhy it matters
1Exact employer or organisation nameSimilar names can belong to different businesses.
2Town/city and organisation identityHelps distinguish organisations with similar names.
3Relevant sponsorship routeA licence must cover the route connected to the job.
4Current rating/status shown in the registerUse the live register, not a cached third-party result.
5The vacancy itselfA licensed sponsor can still be impersonated in a fake advert.
