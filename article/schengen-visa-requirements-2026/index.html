Schengen Visa Requirements 2026: The Complete Document Checklist

The Schengen requirements are a fixed, published list — and every refusal maps to a line on it. Here is the complete 2026 checklist, item by item.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20268 min read

The Schengen visa has no hidden requirements: the European Commission publishes the document list, the Visa Code fixes the insurance figure and the fee, and every refusal ground corresponds to a line on the list. This guide walks the complete 2026 requirements item by item, so the file you lodge is the file the rules describe.

Quick Answer

The baseline requirements: a completed application form, a photo, a passport (issued within the last 10 years, valid 3+ months after you leave), travel medical insurance of at least €30,000 covering all Schengen states for the entire stay, and documents proving purpose, means, accommodation, and intention to return. Fee: €90 (€45 for children 6–12). Decision: 15 days standard, up to 45.

Fee €90 · Insurance €30,000 · Decision 15–45 days · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 8 minutes

1. The application form

One form per applicant, completed fully and signed. Every field matters: a blank reads as a gap, and contradictions between the form and the supporting documents are a standalone refusal ground ("information not reliable"). Fill it from the documents, not from memory — dates, employer details, and travel history must match everywhere.

2. Photo and passport

A recent passport-style photo meeting the standard specifications. The passport must have been issued within the last 10 years and remain valid at least 3 months after you leave the Schengen area — check the issue date, not just expiry: extended-validity passports can breach the 10-year rule while looking valid. A passport failing these rules is its own refusal ground.

3. Travel medical insurance: €30,000

Under Article 15 of the Visa Code, insurance must cover at least €30,000 of emergency medical care, hospitalisation, and repatriation — in all Schengen states, for the entire stay. The certificate must state the amount, the territory, and the trip dates. Inadequate insurance is a standalone refusal ground. Full detail in our insurance requirements guide.

4. Purpose of the visit

Documents proving why you are going: a dated day-by-day itinerary, accommodation for every night, and the purpose document — conference registration, business invitation, or a host's formal invitation. "Justification for the purpose and conditions of the intended stay was not provided" is the refusal form's wording when this line fails.

5. Means of subsistence

Proof of sufficient funds, judged against daily reference amounts set by each state (France: €65/day with prepaid accommodation), and proof the money is lawfully available to you — statements showing regular income over months, not sudden deposits. Sponsored trips must be declared with the sponsor's statements and a signed letter. Full detail in our bank statement guide.

6. Intention to return

Evidence of ties at home: an employer's leave letter with a return date, property or lease, family, ongoing commitments, a history of leaving on time. Doubt that you will leave before the visa expires is the most common refusal tick — it is a judgment about ties, not character.

7. Fee, lodging, and decision

Lodge at the consulate of your main destination (most nights; first-entry country if nights tie), from 6 months before travel and no later than 15 days before. Pay the €90 fee (€45 for children 6–12) — non-refundable. The decision comes in 15 days normally, up to 45 days. Run the whole file through our visa document checklist before the appointment.

Useful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Schengen visa requirements in 2026?

Form, photo, qualifying passport, €30,000 insurance, and proof of purpose, means, accommodation, and return intention — plus the €90 fee.

How much bank balance is required?

No fixed figure: states set daily reference amounts (France: €65/day with prepaid accommodation), judged against your real trip cost and months of statements.

Where do I apply?

At the consulate of your main destination — most nights — or the first-entry country if nights tie.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Seven lines, no secrets: form, photo, qualifying passport, €30,000 of insurance, proven purpose, proven funds lawfully yours, proven ties home — lodged at the right consulate with the €90 fee. Every refusal is one of these lines, failed.
