U.S. Diversity Visa Lottery: How to Use the Official DV System

The Diversity Visa program is an annual U.S. immigrant-visa program. Registration, selection checks and current program notices should be verified only through the Department of State and the official E-DV system.

Current program notice — checked 7 October 2026: The U.S. Department of State says diversity-visa issuances are currently paused under its August 31, 2026 guidance. The Department says applicants may still submit applications and attend scheduled interviews, but no diversity visas are being issued while the pause applies. Check the live State Department notice before taking action.

Read the current Department of State DV issuance guidance →

Where official DV registration happens

The Department of State publishes a limited registration period for each annual DV program. Entries are submitted electronically through the official E-DV website during the announced window; late or paper entries are not accepted. Do not rely on a private website for a future opening date.

Official E-DV website: dvprogram.state.gov →

No verified future registration date is stated here. The Department previously announced changes to the DV-2027 entry period and said it would announce the registration start date separately. CountryPilot will not invent or predict a date.

How the DV process works

  1. Read the current annual Department of State instructions and confirm that you meet that program year's eligibility rules.
  2. Submit one electronic entry during the official registration period. The Department says registration itself is free.
  3. Keep the confirmation page and unique confirmation number. Do not hand control of that number to an agent or facilitator.
  4. Use Entrant Status Check on the official E-DV website to see whether the entry was selected. Selection is not a visa approval.
  5. If selected, follow the current Department instructions for the immigrant-visa application, supporting documents and interview process.
  6. Re-check current program notices before paying fees, travelling or making irreversible plans.

One entry per person per registration period

The Department of State says the law permits only one entry by or for each person during a registration period and that multiple entries can disqualify that person's entries. A spouse may submit a separate eligible entry in their own name if the annual rules permit it.

Protect your confirmation number

The confirmation number is used to access Entrant Status Check and official follow-up instructions. The Department strongly encourages entrants to complete their own entry and retain their confirmation information. Treat messages claiming that you have “won” as suspicious unless the result is confirmed through the official status-check system.

Selection does not guarantee a visa

Random selection only permits a person to continue in the DV process. The applicant must still meet the applicable program requirements, complete the required application steps and be found eligible. Visa-number availability and current Department guidance also matter.

CountryPilot editorial rule for annual DV dates

This page deliberately does not hard-code a future registration window until the Department of State publishes that program year's official instructions. The page should be reviewed when a new annual instruction set or major Department notice is published.
