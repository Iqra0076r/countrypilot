How to use this Sri Lanka ETA guide

Sri Lanka's entry system centers on one mandatory step: the Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA), obtained online before travel at eta.gov.lk. Free does not mean optional — the 2026 free-ETA scheme waives the fee, not the application. For work or study, see the full Sri Lanka Visa & Entry Requirements Guide.

Passport validity and document condition

Your passport must be valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date, with at least one blank page. The ETA is linked to the passport number — renewing the passport after approval voids it; renew first.

The ETA route, by nationality

Nearly every visitor needs an ETA approved before departure — the standard 30-day double-entry tourist ETA costs USD 50 (SAARC nationals USD 20), except the 40 nationalities whose ETA is free under the May 2026 scheme. There is no general visa on arrival, and carriers check ETA approval before boarding.

Transit and onward travel

If you will clear immigration in Colombo, you need the appropriate ETA in advance. Passengers remaining airside on a single through-ticket generally do not.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Admission is granted by the officer at the port of entry, whatever your ETA says. Carry the passport, the printed ETA approval, onward travel, your first accommodation address, and proof you can support yourself.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Vaccination certificates — yellow fever above all — follow your travel history, not your nationality.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Travel insurance is not an ETA condition — but hospitals always bill. Buy the policy before departure. See the Sri Lanka Travel Insurance & Health Guide for what to look for.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

Two dates matter on entry: the visa's expiry and the stay granted at the border. The second one governs — verify the admitted-until date on arrival. An overstay can void a multi-entry visa and trigger entry bans.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

The tourist ETA never authorizes employment — including remote work while physically in Sri Lanka. The alternative is the Digital Nomad Visa effective 20 April 2026: renewable 365-day stay, USD 500 first-year fee, minimum USD 2,000/month foreign-source income (+ USD 500 per dependent).

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Each child needs their own passport and ETA application. Carry birth certificates when family names differ, and a consent letter if only one parent travels.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

  1. Check whether your nationality is among the 40 free-ETA countries on eta.gov.lk.
  2. Apply only on the official portal — never through a copycat site.
  3. Wait for the approval — usually within 24 hours.
  4. Carry the printed approval with its linked passport.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Reading 'free' as 'not needed'. The 40-country scheme waives the fee, not the ETA — carriers check approval before boarding.
  • Paying a copycat site. eta.gov.lk is the official portal — lookalikes charge for ETAs that are free there.
  • Renewing the passport after approval. The ETA is linked to the passport number — renew first, then apply.
  • Working remotely on a tourist ETA. Remote workers should look at the Digital Nomad Visa instead.

Quality means completeness plus consistency: identical name spellings, matching dates, funds that plausibly cover the itinerary.

Standard ETAs are typically approved within 24 hours (official guidance: allow 24–72 hours; apply at least a week before travel). Do not board without the approval — carriers check.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Verify the site is eta.gov.lk before entering card details — legitimate portals never ask for crypto or gift-card payment. Keep the approved ETA printed and on your phone.

Translations, certifications and document format

The ETA needs a valid passport and accurate details. Names, dates and amounts must match across your file.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

No pre-departure biometrics could be verified for the ETA. Expect standard identity checks at the immigration counter on arrival.

Families and group travel

Minors need their own passport, their own form, and their own approval — with its own lead time. If the family includes dual nationals, check Sri Lanka's entry rule per person before booking.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Currency declaration thresholds apply per person or per family — know which. Medications in original containers with prescriptions. Verify drone, radio and satellite-device rules specifically. For safety context, see the Sri Lanka Travel Safety & Security Guide.

Departure and future travel

Keep evidence of timely departure: boarding passes, exit stamps, the electronic record where one exists. A clean record is the simplest possible answer on future applications.

How to research a route without getting misled

Start at the ETA portal, confirm with the embassy, sanity-check against a current official advisory. Watch for the two classic patterns: copycat sites presenting themselves as official, and agents presenting their own service bundle as the government fee.

A final document audit before travel

  • ETA approved (free or paid) on the official portal — correct passport number on the approval
  • Passport valid 6+ months beyond arrival with a blank page
  • All names, dates and amounts identical across the application file
  • Onward travel provable at the border, not just intended
  • Health and insurance paperwork separate from — and as complete as — the visa file

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Sri Lanka ETA free now?

For citizens of 40 countries (including India and Australia), the 30-day tourist ETA has been free since 25 May 2026 — but the ETA itself remains mandatory and must be approved before travel. Everyone else pays the standard USD 50 (SAARC nationals USD 20). Apply only at eta.gov.lk.

How long does the Sri Lanka ETA take?

Most tourist ETAs are approved within 24 hours; official guidance says allow 24–72 hours and apply at least a week before travel. Do not board without the approval — carriers check.

Is there visa on arrival in Sri Lanka?

No general visa on arrival. The ETA, approved before departure, is the entry document.

Can I work remotely in Sri Lanka on a tourist ETA?

No. Remote workers should look at the Digital Nomad Visa (from 20 April 2026): renewable 365-day stay, USD 500 first-year fee, minimum USD 2,000/month in foreign-source income.

What happens if I renew my passport after ETA approval?

The ETA is linked to your passport number — a new passport voids it. Renew first, then apply for the ETA.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Sri Lanka, everything starts at eta.gov.lk — and only there, since copycat sites monetize the same application. The 30-day tourist ETA is USD 50 (free for 40 nationalities since May 2026, SAARC USD 20), usually approved within a day. Remote workers have a real new option in the April 2026 Digital Nomad Visa. Verify the current scheme list before paying anything.
