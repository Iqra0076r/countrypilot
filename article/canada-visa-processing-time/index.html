Canada Visa Processing Time 2026: IRCC's Two Numbers: The Standard and the Actual

IRCC publishes both a service standard and weekly actual processing times for Canada visas — and in 2026 the two rarely match. Here is how to read both numbers and plan an application that survives the real queue.

Canada is one of the few countries that publishes two processing numbers: a service standard (the target IRCC aims to meet for 80% of complete applications) and weekly actuals showing how long recent applications really took, broken down by country. In 2026 the actuals frequently run longer than the standards — and knowing both is the difference between a plan and a guess.

This guide sets out the 2026 standards, the most recent weekly actuals, where the hidden queues sit (biometrics appointments, peak-season backlogs), and how far ahead to apply from high-demand countries. All figures are October 2026; IRCC updates the actuals weekly, so confirm the current numbers on the live tool before planning.

The two numbers that matter: standard vs actual

The service standard is IRCC's promise about what it aims to do: 14 days for a visitor visa (temporary resident visa), 60 days for a study permit, 60 days for a work permit, for applications filed outside Canada. It covers complete applications — a file that triggers a request for more documents leaves the standard behind.

The weekly actuals are the reality check: IRCC publishes how long recently finalized applications actually took, by visa type and applicant country. These move with demand, staffing and season — the September 2026 actuals showed India visitor visas at ~27 days, nearly double the standard, while some low-demand countries beat the standard comfortably. Plan from the actuals, not the standards.

The hidden queue: biometrics appointments

The published times start when IRCC considers the application complete — but before that, most applicants must give biometrics (fingerprints and photo, CAD $85 individual / CAD $170 family maximum, 2026) at a Visa Application Centre. In peak season, the VAC appointment itself can add weeks of lead time that appears in no processing-time table.

Book the VAC appointment the moment the instruction letter arrives, and treat the biometrics date — not the online submission date — as the real start of your timeline. Transit applications go through the same system with the same lead time as a visitor visa, so secure transit permission with the same margin.

What slows a Canada file down

  • Incomplete files. The standards only cover complete applications; a request for additional documents restarts the effective clock.
  • Peak-season volume. Study-permit season (May–August for September intakes) and summer visitor demand stretch actuals well past standards.
  • Country-specific verification. Employment, funds and travel history that need confirming in high-fraud environments add manual review time.
  • Medical exams. If IRCC requests one, the panel-physician appointment and results transmission add their own weeks.
  • Copycat eTA sites. Third-party sites charge CAD $50–80 to 'process' a CAD $7 eTA that takes minutes on canada.ca — a pure markup with no speed benefit.

When to apply: the planning formula

For visitor visas from high-demand countries (India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines): apply 3–4 months before travel. The ~27-day India actual is an average — your file can land on either side of it, and the VAC appointment comes on top.

For study permits: apply the moment the acceptance letter and funds evidence are ready — for September intakes, that means March–April, not June. Nigeria's 10-week actual in September 2026 is a warning: late applications miss semesters.

For work permits: the employer's LMIA or LMIA-exempt offer drives the timeline — the 60-day standard starts from a complete file, and employer-side steps come first. Coordinate the filing date with the employer, then add the VAC lead time.

Tracking the live numbers

IRCC's check processing times tool on canada.ca is updated weekly — it is the only source worth planning from. Check it the week you file, and again before booking non-refundable travel. Track the application itself in the same IRCC secure account (GCKey or sign-in partner) used to file; keep the credentials, because extensions and future applications run through the same login.

No agent can buy a faster IRCC decision, and fees are paid only inside the IRCC account or at the VAC — demands for wire transfers or crypto are fraud.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Canada visitor visa take in 2026?

The IRCC service standard is 14 days for complete applications outside Canada, but weekly actuals run longer at busy posts — about 27 days for India in late September 2026. Check the live tool for your country before planning.

How long does a Canada study permit take?

The 60-day standard versus 5 weeks (India), 7 weeks (Pakistan) and 10 weeks (Nigeria) in the September 2026 actuals. Apply 3–4 months before the semester starts from high-demand countries.

Does the processing time include the biometrics appointment?

Effectively no — the published times assume the file is complete, and VAC appointment availability in peak season can add weeks before biometrics are even given. Book the VAC slot immediately.

How fast is the Canadian eTA?

Usually minutes after applying on canada.ca (CAD $7). Do not pay third-party sites CAD $50–80 for the same submission.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Plan from IRCC's weekly actuals, not the service standards: 14 days is the visitor target, but ~27 days was India's reality in September 2026, and study permits stretched to 10 weeks for Nigeria. File 3–4 months ahead from high-demand countries, book biometrics immediately, and keep every step inside the official IRCC account.
