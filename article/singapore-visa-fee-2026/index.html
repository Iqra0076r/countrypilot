Singapore Visa Fee 2026

Singapore visa fees 2026: S$30 tourist visa, S$330 Employment Pass, S$105 Student's Pass, free SG Arrival Card — processing times, refund rules, agent charges.

Quick Answer

In 2026, Singapore's official visa fees are fixed: the tourist visa costs S$30 per application (non-refundable), the Employment Pass costs S$105 + S$225 = S$330 total, the Student's Pass costs S$45 + S$60 = S$105, and the SG Arrival Card is free. Add S$30 for a multiple-journey visa where needed, and the agent's service charge on top of the tourist visa.

Singapore visa fees at a glance (2026)

Singapore's official fee schedule is short and fixed — the numbers below come from ICA and MOM publications verified in October 2026. Agents and service providers add their own charges on top, but the government portion never moves.

Tourist visa fee: S$30 per application

The tourist visa is required for nationals of countries including India, China, Pakistan and Bangladesh. The processing fee is S$30 per application — and that means per traveller, including infants: each child needs a separate visa application and pays the same S$30.

Applications are agent-mediated — individuals generally cannot apply direct, so you apply through an authorized visa agent and pay the S$30 processing fee plus the agent's own service charge. The fee is non-refundable even if the visa is refused, and processing takes 3–5 working days (2026). There is no interview as standard, but your photograph must meet ICA specifications — a photo rejection wastes the non-refundable fee.

Employment Pass fee: S$330 total

The Employment Pass is employer-petitioned: the Singapore-registered employer applies via the myMOM Portal after running the self-assessment tool. The official fee stack is S$105 application + S$225 issuance = S$330 total (2026), plus S$30 for a multiple-journey visa if the pass holder needs it.

Processing runs ~10 business days for Singapore-registered companies and up to 8 weeks for overseas companies (3+ weeks is typical in 2026). All pass fees are non-refundable — so the employer's eligibility filing has to be right the first time, including the salary threshold: the EP qualifying salary climbs to S$6,000/month (S$6,600 for financial services) for new applications from 1 January 2027, up from S$5,600/S$6,200.

Student's Pass fee: S$105 plus compulsory insurance

For the Student's Pass the institution registers the application via SOLAR+ / the ICA e-Service. The fee stack is S$45 processing on submission + S$60 issuance after approval = S$105 (plus S$30 multiple-journey visa if applicable), all non-refundable (2026). Allow 2–4 weeks (1–2 weeks for IHLs, longer at peak intake).

The bigger student cost line is insurance: medical insurance is compulsory for the Student's Pass, and most institutions arrange mandatory group plans bundled into fees, typically S$200–600 per year (2026) — see our Singapore travel insurance & health guide for the full picture. Note there is no fixed government minimum for proof of funds — ICA assesses whether funds cover actual tuition plus living costs for the full program; a practical planning figure is around S$10,000–12,000 per year in living costs.

What costs nothing: Arrival Card and 96-hour transit

The SG Arrival Card — the electronic arrival and health declaration submitted via ICA within 3 days before arrival — is free and mandatory for every traveller, including visa-free nationalities. Anyone charging you for it is running a scam.

Singapore also offers the 96-hour Visa-Free Transit Facility (VFTF) — free — for eligible transit passengers: nationals of countries including India and CIS states who are transiting to or from a third country, hold an onward ticket departing within 96 hours, and hold a valid visa or long-term pass for Australia, Canada, Germany, Japan, New Zealand, Switzerland, the UK or the USA. There is no general visa-on-arrival outside the VFTF.

Agent charges and scam avoidance

Because the tourist visa is agent-mediated, the S$30 government fee is fixed — everything above it is the agent's service charge. Treat agents adding large 'express' surcharges for the standard 3–5-day service as padding: there is no official express lane being sold. And remember there is no self-service 'Singapore eVisa' portal for individuals — that claim is a scammer's front. No one can guarantee pass approval; MOM and ICA decide on the file.

Refund rules: everything is non-refundable

Every official Singapore visa and pass fee — the S$30 tourist visa, the S$330 Employment Pass stack, the S$105 Student's Pass stack — is non-refundable. That makes pre-filing accuracy the real money-saver: correct salary against the applicable EP threshold, compliant photos, and the institution's accurate SOLAR+ registration for students. A refused application costs the fee plus the agent's charge all over again.

Budgeting beyond fees: the 2026–2027 salary floors

Fees are the small part of work-route budgeting. The S Pass salary floor rose to S$3,600/month (S$4,000 financial services) effective 1 September 2026, with 8 new occupations added to the NTS Occupation List. The EP threshold rises to S$6,000/month (S$6,600 finance) for new applications from 1 January 2027, with an age-progressive ladder reaching S$11,500–S$12,700 at 45+. Budget the pass against the threshold that applies on the filing date — see the full Singapore visa & entry requirements guide for the complete entry framework.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Singapore tourist visa fee in 2026?

S$30 per application, non-refundable — paid through an authorized visa agent (individuals generally cannot apply direct), plus the agent's service charge. Processing takes 3–5 working days.

Do I need to pay for the SG Arrival Card?

No — the SG Arrival Card is free and mandatory for all travellers, including visa-free nationalities. Anyone charging you for it is running a scam.

How much does a Singapore Employment Pass cost?

S$330 total (S$105 application + S$225 issuance), plus S$30 for a multiple-journey visa if needed — all 2026 figures, all non-refundable. The employer applies via the myMOM Portal.

How much is the Singapore Student's Pass?

S$45 processing + S$60 issuance = S$105 (plus S$30 MJV if applicable), non-refundable (2026). Compulsory medical insurance adds S$200–600 per year via institutional group plans.

Are Singapore visa fees refundable?

No. The S$30 tourist visa, the S$330 Employment Pass stack and the S$105 Student's Pass stack are all non-refundable — get the eligibility filing right before paying.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

In 2026, budget Singapore on fixed numbers: S$30 per tourist-visa application (agent charge on top), S$330 for the Employment Pass stack, S$105 for the Student's Pass stack — all non-refundable — and zero for the SG Arrival Card and 96-hour transit facility. The real money is in the salary thresholds behind the fees: S$3,600/month S Pass floor from September 2026, S$6,000/month EP qualifying salary from January 2027. Confirm everything on ica.gov.sg / mom.gov.sg before paying.
