Current official context — checked 5 October 2026

A Green Card grants lawful permanent residence, but the route can be family-based, employment-based, humanitarian or another statutory category. USCIS controls the petition/adjustment framework, while the Department of State handles immigrant-visa processing abroad.

Official page: https://www.uscis.gov/green-card

Map the path from temporary status to long-term status

In United States, lawful permanent residence (Green Card) is not simply a longer version of a temporary visa. The qualifying route may depend on years of lawful residence, type of permission, absences, employment, language/integration, taxes or other conditions. Some temporary categories count differently from others.

Country-specific decision map

For the United States, separate the Department of State visa process from USCIS petitions/status benefits and from admission at the border. A visa stamp, an approved petition and lawful status are related but not identical concepts. Student cases normally involve a SEVP-certified school and Form I-20; employment routes vary substantially by classification and sometimes begin with an employer petition. re-check U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) and U.S. Department of State before relying on it.
