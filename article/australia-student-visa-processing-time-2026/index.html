Australia Student Visa Processing Time 2026: Decision Speeds Explained

Australia publishes its visa decision speeds every month — and the student figure is one of the most watched. Here is what the 2026 numbers say and how to use them.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20266 min read

Australia is unusually transparent about visa timing: the Department of Home Affairs publishes monthly processing-time headlines by visa category. For students planning around intake dates, those headlines are the planning instrument. This guide explains the 2026 figures and how to read them.

CountryPilot Research Guide · Australia · Visas & Immigration

Source-led planning guide · Updated October 14, 2026

Quick Answer

Home Affairs' August 2026 processing headlines: Visitor visas (subclasses 600, 601, and 651 combined) decided in under 1 day; Student visas in 22 days. The figures are published monthly and move with volume — always check the current month's publication on homeaffairs.gov.au rather than planning on a remembered number.

Student 22 days (Aug 2026) · Visitor under 1 day · Published monthly · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 6 minutes

How the monthly headlines work

Each month Home Affairs publishes how long recent decisions took, by visa subclass — the headlines (like "22 days" for students) describe the recent centre of the distribution, not a promise for your file. They are backward-looking: they tell you how fast the system was, which is the best available guide to how fast it is. The publication to watch is the department's visa-processing-times page; third-party summaries lag it.

Student visas: the 22-day headline in context

Twenty-two days is fast by world standards — but it is an average of straightforward files. Applications needing further checks (unverifiable enrolment, funds questions, health or character requirements) take longer, and intake peaks slow the whole pipeline. The planning rule: treat the headline as the best case, add weeks of margin around intake deadlines, and lodge the day the file is complete rather than the day the deadline looms.

Visitor visas: under 1 day

The August 2026 visitor headline — under 1 day across the 600, 601, and 651 combined — covers the subclass 600 Tourist stream (from AUD $250 since 1 July 2026) and the ETA subclass 601 (AUD $20 via the app). It is among the fastest visitor decisions anywhere. Even so, apply ahead of fixed travel dates: the headline describes the system, not your file. Full fee detail in our Australia tourist visa fee guide and ETA vs visitor visa guide.

Fees changed on 1 July 2026 — times did not

Do not confuse the two July stories. Fees rose by roughly 25% across nearly all categories on 1 July 2026 — older guides quoting AUD $200 for the 600 or AUD $2,000 for the student visa are out of date on price. Processing times are a separate monthly publication and kept improving. Check the visa listing pages for the current charge and the processing-times publication for the current speed — two live checks, not one remembered guide.

What slows an Australian file down

The reliable delay-makers: incomplete applications in ImmiAccount, funds evidence that needs verification, and health examinations where required. Each is a file problem, and each is fixable before lodgement. Total your funds honestly with our proof-of-funds calculator and run the documents through our visa document checklist before you lodge.
