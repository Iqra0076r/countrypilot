Quick Answer

The UAE visa fee for Pakistani citizens in 2026 starts at AED 250 for a 30-day single-entry visa (+ 5% VAT, GDRFA Dubai schedule). The 90-day multiple-entry visa costs AED 1,740 + VAT. The UAE runs a sponsor-based system — your airline, hotel or a licensed travel agency applies inside the UAE — and Pakistani citizens need a pre-arranged visa before flying.

How this UAE visa fee for Pakistani citizens 2026 guide works

Almost every UAE visa is issued through a sponsor — an airline, hotel, travel agency, employer or family member — via the ICP or GDRFA Dubai. The figures below come from GDRFA Dubai's 2026 fee schedule. The exact fee depends on the channel and the emirate, so always confirm the live figure on u.ae or the GDRFA/ICP site before paying.

What Pakistani citizens actually pay

Pakistani citizens need a pre-arranged visa — apply through your airline, hotel or a licensed travel agency, which submits the application inside the UAE via ICP or GDRFA. Budget from AED 250 + 5% VAT for the 30-day single-entry visa, and get an itemised quote: the sponsor's service charge sits on top of the government fee and varies widely between channels.

What the quoted fee does not include

The GDRFA figures exclude 5% VAT and the sponsor's own service fee, which varies by airline, hotel and agency. They also exclude travel insurance and any document-attestation costs your sponsor asks for. Always get the quote broken down: government fee, VAT, service charge.

Where and how to apply

Your sponsor applies inside the UAE through the ICP or GDRFA system — you don't visit an embassy. Once approved, the e-visa is emailed to you; carry a printout with your passport. Typical issuance is around 48 hours for tourist visas. Confirm the current process on u.ae, the UAE government's official portal.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

No UAE government source publishes a '$4,000 bank balance requirement' for tourist visas — the claim circulates through visa agencies only. Pay the visa fee only through your sponsor's official channel with a receipt, never to a personal account. And remember the GDRFA schedule excludes VAT: a quote far above the tiers here should come with an itemised breakdown or be walked away from.
