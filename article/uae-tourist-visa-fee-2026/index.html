UAE Tourist Visa Fee 2026: 30-Day and 90-Day Costs Explained

The UAE prices its tourist visas by duration and entries — and the 2026 GDRFA schedule sets the numbers. Here is every option, priced.

CountryPilot Editorial TeamUpdated October 14, 20266 min read

The UAE does not sell one tourist visa — it sells a menu: 30 or 90 days, single or multiple entry, each with its own government fee on the 2026 GDRFA schedule. Add the mandatory health insurance and 5% VAT, and the sticker price is only the start. This guide prices every option.

Quick Answer

On the 2026 GDRFA Dubai schedule: AED 250 for the 30-day single-entry tourist visa (plus 5% VAT), AED 690 for 30-day multiple-entry, and AED 600 for the 90-day single-entry visa. Health insurance is mandatory for visitors. Some travellers with qualifying residence permits get visa on arrival: 14 days for AED 100 or 60 days for AED 250.

30-day single AED 250 · 30-day multiple AED 690 · 90-day single AED 600 · Updated 2026-10-14 · Reading time 6 minutes

The 2026 fee menu

The GDRFA schedule prices the tourist options as follows:

  • 30-day single-entry: AED 250 (+ 5% VAT)
  • 30-day multiple-entry: AED 690
  • 90-day single-entry: AED 600

The 30-day single entry is the standard tourist choice; the multiple-entry variant suits travellers hopping in and out of the Emirates within the month; the 90-day single entry covers long family visits and extended stays. Choose by itinerary, not by price alone — the wrong duration is the expensive option.

What sits on top of the fee

Two additions apply. First, 5% VAT on the visa fee (shown explicitly on the 30-day single entry). Second — and larger — mandatory health insurance: visitors must hold cover, with basic plans running about AED 600–1,200 per year and comprehensive cover up to about AED 3,000. A realistic tourist budget is the visa fee + VAT + insurance, not the fee alone.

Visa on arrival: the cheaper door for some

Travellers holding qualifying residence permits from listed countries may be eligible for visa on arrival instead of a pre-arranged visa: 14 days for AED 100 or 60 days for AED 250. Eligibility turns on the specific residence permit held — it is not a general nationality rule — so verify your permit's position before travelling on the assumption. Where it applies, it undercuts every pre-arranged option on price.

Pre-arranged e-visa vs on arrival

Most nationalities needing advance permission use the pre-arranged e-visa route — applied for before travel through the official channels or a sponsor (airline, hotel, or travel agency). The e-visa equivalent carries the same GDRFA fee schedule. Apply with margin: the visa must be issued before departure, and last-minute applications compress every downstream plan.

Paying correctly

Fees are paid through the official GDRFA/ICP channels or an authorised sponsor. Third-party "visa fee" sites add markups to a process the official channels handle directly. Keep the payment receipt and the issued e-visa accessible for the journey — airlines check the visa before boarding.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a 30-day Dubai tourist visa in 2026?

AED 250 single-entry (+ 5% VAT) or AED 690 multiple-entry, on the 2026 GDRFA schedule.

How much is the 90-day UAE visit visa?

AED 600 for the 90-day single-entry visa (2026 schedule).

Is health insurance required for a UAE tourist visa?

Yes — health insurance is mandatory for visitors, roughly AED 600–1,200/year for basic plans.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

AED 250, 690, or 600 depending on duration and entries — plus VAT and mandatory insurance: the UAE tourist visa is a priced menu, so match the option to the itinerary, verify any visa-on-arrival eligibility, and pay through official channels only.
