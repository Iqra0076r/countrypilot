Germany visa fee for Indian citizens 2026

Germany visa fee for Indian citizens in 2026: the €90 Schengen Type C fee via the German mission — plus the separate €75 national long-stay fee for work and study routes.

Quick Answer

The Germany Schengen visa fee for Indian citizens in 2026 is €90 for adults (€45 for children aged 6–11, free for under-6s) — the EU-wide Type C schedule, applied for at the German mission or VFS centre when Germany is your main destination. Germany's national long-stay (Type D) visa for work or study is a separate €75, paid at the embassy.

How this Germany visa fee for Indian citizens 2026 guide works

Germany applies the EU-harmonised Type C fee for short stays: tourism, business or family visits up to 90 days. You apply at the German mission or its VFS centre when Germany is your main Schengen destination (the country where you will spend the most days). Long stays — work, study, Ausbildung, family reunion, Chancenkarte — need the national Type D visa instead: a different form, a different timeline, and a €75 fee paid at the embassy (2026).

What Indian citizens actually pay

Indian applicants apply through the German mission/VFS network in India — the Federal Foreign Office publishes India-specific visa information via india.diplo.de. Apply when Germany is your main destination; if another Schengen state gets more of your days, apply there instead, since the wrong mission can refuse to accept the application.

What the quoted fee does not include

The €90 covers the application only. It does not include the VFS service fee, passport courier charges, the mandatory travel medical insurance (minimum €30,000 for the whole Schengen area), or document translations.

Where and how to apply

Book through the German mission or VFS centre serving your area, submit biometrics and documents, and pay the €90 (plus the VFS service fee) at submission. Standard processing is about 15 calendar days; you can apply up to 6 months before travel. For the Type D national visa, the €75 is paid at the embassy or consulate.

Fee scams and myths to avoid

Official fees are paid at the mission or VFS counter with a receipt, or through the official booking portal — never to a 'visa fee' personal account. Anyone quoting a Type C fee far above €90, or promising a guaranteed Schengen visa for an extra payment, is running a scam.
