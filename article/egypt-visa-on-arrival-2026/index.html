Quick Answer

Egypt issues visa on arrival to around 46 nationalities for $30 (single entry, 30 days) from 1 March 2026 — including the US and UK. Since 1 August 2026 a digital VoA pilot at Cairo airport lets you pay by QR code ($36). Travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh need an e-Visa or consular visa instead ($30 single / $65 multiple from 27 April 2026).

How to use this Egypt visa-on-arrival guide

Egypt runs three parallel tourist channels: the e-Visa at visa2egypt.gov.eg for around 75 nationalities, visa on arrival for around 46, and — since 1 August 2026 — a digital VoA pilot at Cairo airport with QR-code payment. The on-arrival fee is $30 (raised from $25 on 1 March 2026; single entry, up to 30 days). The US and UK qualify for visa on arrival; travellers from India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh need an e-Visa or consular visa instead. Unsure of your channel? Expect six months' validity beyond your planned stay — the common airline-enforced standard. The exact rule for Egypt was not verifiable in October 2026; confirm on visa2egypt.gov.eg or via IATA Timatic. Keep a blank page and an undamaged document.

Transit and onward travel

Transit is its own immigration question in Egypt. If you change planes on Egyptian soil, check whether a transit permission is required — denied boarding at origin is the common failure mode.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

A visa gets you to the border; the officer decides admission. Carry the passport (with visa sticker or e-Visa approval), a return or onward ticket, your accommodation address, and funding evidence. Answer directly and consistently.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Yellow-fever certificates are the classic trigger for arrivals from endemic countries. Check with the airline close to departure; carry certificates in original form.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Not an entry condition, but medical care abroad is billed at full price. Buy comprehensive cover before departure — see our Egypt Travel Insurance & Health Guide.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

The period stated on your visa or entry stamp is what counts — typically 30 days, single entry. Check the stamp before leaving the counter.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

A visitor entry never authorizes employment — including remote work for a foreign employer. Work and study need their own permissions.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every traveller — children included — needs their own travel document and own visa on arrival. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

Visa on arrival: 1) Confirm your nationality is in the ~46-country VOA list. 2) Have $30 in cash. 3) Buy the visa sticker at the bank/visa counter before the immigration queue. 4) At Cairo, the digital pilot offers QR payment ($36) — not at other airports. e-Visa nationalities: apply ahead ($30 single / $65 multiple from 27 Apr 2026).

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming visa on arrival means no preparation — bring the fee in cash and documents ready.
  • Quoting the old $25 fee — it is $30 since 1 March 2026.
  • Travelling on last year's rules — Egypt changed entry rules three times in 2026.
  • Relying on the digital pilot outside Cairo — other airports still use the cash counter.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Most visitor refusals come down to one doubt: will this person leave? Answer it with a leave letter, property or lease documents, family ties, and bank statements showing you can fund the trip.

Processing: issued at the counter — the variable is queue time. e-Visa approvals slip; apply ahead and do not book non-refundable flights first.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

Start every application at the official e-Visa portal (visa2egypt.gov.eg) — the only channel whose outcome counts. Copycat sites buy ads on the portal's name. Red flags: 'guaranteed approval', personal-account or crypto payments.

Translations, certifications and document format

Bring originals plus one copy of everything: bank statements (3–6 months), an employment letter with approved leave dates, property or lease documents, prior visas and entry stamps, the trip itinerary. Other-language documents need an English translation.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

No pre-travel interview for visa on arrival, but expect fingerprints and a photograph at the counter.

Families and group travel

Every traveller needs their own authorisation — each family member, including infants, needs a separate visa on arrival at the counter. If one member is refused, address the refusal reason.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Declare cash above the reporting threshold and keep medications in original packaging with prescriptions. Our Egypt Travel Safety & Security Guide covers border-day details.

Departure and future travel

A clean travel history compounds — future applications get easier. An overstay can void the visa plus trigger multi-year bars; extend through the proper channel before expiry.

How to research a route without getting misled

Frequently Asked Questions

Who qualifies for Egypt's visa on arrival?

Around 46 nationalities, including the US and UK. India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh need an e-Visa or consular visa — confirm on visa2egypt.gov.eg.

How much is Egypt's visa on arrival in 2026?

$30 single entry (from 1 March 2026). The digital VoA pilot at Cairo costs $36 via QR payment (since 1 August 2026).

e-Visa or visa on arrival — which should I pick?

The e-Visa ($30 single / $65 multiple from 27 April 2026) lands you pre-approved — and multiple-entry helps if you re-enter Egypt. VOA suits a single straightforward visit.

Can I pay the visa-on-arrival fee by card?

The classic counter is cash ($30). Cairo's digital pilot takes QR payment ($36) — do not rely on it at other airports.

How long can I stay on an Egypt visa on arrival?

Up to 30 days as stated on the visa/entry permission. Check the stamp before leaving the counter.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Egypt, the route check comes first: visa on arrival ($30 cash, single entry, 30 days) for around 46 nationalities including the US and UK, or an e-Visa ($30 single / $65 multiple from 27 April 2026) for around 75 nationalities. At Cairo, the digital VoA pilot ($36 QR payment, since 1 Aug 2026) can shortcut the queue. Older guides quoting $25 are stale — verify on visa2egypt.gov.eg close to departure.
