Decide what the score will be used for

For Denmark, separate university admission, scholarship selection, professional registration and immigration. Each can accept different tests and minimum scores. A university may accept TOEFL or PTE while an immigration route requires a specific approved English test version.

Score targets: minimum versus competitive

A published minimum is the lowest acceptable score, not necessarily a competitive score for selective programmes or scholarships.
