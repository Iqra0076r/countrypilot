How to use this Hungary visa and entry guide

Hungary runs Schengen short stays (€90 C visa, no e-Visa or VOA) alongside a distinctive long-stay system: residence-permit applications are filed online through the official Enter Hungary platform, with in-person biometrics still required. Consular fees are refreshingly specific — €110 for student and work residence permits at the mission, with in-Hungary fees in forint.

The 2026 headline is a hard stop: Government Decree 92/2026 suspended NEW guest-worker residence-permit applications from 6 June 2026 — no third countries are currently designated as eligible. EU Blue Card and National Card routes remain open. Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship holders are exempt from the student fee. This guide was verified against NDGAP and Hungarian MFA sources in October 2026.

Passport validity and document condition

Standard Schengen rule for C visas: passport issued within the last 10 years, valid 3+ months beyond departure. For residence permits, longer validity is wise — the permit process rewards documents that outlast the first permit year.

Identify the correct visa or entry route

EU-harmonised rules for short stays: US and UK nationals visa-free 90/180; India, Pakistan, Nigeria, the Philippines and Bangladesh need the €90 Schengen C visa. Long stays go through Enter Hungary regardless of nationality.

Transit and onward travel

Budapest connections follow Schengen transit logic: airside transit without entering Schengen needs no visa for most, with airport-transit-visa requirements for listed nationalities. Any landside step — hotel, terminal change — is an entry needing the C visa or visa-free status.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Whatever Hungary's official rules say on paper, the officer at the desk decides your admission. Carry a return or onward ticket, proof of accommodation, and evidence you can fund the trip — bank statements or a card in your own name, not someone else's screenshots. Keep the Enter Hungary platform (NDGAP) approval or visa printout accessible offline; airport Wi-Fi is not a document strategy. Visiting friends or family? Their full address and a contact number answer the two most common questions before they're asked.

Answer questions directly and consistently with your application. If anything changed since you applied — new passport, different flight, longer stay — say so upfront rather than letting the officer discover the mismatch.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

Hungary requires no vaccination certificate for ordinary visitors. No health document is checked at Hungarian border control for short stays.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

The Schengen C application requires €30,000-minimum travel medical insurance. For students, the economics are notable: the University of Szeged's Generali Studium group policy runs HUF 49,000/semester (~HUF 98,000/year) and is often included in tuition, while the NEAK public-healthcare agreement for self-funded students costs HUF 96,840/month in 2026 — Stipendium Hungaricum students get public care plus supplementary insurance up to HUF 65,000/year.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

Schengen admission: 90 days per 180 across all member states. Hungarian border officers may ask for the return ticket, accommodation, insurance and funds. Keep documents accessible — checks happen at airports and land borders alike.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Study: €110 residence-permit fee at the consulate (Stipendium Hungaricum scholars exempt), decided in about 15 days; Hungary publishes no fixed official minimum for student funds — NDGAP assesses means of subsistence case-by-case (third-party guides estimate ~€600–800/month, but that is not an official threshold). Work: €110 at the consulate (HUF 26,000 via Enter Hungary / HUF 39,000 in person in-country), decided in about 21 days (60–70 in specific cases) — but new guest-worker applications are SUSPENDED since 6 June 2026 (Decree 92/2026); EU Blue Card and National Card remain open. A visitor visa never authorises work.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Every child needs their own passport and their own Hungary permission — there is no family visa or shared authorization. Minors travelling without both parents should carry a consent letter from the non-travelling parent(s); it is not always a statutory requirement, but officers routinely ask, and a missing letter can delay or derail entry.

Dual nationals should enter and leave on the same passport, and check whether Hungary restricts dual nationality — some countries treat entry on a foreign passport as a declaration with consequences. Travellers on refugee travel documents or laissez-passers face different rules everywhere: verify with the mission, not with forums.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

Schengen C route: consulate/centre appointment, €90 fee, €30,000-minimum insurance. Residence route: 1) File on the official Enter Hungary platform (cheaper than in-person: HUF 26,000 vs 39,000 in-country). 2) Attend biometrics in person. 3) Study: ~15-day decisions; work: ~21 days. 4) Guest-worker route: do not apply — suspended since 6 June 2026; use Blue Card or National Card instead. 5) Stipendium Hungaricum scholars: fee-exempt.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Applying for the guest-worker permit now. New applications suspended since 6 June 2026 — they will not be processed.
  • Treating €600–800 as the official student minimum. No fixed official figure exists; NDGAP decides case-by-case.
  • Filing in person instead of Enter Hungary. In-country filing costs HUF 39,000 vs 26,000 online.
  • Skipping the €30,000 insurance for the C visa. Mandatory application document.
  • Counting 90 days per visit. The 90/180 rule spans all Schengen states.

Building a stronger visa or entry file

Caseworkers and officers are testing two things: will you leave, and can you afford the trip. The strongest files show stable, genuine funds over several months — a single large unexplained deposit reads as funds parking and hurts more than it helps. Tie the itinerary to the money: the trip you're describing should be one your documented income plausibly supports.

Show a reason to return — employment with approved leave, enrolment, family, property — and keep the story consistent across every document. If you've been refused before, address the refusal reasons directly with new evidence; resubmitting the same file invites the same answer.

eVisas, online forms and scam avoidance

The Enter Hungary platform (enterhungary.gov.hu, via NDGAP) is the official online channel for residence permits — and the cheaper one. Schengen C visas remain consular/in-centre with in-person biometrics. 'Hungary e-Visa' sellers are not official.

Translations, certifications and document format

Any document not in Hungary's accepted language(s) needs a proper translation — check the mission or the Enter Hungary platform (NDGAP) for whether certified translation is required, and by whom. Bank statements should be official downloads or stamped originals, not screenshots; employment letters should be on letterhead with a verifiable contact.

Submit scans well before any appointment so there's time to fix rejections. Keep originals for the appointment itself, and keep a full copy of everything you submitted — if the file is queried months later, you'll need to know exactly what the officer saw.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Where Hungary requires in-person biometrics — fingerprints and a digital photograph — book the appointment early; centres fill up in peak seasons and the decision clock usually starts at the appointment, not the application. The photograph is captured there, so check whether a separate photo upload is even needed before paying for passport photos.

Interviews are the exception rather than the rule for short stays, but they happen where credibility needs testing. Answer from your actual plans, not from what you think the officer wants to hear — memorised scripts are easy to spot and hard to recover from.

Families and group travel

Each traveller — including every child — needs their own permission; there is no family authorization that covers a group. Apply in the same window so decisions land together, but understand each application is decided on its own merits: one refusal doesn't cancel the others, though it should prompt the rest of the group to re-check their own files.

For organised groups, keep a shared folder with every member's approval, the full itinerary, and the group leader's contact details. If one member is pulled aside at the border, the group should know where the documents are — not just that they exist somewhere in someone's email.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

Cash of €10,000+ entering or leaving must be declared. Standard EU duty-free allowances apply. Keep the visa, insurance and return ticket with your passport.

Departure and future travel

Schengen C visas are not extendable in-country except in narrow cases. Residence holders extend via Enter Hungary before expiry — and guest-worker holders should note the suspension affects new applications, not existing permits.

How to research a route without getting misled

The hierarchy: 1) oif.gov.hu — NDGAP's procedural-fee factsheets are the controlling fee source. 2) Hungarian MFA mission pages (e.g. Istanbul) for consular specifics. The single most important 2026 fact: guest-worker applications are suspended — any guide omitting Decree 92/2026 is dangerously stale.

A final document audit before travel

  • Passport valid per Hungary's rule, with blank pages, matching the application exactly
  • Visa / e-Visa / authorization approval saved offline and printed — verify it's linked to the current passport
  • Return or onward ticket and accommodation details accessible without internet
  • Proof of funds and ties-to-home evidence packed
  • Travel insurance documents with emergency numbers
  • Any required arrival forms (health, customs, digital entry cards) completed in advance
  • Certified translations for documents not in the accepted language
  • Emergency contacts: your embassy, your insurer, and someone at home who has your itinerary
  • Previous refusals or complications declared honestly wherever asked

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

Hungary's 2026 system has one red line: no new guest-worker applications (Decree 92/2026) — plan around Blue Card or National Card instead. Everything else is orderly: €90 Schengen C, €110 residence permits via Enter Hungary, ~15–21 day decisions, and no invented funds threshold — show real finances. Verify fees on oif.gov.hu.
