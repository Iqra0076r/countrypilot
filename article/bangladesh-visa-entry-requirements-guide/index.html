How to use this Bangladesh visa and entry guide

Visa rules are not a single yes-or-no fact attached to a destination — they depend on your passport, purpose, length of stay, transit airports and sometimes recent travel history. Start with those facts before reading a visa list. The entry-status table below is the fast answer for the most common passports; the detail follows it. Always confirm the decisive rule on the official channel — Bangladesh online visa application — before paying or booking.

Passport validity and document condition

Carry a passport valid for at least six months beyond your intended stay with blank pages.

Transit and onward travel

Dhaka airside transit on a through ticket normally needs no visa. Leaving the airport requires the visa or an eligible VoA.

Evidence immigration officers may ask to see

Financial proof is reported as required with no fixed amount (2026). Carry recent bank statements.

Common documents beyond the visa itself: onward or return ticket, proof of accommodation, and evidence you can support yourself. Print everything — phone batteries die at the worst moments.

Health documents and vaccination certificates

No vaccination certificate required for entry from most countries. Standard travel immunizations apply; consider the region's health guidance.

Insurance as an entry or visa condition

Travel insurance is reported as recommended but not mandatory (2026). Recommended given healthcare costs for visitors.

Arrival permission and permitted stay

Your permitted stay is set by the visa, stamp or entry record — visa validity and authorised duration are not necessarily the same thing. Read the entry stamp and calculate days using the method specified for your route. Overstays in Bangladesh can mean fines, detention or a future entry ban — know your date.

Work, study and residence are separate questions

Work and study need separate Bangladeshi permits with local sponsorship — the visitor entry never authorizes employment. Fees are not published; confirm with the embassy or sponsor.

Minors, dual nationals and special documents

Minors generally need their own passport and sometimes a parental consent letter — check the requirements for your departure country too. Dual nationals should check the rules for the passport they plan to enter on, since visa treatment follows the document presented at the border. Refugees, stateless persons and emergency travel-document holders should confirm with Bangladesh’s embassy directly, as standard routes may not apply.

A reliable pre-travel workflow

  1. Check your category on visa.gov.bd — online application or VoA eligibility.
  2. Confirm the current fee for your nationality (reported figures are secondary-sourced).
  3. Prepare passport (6+ months validity), photographs, financial evidence and travel bookings.
  4. Apply at least a month ahead (reported 7–10 working days).
  5. If using VoA, carry USD cash and confirm eligibility before boarding.

Prepare passport, photographs, the online application printout, bank statements and travel bookings. The portal's checklist governs.

Translations, certifications and document format

Documents not in the local or accepted language may need certified translations — the official checklist states the rule. Certifications, bank statements and invitation letters should be recent (typically within 3 months). Photograph standards matter more than people expect: follow the portal’s specification exactly.

Interviews, biometrics and identity checks

Biometric capture is not documented. Expect standard checks on application and arrival.

Families and group travel

Each traveller needs their own application or authorisation — visas are not transferable within a family or group. Apply together where the portal allows, and carry the same document set for each person. With minors, bring birth certificates or consent documentation in addition to passports.

Customs, cash and restricted goods

A visa is not a customs clearance. Declare cash above the local reporting threshold, check restricted items before packing (medications, drones, satellite equipment and certain food products are common flashpoints), and know that border officials can inspect devices. Keep receipts for high-value items you intend to bring back out.

Departure and future travel

Before leaving, confirm your exit is recorded — keep boarding passes and entry/exit stamps. If you overstayed or had any immigration incident, note it: future applications to Bangladesh and other countries ask about it. Some nationalities need to deregister or obtain exit clearance after long stays; check before a long-term visit.

How to research a route without getting misled

Rank sources by authority: Bangladesh’s immigration authority and foreign ministry first, then Timatic/IATA, then consular posts, then recent reputable reporting. Treat aggregator visa sites as leads, not answers — they go stale. If two credible sources conflict, say so in your notes and confirm with the authority; this guide flags conflicts the same way.

A final document audit before travel

Forty-eight hours before departure, check the passport number on the visa or authorisation, validity dates, name spelling, permitted entries and any conditions. Confirm transit airports and terminals have not changed. Put the approval, insurance and accommodation in an offline folder and print critical documents when the destination or airline may expect paper evidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Bangladesh?

Most visitors apply online at visa.gov.bd or use the reported USD 50 visa on arrival (30 days, eligible nationalities). Confirm your route on the portal.

Is there visa on arrival in Bangladesh?

Reported at USD 50 for 30 days for eligible nationalities at Dhaka airport (2026, secondary). Confirm eligibility before relying on it.

Do Indians need a visa for Bangladesh?

A visa is required but the fee is reported as waived for Indian nationals under the bilateral arrangement (2026).

How long does the Bangladesh visa take?

About 7–10 working days is reported (2026). Apply at least a month ahead.

Can I work in Bangladesh on a tourist visa?

No — work needs a separate sponsored permit.

The practical takeaway

Bottom Line

For Bangladesh, apply online at visa.gov.bd a month ahead, or confirm eligibility for the reported USD 50 visa on arrival. Verify the current fee for your nationality on the portal (reported figures are secondary), carry financial proof, and keep the return ticket ready.
