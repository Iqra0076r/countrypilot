A practical verification worksheet

Build a one-page eligibility sheet before applying: purpose of stay, route name, sponsor/institution if required, status from which you are applying, eligibility tests, evidence, fees, biometrics/identity step, expected decision point, visa conditions and next-stage options. Then label every item as 'officially confirmed', 'still to verify' or 'not applicable'. This prevents a common failure: collecting generic documents without proving the specific legal requirement. Choose the family-visa category before comparing subclasses

Australia's Family Migration program groups permanent family migration into four broad categories: partner, parent, child and other family visas. Start by identifying the relationship category first, then compare the subclasses and applicant-location rules within that category.
